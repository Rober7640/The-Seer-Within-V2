# Implementation Notes

**The unifying spine:**
Every email leans on the same proprietary mechanism — *"I read the syllables of your full birth name and draw the face the sound is calling toward."* This is the single most important line in the sequence. It does three things at once:
1. **Differentiates** Evelyn from every AI tool, every other psychic, every "soulmate generator" online (none of them say this)
2. **Explains** *how it works* — modern audiences want a mechanism, not just a claim
3. **Justifies** the 91.2% recognition rate — the stat lands because the mechanism has earned it

Use this line — or close paraphrases — on the landing page, in form copy, in ad creative. Consistency across channels is the multiplier.

**Why no price in the body:**
Pay-what-you-can pricing is one of the funnel's strongest hooks, but revealing it in the email kills the click. *"Pay what you can"* creates a curiosity gap — they have to click to find out how much. Pricing is revealed only on the landing page.

**About the 91.2% stat:**
Illustrative direct-response specificity number in the style of your example. If you have a measured customer-recognition number, swap it in. Whatever you settle on, **use the same number everywhere** — inconsistency kills the proof.

**About the operational mechanism details:**
The recurring lines — *"the middle name carries the harmonic"*, *"the syllables of your full birth name"*, *"the sound the apps don't know"* — make the mechanism feel concrete and operational, not abstract spiritual fluff. Carry these into the form on the landing page (require middle name, label the field *"middle name (carries the harmonic)"*). The form copy is part of the persuasion.

**Suggested GIF asset:**
A 2–3 second loop of a pencil sketching the start of a face on cream paper. Place under every CTA. If you don't have one yet, a single still image of a partly-finished pencil portrait works as a fallback — but the animated version will lift CTR meaningfully in this niche.

**Email structure recap (so you can write more in this voice):**
1. `Dear {{first_name}},`
2. **Hook** — either prospect behavior (their AI experience, their 3am thought, their dating-app pattern) *or* mechanism direct (your name has three syllables)
3. **Mechanism reveal or reinforcement** — *"I read the syllables of your full birth name..."*
4. **Proof beat** — testimonial, story, or three-number stack (12,973 / 30 years / 91.2%)
5. **CTA written in the prospect's voice, name in the button**
6. **GIF placement**
7. **Italic friction-reducer line — no dollar amounts**
8. **Sign-off**
9. **P.S. that closes a loop on the mechanism** — operational detail (middle name harmonic), story payoff (*"We're moving in together"*), or reframe kicker (*"the sketch makes it pointless"*)

**Tracking:**
