## The pilot, and why it was thin

Pilot script: *"Three cards went down in front of you. / You turned one. / Two stayed face down. /
I turned those two myself. / Tell me your first name and I'll show you."*

Scored against AUDIENCE.md's own three tests it **misses all three** (it names an interaction, not
a wound; it offers information, not absolution), and against the four-beat arc it is **Action
only**. Cause: optimising for *verifiable* truth about the interaction — a compliance virtue, not
a persuasion one. It announces that something is withheld without demonstrating there is anything
worth withholding.

## Current script — the worry spine (generic, no wound named)

Operator call 2026-10-05: keep the worry, do NOT name a wound, add a discovery.

| # | Beat | Line |
|---|---|---|
| | **Hook** (silent) | she shuffles, **sets the deck down**, lifts her eyes to the lens |
| 1 | Anchor | "You asked me for a reading, and I gave you one." |
| 2 | Release the blame | "Then the week took you. That's no crime." |
| 3 | **The worry** | "But I've thought about your two cards more than once since." |
| 4 | **The discovery** | "And since we spoke, I've found something in them I wasn't expecting." |
| 5 | Why it matters | "It changes what I would have told you." |
| 6 | The offer | "I've kept it for you." |
| 7 | Action | "Tell me your first name and I'll show you." |

66 words ≈ **33s** at her measured 2.5 w/s.

**Beat 5 is the engine:** it makes the free reading *out of date* rather than merely incomplete —
a reason to return that is true for everyone, names no wound, and carries urgency without
promising an outcome.

**The trade, made knowingly:** dropping the wound drops Recognition and Relief, including the
strongest lever the funnel has. Generic buys a **wider top**; specific buys a **deeper pull**.
Expect this to win on click-through and lose on conversion — so run both. Beats 1, 2, 3 and 7 are
identical in either version, so a second arm is three renders, not eight.

### The wound version (arm two), for reference
Beats 5–7 become: *"They showed me a woman who has got very good at setting herself down." /
"You didn't invent that, and you weren't the first to be taught it." / "A thing that was handed
to you can be handed back."* Recognition is about **what she literally just did** — got a reading
and walked off — so she can verify it against her own behaviour, it needs no lookup, and her
walking away becomes the evidence rather than the problem.

## Build rules
One sentence per shot · 5s and two lines max per render · the expression must be in the still AND
unopposed in the prompt · a clean static still animates better than a mid-motion one · inserts
silent · captions phrase-chunked at y 0.76 · static gain to −2.0 dBFS, never loudnorm ·
download every render inside 24h. Full detail in `03-build-log.md`.
