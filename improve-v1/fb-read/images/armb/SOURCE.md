## Why the symbols were not asked for

The generation prompt banned anything nameable and never mentioned a bird, a tree
or an anchor. A cup that visibly contains a bird is a logo with tea on it: she is
meant to pick a NAME and have Evelyn find it, not read the answer off the picture.
The ring supplies the where; the copy supplies the symbol; she does the seeing.

Assignment is therefore driven by **position, not shape** — which is also why the
names could be reassigned later without touching the art, as long as the copy moves
with them.
