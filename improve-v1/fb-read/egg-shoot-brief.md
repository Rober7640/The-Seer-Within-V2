# The egg shoot — brief

**Ten minutes, a kitchen, a phone.** Everything below is derived from studying the
reference video in `images/reference/egg/` (© its creator, study only), plus what tea
and coffee cost us to learn. Shoot it and we own it outright — no licence question, no
takedown risk, and we compose it to our brief instead of inheriting someone else's.

---

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Glass | a **plain straight-sided tumbler**. Clear, no pattern, no facets, no logo. Straight sides matter: a tapered glass distorts height, and height is the whole reading |
| Water | room temperature, filled to **~85%**. Too full and the meniscus sits in frame; too low and the column is short |
| Egg | **one whole egg**, white and yolk. Room temperature. Fresh — an older white is runnier and gives limp threads |
| Backdrop | **a dark, plain card behind the glass.** Non-negotiable, see below |
| Light | one soft source from the front and slightly to the side. No hard flash, no overhead spot |

---

## 🔴 The one staging lesson from the reference

In her video a dark object happens to sit behind the middle of the glass, and **the
white spikes only read clearly where they cross it.** Above that band, against a pale
background, the same threads nearly vanish.

That is luck in her frame and it must be design in ours: **a dark card behind the
glass, covering the whole water column.** It is the difference between three findable
marks and a glass of cloudy water.

Same lesson coffee taught in another form — the field has to contrast or there is
nothing to find.

---

## Camera

- **Side on**, through the glass. Not overhead. This is a cross-section of a water
  column, and it is the first device in the deck that is not shot down into a vessel.
- Lens height **level with the middle of the water**, so the surface reads as a line
  and not an ellipse.
- Close enough that the glass fills the frame vertically with a little air top and
  bottom.
- **Shoot 9:16 vertical**, and frame so a **1:1 centre crop still holds all three
  marks** — that square is what becomes the lander photograph.
- Locked off. A tripod or a stack of books. Any drift and the ring coordinates are
  wrong, which `SOURCE.md` calls the one unrecoverable fault.

---

## The shoot

1. Frame and lock the camera on the empty glass of water. Start recording.
2. Crack the egg in **slowly, close to the surface**. Dropped from height it breaks up
   and gives cloud instead of structure.
3. **Do not touch the glass again.** Let it record for at least three minutes.
4. Shoot it **five times**, not once. Same reason five candidates beat one for the cup:
   generation is cheap and judgement is the bottleneck. Egg behaviour varies per egg.

**Timing, measured off the reference:** faint threads within ~10 seconds, fully formed
structure by ~90 seconds. That is far too slow for a feed, which is what the edit is for.

---

## The three marks to aim for

The egg gives three shapes that differ in **kind**, not merely position — which is what
coffee had to hunt for and this device does naturally:

| Mark | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Spikes** | rising toward the surface | fine vertical needles, sharply defined |
| **Web** | through the middle of the column | a spreading translucent veil |
| **Yolk** | resting on the floor | the intact mass, and the only warm colour in frame |

**The axis is vertical: surface / middle / floor.** That is coffee's depth grammar made
literal — and unlike coffee, it cannot be misread off a tilted vessel. Coffee's three
readings port almost directly.

A take is good if all three are present and separated. If the white collapses into one
cloud, that take is the egg equivalent of coffee's heavy draw — bin it and shoot again.

---

## The edit

- **Compress the 90 seconds** to roughly 6–10 seconds. Speed ramp, not a hard cut, so
  the forming reads as continuous.
- **The last frame must be the still we ship as the lander image.** Hold on it for a
  beat. That single decision is what lets one shoot serve the ad AND the lander, and
  keeps the gold-ring reveal working.
- Type and options row are set in code over the final frame, exactly as
  `build-read-ad.mjs` does today. Nothing is burned into the footage.

---

## Never in frame

- **No hands.** Every device so far is the instrument alone.
- **No branded packaging, no other creator's props, no cluttered altar.** The reference
  video has candles and labelled jars behind it; ours is a dark card and a glass.
- **Nothing nameable in the water.** The whole premise: she names the shape and Evelyn
  finds it. A visible heart is a logo with an egg in it.

---

## ⚠ Register, before any copy is written

The reference practice is an **egg cleanse** — spiritual harm, in the limpia tradition —
not love divination. Real practice, real vocabulary, wrong emotional register for the
three love hooks. It has to be steered deliberately, the same way coffee's evil-eye
lineage was. Do not inherit the cleanse framing by accident.
