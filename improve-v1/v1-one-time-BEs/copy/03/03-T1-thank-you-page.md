# 03-T1 — Thank-you page *(Judgement Day)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Archetype** | **ACT → the page is an INTAKE GATE**, not a receipt (P7) |
| **The rule** | the reply instruction goes **above** the delivery promise. It is the only thing on the page with a real deadline, because the work cannot start without it |
| **Component** | [00e §7](../../docs/00e-FRAMEWORK-BEs.md) |

---

## The copy

### Your page is open, %FIRSTNAME%.

I've written your name at the top of it and dated it. That much is done.

---

## Now the part only you can do

<!-- P7 · ACT — this goes ABOVE the delivery promise, deliberately -->

**Go to your inbox and reply to the email I've just sent you.**

Tell me who it is. What they did. Roughly when, and how long you've been carrying it.

In your own words — as much or as little as comes out, in whatever order it comes. Don't tidy it
and don't make it fair to them. I am not going to be adjudicating; I am going to be **entering**
it, and an entry that has been made reasonable isn't accurate.

That reply is the Entry. It is the first of your three nights, and I can't make it for you.

**Write it tonight if you can.** Not because anything expires — nothing here does — but because
the page is open now, and an open page is the one part of this that's uncomfortable to leave
sitting.

---

## Then what happens

Once your Entry is in, the three nights run: the Entry, the Transfer, the Closing.

Your record comes to you after the third night. It will be titled:

> ### %FIRSTNAME% — your account is closed

Nothing else I send looks like that.

---

Take your time with the writing, dear. It's the only hard part.

— Evelyn

---

## Build notes

- **P7 inverted from `02-T1` on purpose.** 02's page is a receipt and ends. This one pushes her
  straight back to her inbox, and the delivery promise is *below* the ask so she reads the ask
  first. A woman who leaves this page without replying is a paid order that cannot be filled.
- **"Write it tonight if you can" is urgency without a deadline.** The reason given is emotional
  and true — an open page is uncomfortable to leave — rather than an expiry. That matters here more
  than anywhere: the letter's whole premise is *this has been open for years*, so a countdown would
  contradict the offer.
- **"Don't make it fair to them"** pre-empts the commonest bad Entry: the balanced, both-sides
  account that leaves out the part that actually hurt. A fair entry is a useless one, and saying so
  here raises intake quality at zero cost.
- ⚠ **No bump delivery on this page.** `02-T1` hands over Astro Force immediately because that copy
  promised *tonight*. The Unburdening is aftercare and ships with the record — delivering it now
  invites her to burn the cost before the debt has been closed, which is the wrong order.
- **The delivery subject must match `03-P4`** exactly.
