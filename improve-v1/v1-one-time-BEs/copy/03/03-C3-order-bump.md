## The copy

**☐ Yes — I want the replaying to stop as well, and stop for good.**

<!-- RULE 3 · names the MAIN offer's limit -->

Closing the account takes the debt off my name. It does not, on its own, stop me running the
conversation again in the car.

So yes — send me **The Unburdening** too.

<!-- BEAT 3 · what it removes, and what it has been costing -->

The debt is one thing. What it *cost* me is another, and that part is still mine: the years of
being careful, the friendships I got quieter in, the version of myself I put away because it wasn't
useful any more.

Nobody owes me that back. That's the part I have to set down myself.

<!-- BEAT 4 · ease + immediacy -->

The instructions are short and there is nothing to work out. One sitting, the night my page is
sealed. I
write out what it cost me — **not their name, not what they did** — and then I burn that page and
watch it go.

Twenty minutes. Once.

**Add The Unburdening — $12.77**

## Build notes

- **Rule 1 — adjacent, not identical.** The working closes a *debt*; this sets down a *cost*. They
  are genuinely two things, which is why the bump doesn't cannibalise: a woman can have her ledger
  ruled off and still be rehearsing in the shower on Thursday, and the letter already told her she
  does exactly that.
- ⚠ **She burns what it COST her, never the target's name** (§4a, hard rule). Burning a named person
  is a different product from the one we sell and reimports the whole revenge frame the offer was
  reframed away from. The copy states the exclusion **in the buyer's own voice** — *"not their name,
  not what they did"* — which is the safest place for it to live.
- **Rule 4 — it protects the main product.** The most likely way 03 gets judged a failure is that
  she replays it on night four and concludes nothing worked. The Unburdening gives that predictable
  moment somewhere to go, which makes the working more likely to be counted a success.
- ⚠ **Fire is 03's, once.** 02's bump is salt and a threshold, 04's is a nightly cup, 05's is a
  written self-audit that is **kept**. Nothing else in the deck burns. Do not let a later pass add
  a candle to 02 or a burning to 05.
- **Timing is tied to the sealing, not to purchase** — *"the night my page is sealed"*. Unlike 02's
  bump (which is preparation and wants doing first), this one is aftercare and only makes sense
  after the third night. That also means `03-T1` must **not** deliver it as an immediate download
  the way `02-T1` does; it ships with the record.
