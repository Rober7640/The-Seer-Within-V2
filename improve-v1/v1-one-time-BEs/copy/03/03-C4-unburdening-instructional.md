---

## The copy

# The Unburdening

### One page, one fire, once

%FIRSTNAME%,

Your account is closed. This is the other half, and it's the half only you can do.

Read it through first. It takes twenty minutes and it wants doing on the night your page was
sealed, or as near to it as you can manage.

---

## What this is for

The debt is settled. What it *cost* you to carry is not part of the debt, and nobody is going to
give it back.

That's the honest position, dear, and there's no working I can do that changes it. The years of
being careful. The friendships you got quieter in. The version of yourself you put away because she
wasn't useful for what you were dealing with.

Nobody owes you those. There is no account to close and no one to close it against.

Which means it doesn't get settled. **It gets set down** — by you, on purpose, once — and this is
how that is done.

---

## What you need

Paper. A pen. Something to burn it in safely: a sink, a metal bowl, a hearth, a bucket in the
garden. Water within reach.

Nothing else, and nothing special. Ordinary paper is correct.

---

## The writing

Sit down somewhere you won't be interrupted, and write out **what it cost you.**

⛔ **Not their name. Not what they did. Not one word about them.**

That is on the sealed page in my book and it is finished; putting it on this one would drag it back
out. This page is about you and what you have been paying, and if their name appears on it you are
doing a different thing from the one I am describing — and the different thing does not work and
does not put anything down.

So: what did it cost?

Write in plain sentences, in any order, for as long as it keeps coming. Things like —

*It cost me four years of being the reasonable one.*
*It cost me telling people things.*
*It cost me the way I used to be at parties.*
*It cost me trusting my own read on somebody.*
*It cost me my thirties, if I'm honest about it.*

Keep going until you're finished rather than until it looks complete. The last two or three lines
are the ones that matter, and they arrive after you've decided you're done.

Don't make it fair. Don't add the part where you were also difficult. That belongs somewhere else
and it does not belong here.

---

## The reading

Read it back to yourself once, out loud, in the room.

Once, %FIRSTNAME%. Not twice.

You'll want to fix it — a better word here, a sentence you left out. Leave it. The page is not for
keeping and it doesn't have to be good; it has to have been said out loud by the person it happened
to, which for most women is the first time.

---

## The burning

Burn it.

Safely and unhurriedly. Watch it go — don't light it and turn away. This is the whole of the rite
and it takes ninety seconds, and you should spend them looking at it.

When it's out, run the ash away with water. Wash your hands.

Then say once, out loud:

> **"I've paid that. I'm not paying it again."**

And go to bed.

---

## Afterwards

You may feel worse for a few hours. That is common and it is not a bad sign — you have just read
out a list of things you lost, which is a genuinely sad thing to do, and sad is the correct response
to it.

What follows in the next fortnight is different. Women describe it as being less tired than the
situation warrants. Not lighter, exactly. Less braced.

**And once is enough.** Do not repeat this monthly, do not keep a copy, and do not start a new page
when something reminds you. A cost you set down twice is a cost you picked back up in between.

If a new one is genuinely owed to you one day, that's a new account and it belongs in my book, not
on this page.

— Evelyn

---

## Build notes

- ⛔ **The exclusion is stated three times** — in the setup, in the writing instruction, and by
  implication in the burning. §4a's hard rule is that she burns what it cost her and never the
  target's name: burning a named person is a compliance problem and a different product, and it
  would reimport the revenge frame the whole offer was reframed away from. This is the one rule in
  03 that a later pass must not soften for the sake of a punchier line.
- **Rule 2 — self-performed.** Paper, pen, a sink. Evelyn ships nothing and does nothing.
- **Rule 4 — it protects the main product.** The predictable failure of 03 is that she replays the
  grievance in week two and concludes nothing worked. This gives that moment a place to go, and its
  closing line — *"I've paid that. I'm not paying it again"* — is designed to be the sentence she
  reaches for when it recurs.
- **The example lines are load-bearing.** *"It cost me my thirties, if I'm honest about it"* is the
  register the exercise needs, and without examples most women write three tidy sentences and stop.
  ⚠ Keep them concrete and keep them about *her*.
- **"You may feel worse for a few hours"** is inoculation on the one deliverable most likely to
  produce an immediate negative reaction. Predicting it converts a refund into a confirmation.
- ⚠ **Fire is 03's alone** — 02 uses salt, 04 a cup, 05 a written list that is *kept*. And 03's own
  free gift is water, which is deliberate: the gift holds and releases, the bump consumes. They are
  the two halves of the same offer and they must not use the same element.
