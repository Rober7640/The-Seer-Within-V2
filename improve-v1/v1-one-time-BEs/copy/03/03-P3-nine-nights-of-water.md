## The copy

### And now the second part, %FIRSTNAME% — nine nights, and then you stop

The first nine days are the quiet ones. Nothing is visible yet, and that is the hardest stretch of
this whole business — not because anything is wrong, but because there is nothing to look at and
you will want something to look at.

So I'm giving you something to do with your hands.

---

### What to do

Every night for nine nights, before you sleep: **a glass of water, filled, on the windowsill.**

Ordinary water from the tap. Any glass you own. Fill it properly — to the top, not half — and put
it where the window is, not on the nightstand.

In the morning, **pour it out.** Onto the earth if you have any, out of the window if you're high
up, down the sink if neither. It doesn't matter which. What matters is that it goes, and that you
are the one who does it, and that you do it before you do anything else.

Then fill it again that night.

Nine nights. On the tenth morning you pour it out and you put the glass away, and that's the end of
it.

---

### Why water

Because it's the only thing in the house that will hold a shape without keeping it.

That is the whole of the teaching, dear, and it is not a metaphor I've dressed up — it is why this
practice uses water and not a candle or a stone. A glass of water sits there all night holding
whatever the room is. In the morning you tip it out and there is nothing in the glass. It doesn't
remember. It can't.

Nine mornings of pouring something out and finding the vessel empty afterwards is not a symbol you
think about. It's a thing your hands learn.

---

### What it is for, plainly

Two things, and I'd rather be straightforward about both.

**It gives the nine days a shape.** You are going to be waiting for evidence during a stretch when
there isn't any. Waiting with nothing to do is when a woman starts checking, and checking is the
one thing that costs you the ledger. This is what your hands do instead.

**And it marks the days.** On the ninth morning you will know it is the ninth morning — you will
have counted, because you had to fill it nine times. Which means the private window ends on a day
you can actually name, rather than dissolving into a vague fortnight where you're never sure whether
it's working.

---

### If you miss a night

Start the nine again.

Not as a punishment — because the count is the point. A woman who misses the fourth night and
carries on to nine has done eight nights and a gap, and she will know that, and on the ninth
morning she won't quite trust it.

Start again and it costs you four days. It's worth four days.

---

### And then stop

Nine nights, and it's finished. Don't keep it going.

This is not a practice to take up; it is a bridge across a specific nine days, and a woman still
putting water on the sill in March is telling herself the account is still open.

Put the glass away. That's part of it.

## Build notes

- **§6c — a second act with its own schedule, not a bonus paragraph.** It has a start, a count, a
  failure rule and a defined end, which is what makes a free gift retain rather than decorate.
- **It exists to prevent the checking.** That is the operative job: the letter, the confirmation and
  the product all forbid checking during the window, and forbidding a behaviour without replacing it
  is how you get the behaviour. This is the replacement, and the copy says so plainly rather than
  pretending the water does the work.
- **The countable end is the real device.** *"The private window ends on a day you can name"* fixes
  the commonest complaint about unfalsifiable products — that there is never a moment when you know
  where you are.
- ⚠ **Deliberately not a "practice you take up."** *And then stop* protects the product: an
  open-ended nightly ritual would contradict the Closing, and would also be a fourth nightly
  discipline in a deck that already has 02's 28 nights and 04's Still Cup. **Nine, then the glass
  goes away.**
- ⚠ **Nothing is written and nothing is burned here.** Writing belongs to 02's ledger, burning to
  03's own bump. If this ever acquires a written element it collides with `03-C3` inside the same
  offer.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 1.** The product carries five already; the gift is a set of instructions and
  reads better plain.
