# 03-E1 — Subject-line bank *(Judgement Day)*

Same rules as [`02-E1`](../02/02-E1-subject-lines.md): one emoji at the front · `%FIRSTNAME%`
second, building to `{{ subscriber.first_name | capitalize }}` · curiosity not reveal · under
120 bytes measured **with the tag expanded** · no fallback filter · preheader continues rather
than repeats.

⚠ **Both of the source's subjects are dead.** *"Rain justice on that cheating snake"* and
*"Someone is stealing your love and happiness"* sell the revenge frame the whole offer was
reframed away from. Nothing in this bank promises anything happening to anybody.

⚠ **The hard constraint on this offer's subjects.** 03's inbox competition is her own grievance,
so the temptation is to write the anger back at her — *make them pay*, *settle the score*. Every
one of those is off-brief and most are a Stripe UDAAP problem in eleven words. **The line this bank
holds: name the carrying, never the punishing.** Longest below is 79 bytes expanded.

withholds what the account is. It is also the only one that would make no sense from any other
sender, which is the test a lead subject should pass.

## Notes for the build

- **Subject 2 is the sleeper and should be tested early.** *"You're still rehearsing it, aren't
  you"* is the single highest-recognition line in the letter, it is a question, and it is the one
  sentence in the bank that a woman cannot read without answering. It is second rather than lead
  only because it gives away the free read's best tell before she opens.
- **Subject 4 is the reframe as a hook** — *nothing ever happened to them* is the problem in her own
  words (`00a` PROBLEM), and the preheader turns it. That pairing is doing the offer's whole
  argument in two lines, so if any subject here wins on click-through rather than open, expect it.
- ⚠ **Subject 6 promises sleep.** That is the brief's PROMISE and it is allowed, but it is the only
  subject in the bank making a claim about an outcome. If complaints ever come in on this offer,
  retire this one first.
- **Emoji set is deliberately narrow** — 🕯️ 📖 🌙. 02 runs 🔮 ⚡ ⭐ 🌙; the overlap is the moon
  alone. A buyer on both lists should be able to tell the two programmes apart in the inbox at a
  glance, and 📖 is 03's alone because the book is 03's mechanism.
- **Send-time:** 6:30pm SGT, the list's proven slot. An account-closing letter arriving in the
  evening is also better matched to its subject than a morning send would be.
- ⚠ **Segment harder than 02 did.** This letter names anger and asks a woman to write down the worst
  thing anyone has done to her. Send to the most-engaged slice first, read complaints before
  widening, and expect a higher unsubscribe rate than 02 without treating it as a failure.
