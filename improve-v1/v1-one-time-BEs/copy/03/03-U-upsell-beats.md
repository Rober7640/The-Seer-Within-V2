# 03-U2a — U2 opening beats

✅ **U2 RUNS after 03** *(operator, 2026-08-04 — full ladder everywhere)*. An earlier draft
recommended suppressing it. It ships, so it is written to ship rather than written to be skipped,
and the angle below was rebuilt after that decision.

⚠ **The trap this copy has to avoid.** 03's promise is *you put it down and you sleep*, and
`03-P1`'s thesis is that **closure feels like boredom**. A straight "now manifest what you want"
pitch contradicts the product she has just been promised, and she will feel it. The resolution is
not to sell her a feeling to fill the space with — it is to point out that **the space has never
once been hers to allocate.** That is continuous with the closing rather than against it: the
account stops occupying her, and choosing what does instead is the first thing she has been free to
decide in years. Selling the *choosing*, not the *filling*.

## Build notes

- **The U1 hinge is derived, not invented.** `03-P1` verdict 2 names vigilance as what the account
  cost her, and section 8 says plainly that the Closing doesn't reach it. This upsell is that
  section, sold. A buyer who takes both reads the same argument twice in the same words — which
  here is continuity rather than repetition, because one is a promise and the other is its receipt.
- ⚠ **The risk beat must stay about HER guard, never about a returning enemy.** *"Old shadows try to
  return"* is V1's clearing frame and it would smuggle the backlash premise back in. What is at risk
  is her openness, not her safety from a person.
- **Q1 is the strongest question in the deck** — *have you found yourself holding a little back from
  people who had nothing to do with it?* — because almost every buyer answers yes, and answering it
  produces the sale rather than Evelyn arguing for it.
- **U2 ships** *(operator, 2026-08-04)*, and was rewritten once that was settled. The first draft
  opened on *flat* and hedged — *"I'm not going to sell you a feeling to put in it"* — which was
  honest but was a weak pitch, and it read as an offer apologising for existing.
- ⚠ **The angle that resolves the contradiction is AGENCY, not filling.** *You never chose what went
  in that room.* That runs with `03-P1` rather than against it: the product takes the occupation
  off her, and the upsell is about who allocates the space afterwards — which is the first genuinely
  free decision the whole offer has given her. **Do not let a later pass turn this into manifesting
  a new relationship**; the moment it promises a feeling, it contradicts *boredom is what closure
  feels like* and the buyer will notice.
- **Path B is the stronger of the two** and it earns its pitch honestly: a room that opens does not
  stay open, and *whatever is nearest and loudest* is a true observation about how attention works
  rather than a manufactured threat.
- **`{duration}` in Path A** merges from the intake (`{{HOW_LONG}}`). If it's missing, cut the
  clause rather than rendering a bare token.
