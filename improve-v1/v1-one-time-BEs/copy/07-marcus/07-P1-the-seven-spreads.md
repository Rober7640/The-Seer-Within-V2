# 07-P1 — the seven spreads

The product mechanism for offer 07. One spread per weekday. The email lays the **opening**; her
question buys the **completion**. ⛔ These position lists are a contract — the daily email may only
read positions in its own free block, and the paid reading must deliver every paid position.

| Mon | The Weight | 1 | +5 | 6 | What am I carrying that was never mine? |
| Tue | The Two Doors | 2 | +6 | 8 | Which of these two? |
| Wed | The Small Instruction | 1 | +5 | 6 | What do I actually do today? |
| Thu | The Undertow | 2 | +5 | 7 | What is really driving this? |

| Sat | The Other Chair | 2 | +5 | 7 | What is he actually doing? |

## The pitch line, per spread

Card-native every day, but the shape is fixed: **how many are down · how many aren't · what the rest
would answer · $35 · 24 hours.** The count comes from the table above, so it is different by
construction on most days.

> **Tue** *"Two cards are down. The other six don't go down until I know what you're asking."*
> **Fri** *"Three down, six to go, and the six are the ones that add up."*
> **Sun** *"Three houses. Nine still empty, and they don't fill from a mass email."*
