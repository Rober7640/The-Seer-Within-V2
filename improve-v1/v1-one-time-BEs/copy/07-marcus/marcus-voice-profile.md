# MARCUS STONE — THE DAILY LETTER, v2 (the card routes the cut)

**Written 2026-09-07.** This replaces the ESL letter profile for the DAILY programme only. That
file is preserved at [`marcus-voice-profile-esl-letters.md`](./marcus-voice-profile-esl-letters.md)
and still governs the one-off backend letters (02–06). ⛔ Do not mix them. This one has no
"Namaste", no uninvited act, and a different opening engine.

---

## 1 · THE ONE SENTENCE

> **The cut gives one card, face up. That card decides which cut Marcus lays. The cut goes down.
> Her question turns it over.**

Everything below serves that sentence. If a rule here ever fights it, the sentence wins.

---

## 2 · WHY THIS REPLACED THE OLD SHAPE

The old daily opened on an **uninvited act** — a deck that broke in two places, a card face up in
the pack, a cut that wouldn't hold. It worked, and it does not scale. Four were written before they
started repeating, and there are thirty mornings to fill.

⭐ **The event is now the card, and what Marcus decides to do about it.** That is new every morning,
free, forever, and it comes out of the deck instead of out of a writer's imagination.

It also fixes the deeper problem. A container like *Problem, Action, Outcome* has no subject of its
own — it is three slots and a shape. Something has to supply the subject, and until now that was a
writer choosing a wound. **Now the card supplies it.** Which means the programme needs three or
four containers, not thirty spreads, and the variety comes from a deck of seventy-eight.

---

## 3 · THE TWO READINGS — the rule this whole structure stands on

The same card is read **twice**, for two different jobs, and the two readings must be visibly
different sentences.

| | Reading one · THE SIGNAL | Reading two · THE POSITION |
|---|---|---|
| **What it does** | decides which container gets laid | is the reading she came for |
| **Where it comes from** | what is painted on the card, generally | the position's `job` string, answered |
| **How often it changes** | never — the same card signals the same thing every time | every morning |
| **Example** | *the Hermit: there is nobody else in the frame, so it is the thing she carries alone and hasn't said* | *the Hermit in the problem seat: the thing in her way is that she has been solving it privately* |

⛔ **If reading two restates reading one, the letter has read the card twice and the spread not at
all.** That is the single most likely failure of this structure and the check for it is mechanical:
cover the position name, and reading two should stop making sense.

⭐ **This is how the structure keeps the operator's standing rule** — *read the spread, not the
card.* The dictionary picks the container. The draw does the reading. Two jobs, and neither one
contaminates the other.

### What moved, and what died

⛔ **Dead: the uninvited act.** No deck anomalies. He cuts, and reads what is on the cut. That is
the whole of his method and it is stated plainly in one line, not dramatised.

⚠ **Beat 5 comes after the salutation, not before it.** Opening on the cut and *then* saying
"Marcus here" reads as a false start — he speaks before he has arrived. Found on the first test.

⛔ **Beat 8 carries the open loop, and it is the only place left for it.** The old shape planted
it in a big-idea beat at about a quarter of the way in, and beat 16 pays it off. Folding the big
idea into the signal deleted the promise and left beat 16 settling a debt the reader never knew she
was owed. Found by comparing the same letter written both ways.

⛔ **Dead: "have a proper look before you read what I make of it."** It existed to stall her in
front of a picture she hadn't been told about yet. Now beat 6 reads the picture immediately, so the
stall has nothing to hold.

⚠ **The precedent moved, twice, and here is where it landed.** It used to sit before the card,
buying credibility in advance. The reading now buys its own — she recognises herself in beat 9 — so
it moved late. ⛔ **Late was too late.** Put after the withhold it lands inside the run from
*"two cards say, one can't"* to the count to the ask, and stops the close dead at the moment the
letter is closing. Found on the first test, against the same letter written the old way.

⭐ **It sits at 12 now: after the shame comes off, before the withhold.** That keeps the
withhold → count → CTA run unbroken, and it gives proof the one slot it does best — straight after
the reader's lowest point.

⛔ **And then it got demoted, 2026-09-07, and this is the important part.** Five letters in a row
opened that slot with *"years ago I read for a woman called…"* — five anonymous women, 55 to 90 words
each, all of whom had a small revelation. Boring by the third and quietly implausible by the fifth,
which is worse.

⭐ **The error was a category error, not a wording one.** The ESL profile's own §4 calls the
precedent *"a first-contact device, spent once"* and drops it from the second letter of a two-letter
sequence. It is a cold-open move, and this is a warm-relationship programme: she reads Marcus most
mornings, so trust accrues **across** letters, not inside one. He does not owe her proof daily.

⛔ **So beat 12 is now optional and its default is nothing.** See bank G.

## 5 · THE ROUTING

**A card routes the same way every time.** Consistency is the whole credibility of the device. She
will see the Hermit at the top of an email in week four and know what is coming, and being right
will feel like the deck working.

| The card signals | Container | State |
|---|---|---|
| Something carried alone and unsaid — **the Hermit** · Nine of Swords · Four of Swords | **Problem, Action, Outcome** | ✅ live |
| Something taken once and still running — Five of Pentacles · the Devil · Eight of Swords | Past, Present, Future | ☐ pending |
| A fork she has already leaned toward — Two of Swords · the Lovers · Seven of Cups | Should I Stay or Go | ☐ pending |
| An answer that hasn't come — Eight of Cups · Three of Swords · the Moon | He's Gone Quiet | ☐ pending |

⛔ **The routing is a rule of his practice, never a claim about the cards choosing.** Write *"when
the Hermit opens a cut, I lay it as a problem"* — true, and his. Never *"the cards chose this
spread for you"*, which claims a randomness the programme does not have.

⭐ **A card is never used up.** Operator correction, 2026-09-07, against an earlier draft of this
file that said one card could only ever open one cut. That was wrong. A real cut hands you the
Hermit again, and often. Banning the repeat would have meant a card means one fixed thing forever —
which is the dictionary reading this profile bans everywhere else.

⛔ **What must not vary is the door. What must vary is the finding.** The Hermit signals the same
thing every time, so it opens the same cut every time — that is what makes the system learnable. The
reading it produces has to be new: a different thing found in the same picture, a different act at
the end. `voc/10-broad-frames.md` states the same rule for a repeated frame — *a repeat must change
the finding, not the wording.*

## 6 · CONTAINER ONE — Problem, Action, Outcome

The only live container. Three cards, one free, two paid.

| # | Position | | The job — ⛔ verbatim into the prompt, this IS the reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The problem** | free | What is actually in the way, named as one thing she could point at. Not the version she would give if somebody asked her, and not a feeling |
| 2 | The action | paid | The thing she can begin before this week is out, and what beginning it costs her. ⛔ Not permission, and not something she is waiting on anybody else to decide |
| 3 | The outcome | paid | What is different in her own week once she has begun it — in things she can see. ⛔ Never a date, and never another person's response |

**Count line:** *Three cards. One turned over. Two still face down.*

**Who it is for, said in beat 14** — and it must disqualify, because that is what makes the claim
credible: *"I lay this one when something's live. Not the pattern of your whole life — the thing
that's been sitting on you since Tuesday, and the move that's yours to make before Sunday. If you
want to know why you're like this, that's a different morning and a different cut."*

⚠ **Two paid positions is thin, and the floor knows.** `min_cards_per_answer: 5` tops her answer up
from the open cut, so three of the five she receives are the generic open-six positions. Live with
it while testing the structure; if the structure holds, widen the container to 2 free / 4 paid
before it takes money at scale.

## 7 · HOW HE SOUNDS

Carried over from the proven letters. None of this changed with the structure.

**Claims flat. Withhold only what you are selling.** A hedged psychic is a contradiction in terms.
But *"what this card won't tell me is which of the two"* stays word for word — the withhold is the
offer, not a softener.

⛔ Banned hedges, in full: usually · tends to · almost never · may indicate · might. `copy-check`
catches the first four; the fifth is on you.

**Picture before meaning.** Say what is on the card, then what it means. She is looking at it.

**One symbol, one reading.** ⛔ Never three possible meanings for one image. That is a reader who
does not know what she is looking at.

**No shouting.** No block capitals, no exclamation marks. The urgency is in what he says.

**Em-dashes are his cadence** — about one a paragraph, never two in the same one.

⛔ **No "dear" — that is Evelyn's. No "Namaste" — that was the ESL letters and it does not belong
here.** He opens on the merge tag, an em-dash, and "Marcus here."

**Contractions throughout.** He sounds spoken. No balanced clauses, no appositive tails, no
rhetorical triples. One announced aphorism per letter and not a second.

**The mirror is a feeling, never a biography.** ⛔ No ages, no years, no counts of anything in her
life. *"Since about 1998"* loses every woman whose number differs; *"a mother who sighed"* loses
nobody. This is a blanket send to a mixed list — literal circumstance is confirming on a
pre-qualified segment and exclusionary here.

**She reads at 6am ET.** The cut happened this morning, before light. ⛔ Never narrate a night-time
read. Card artwork described as painted is exempt.

⛔ **No price and no delivery promise, ever.** Money first appears on the booking page.
`copy-check` enforces this on any `-D-` file and it is the only thing standing between a price and
a 76,000-person send.

## 8 · THE REFRAME BEAT WRITES HER BOX

⭐ **Beat 17 does not hand her a question to think about. It hands her the sentence she types into
box one on the booking page** — which sits *before* payment (`07-C5` §5.1), so the CTA's promise is
kept at the click and she will be looking at an empty box within seconds of reading your line.

Three tests, and the sentence fails if it misses any:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Paste-able** | could it go in the box exactly as written, with no editing? |
| **One thing** | `07-C5` rule 1 — one thing per box. *"What's going on with him"* is not a question and no cards can be laid on it |
| **Layable** | a question about what somebody is *doing* can be read. What somebody will *decide* cannot, at any rung, at any price |

⛔ **A question she answers privately is not the reframe.** *"Who's the one person who doesn't
know?"* is a fine emotional turn and a useless thing to paste — Marcus cannot lay a card on it and
she will sit there rewriting it. Keep the private version as the turn if it earns its place, then
convert it: *"What do I do about the person I haven't told?"*

⭐ **On this container the conversion is nearly mechanical.** Problem, Action, Outcome answers *what
do I actually do about this*, so the sentence she types almost always starts **"What do I do
about…"** and ends on the one thing beat 9 named.

## 9 · THE FRAME STILL MATTERS

The card supplies the subject. It does not supply the **reach**.

⛔ Before writing, check the card's signal against gate 4 from `voc/10-broad-frames.md`: *could most
of the list find themselves in this, on any given morning, without having to match a
life-situation?* A signal that only fits a woman with a specific life is a bad routing card, however
good the picture is.

The Hermit passes: everybody is carrying something they have not said out loud.

| **G5** | ⭐ Nothing. Skip beat 12 and buy back 60–90 words, which every letter needs |

### The rule

⛔ **No variant twice inside seven letters, per bank.** Log every letter's picks in
[`daily/rotation.md`](./daily/rotation.md) in the same commit as the draft. A ledger that lags
reports a variant as free when it has just been spent — which is worse than no ledger, because
somebody trusts it.

⚠ **A bank runs out.** When a bank has nothing free, that is the bank telling you it is short a
variant, not permission to repeat. Write a new one and add it.

## 11 · DON'TS

⛔ **Never:** a reframe she cannot paste into the box · a price or a delivery promise · a hedge on a stated claim · reading a paid position ·
a second aphorism · "buy" as a CTA verb · a date on another person's decision · a number about her
life · the same precedent name twice · reading two that
restates reading one · claiming the cards chose the spread.

⚠ **Avoid:** dramatising the cut (it is one line now) · a better-news beat that quietly promises the
thing the letter says to stop waiting for — that contradiction shipped once and had to be rewritten
· a "some women tell me" pair that describes the same woman twice.

### Before filing

Two readings visibly different · routing stated as his practice, not the cards' choice · picture before meaning · claims flat, withhold named by its
question · no number about her life · count beat says who it is NOT for · one aphorism, announced ·
first CTA after the whole free read · `&s=` is a real registry key · `node scripts/copy-check.cjs
copy/07-marcus` green.

*These catch tells, not quality. There is no word count that makes a sentence believable — use §7.*
