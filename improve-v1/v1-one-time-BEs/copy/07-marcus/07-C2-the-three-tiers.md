# 07-C2 — the three tiers *(booking page)*

⭐ **The tiers are built out of the roster, not invented.** Tier 2 adds **The Undertow**'s five.
Tier 3 adds **The Other Chair**'s five. Both are spreads Marcus already lays and she has already met
in the daily emails — so the daily programme *teaches the ladder* without ever selling it.

⛔ **Each tier answers a different question.** The research is explicit that a ladder selling *more
of the same* reads as padding, and the strongest version found sold the upgrade as a different
question: *"Not just 'what's happening?' but 'why does this keep happening?'"*

---

## The three

| | **The Spread** | **The Pattern** | **The Table** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Answers** | What is this? | Why does it keep happening? | What is he actually doing? |
| **Cards** *(Tuesday example)* | 8 | 13 | 18 |
| **Adds** | the six she watched go face down | + The Undertow's five | + The Other Chair's five |
| **Price** | **$35** | **$57** | **$87** |
| **Words** | ~1,000 | ~1,800 | ~2,600 |

*Card counts move with the day — Wednesday's Spread is 6, Friday's is 9 — because each day's spread
has its own count. **The five that Tier 2 and Tier 3 add never change**, which is what keeps the
ladder legible across all seven days.*

## ⭐ Her question routes her — the arithmetic does the selling

The strongest mechanic in the research: sellers count the question and let it name the price. Nobody
argues for the upgrade.

| If her question is… | It needs |
|---|---|
| one thing, about her | **The Spread** |
| *"why do I keep…"*, or a thing that has happened more than once | **The Pattern** — the repeat is the tell, and the origin cards are what read it |
| about what **he** is doing, thinking or waiting for | **The Table** — his side has to be laid to be read |

⛔ **A "should I stay or go" question is two questions.** It needs both doors read against a pattern,
which is The Pattern, minimum.

**Framed as protecting her, never as an upsell:**

> *Pick the one your question actually needs. I would rather you paid for less reading than more —
> a question that only wants one answer gets muddier, not clearer, when I lay eighteen cards at it.*

## The copy, as it sits on the page

### The Spread — $35
> The six that went face down in this morning's email, laid on your question. This is the reading
> you were promised: what the choice is, what each side of it costs, and who you are on the other
> side. **Eight cards.** If your question is one thing, this is the one.

### The Pattern — $57
> Everything above, and then the five that ask why it keeps happening. When it started — and there
> is always a date, and it sits years before the thing you would point at. What has kept it alive. Who
> it still serves, which is the card nobody expects. **Thirteen cards.** Take this one if you have
> been here before.

### The Table — $87
> Everything above, and then his side of it, laid the way his would be. What he is doing, what he
> thinks he is doing, what he would have to admit, and what he does if nothing changes.
> **Eighteen cards.** ⚠ I read *the cards*, not the man — this is what the spread says about him,
> never a claim to know his mind.

## ⚠ What this does to the order bump

Three tiers **are** the depth ladder, so the bump can no longer be *"the same reading, longer"* —
that is now Tier 2's job and the two would cannibalise.

**The bump becomes speed**, which the market treats as a genuine second axis (same-hour roughly
doubles an Etsy price) and which fixes a real gap: at $35 the market expects same-day, and 07
promises 24 hours.

> **Back before tonight — $12.77.** Yours goes to the front of the table and lands the same day
> instead of tomorrow.

*Operator decision, since the bump was previously settled as an expansion.*

## Rules

- ⛔ **The bump does not appear until a tier is chosen** *(operator, 2026-09-02)*. Shown alongside
  the three it is a fourth option competing with them; shown after, it is an add-on to the one
  she has already picked — which is the only thing an order bump is.
- ⛔ **Never pre-checked**, and the middle tier is not pre-selected either.
- **The count is stated as a bare fact** in every tier — 8, 13, 18 — because she can check it and
  cannot check an adjective.
- ⛔ **No tier promises an outcome**, and The Table's guardrail is not optional.
- The daily email links to the page, never to a tier. **She chooses after she has read the spread.**
