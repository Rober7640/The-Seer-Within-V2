**Preheader** — The Devil and the Eight of Cups. One of them is the one you keep not walking through.

---

Two doors on the table this morning, and you're standing between them.

Door one is the Devil. Look at what's painted there. A horned figure squatting on a block, two people chained at the neck below it. And the chains are loose — wide loops that'd lift straight off over their heads if either of them stood up.

Nobody in that picture is trapped. They're staying. That's the door where nothing changes, and you already know exactly how it feels.

Door two is the Eight of Cups. A figure with a stick, walking uphill into rocky ground, away from eight cups stacked neat behind them. Not looking back. The moon's out.

That's the door where you go. And it costs you eight cups you stacked yourself.

Here's what I'd have you carry today, and it costs you nothing. **Both doors are open.** That's the actual news in this pair. The Devil doesn't say you're trapped — it says you're choosing, every morning, and calling it something else.

Most people reading this are standing between the same two.

What I can't tell you from here is what's behind either one. Three cards go behind each door: what it costs, what it gives, and who you turn into on the other side. Six cards. They don't get laid on a guess.

So tell me. What are your two doors?

Two cards are down. The other six don't go down until I know what you're asking. **$35**, back inside 24 hours.
