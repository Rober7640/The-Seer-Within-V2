**Preheader** — Three cards this morning, and the one piece all six were missing.

---

Six days this week I've put cards in front of you. And six times I stopped at the same place.

Monday I couldn't tell you which chain was yours. Tuesday I couldn't tell you what you were holding. Thursday, whose voice it was. Friday, what you'd already decided. Saturday, who you were protecting.

Every one of those was missing the same thing, and it isn't the cards. It's your question.

Three up this morning.

**Three of Swords.** Three blades straight through a red heart, grey rain behind it, no figure anywhere. Nobody's holding those swords. That's the card — the hurt that arrived, that no one is currently doing to you.

**The Star.** A figure kneeling at a pool, pouring two jugs. One onto the land, one back into the water. Eight small stars and one big one. This card follows bad ones. It's what happens after, and the pouring is the point — some of it goes back where it came from.

**The Hermit.** An old man on a snow peak with a lamp, and there's a star inside the lamp. He's not lost. He's holding the light low so he can see the next step of the path.

Together: the injury is real and it's older than you're admitting. Some of it has already started turning. And the first bit of the road is one you walk on your own, with a small light, one step visible at a time.

That's a true reading. It's also a general one, and I want to be straight with you about the difference.

Last Sunday the Eight of Swords was up. Someone sent me one line: *"I keep telling people I left, but I check his profile every night."* That's a question. Not tidy, not clever — just true. And with that in my hand the Eight of Swords stopped being a card about feeling stuck and became a card about a specific bind she'd tied and could untie. I told her which of the two it was. I couldn't have done that from this email.

That's the whole of what I do. You send me your question. I read it against the card that's up that day, and then I draw fresh on you — your name, your birth date, and his if you have it.

It comes back inside **24 hours**, written out, about a thousand words. **$35.00.**

I'm not going to dress that up. It's a reading, done properly, by someone who's done a lot of them.

So — what would you put to these three?
