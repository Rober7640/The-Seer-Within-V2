## Build notes

- **Rule 1 — adjacent, never identical.** Retention and outflow are two different problems, so the
  bump can't cannibalise the bracelet. The line that does the work is *"it was never going to do
  anything about the doors I left open myself"* — which is also, word for word, the bracelet's own
  honest limit restated from the other direction.
- **Rule 2 — a bump is self-performed; an upsell is something Evelyn does or ships.** The Closed
  Purse is text. The buyer performs it, nothing is posted. This is what keeps it distinct from U1/U2
  (physical objects, reused from V1) — a sealing *object* and a noticing *practice* are different
  products, and the deck's rule is that a bump never physically ships.
- **Rule 4 — it protects the main product.** Someone who's still leaking money through their own
  unnoticed habits, and doesn't close that gap, may come to believe the bracelet "isn't working" when
  the real leak was never Pixiu's to plug. Closing the outflow side first is what lets the main
  claim — *what reaches you stops leaking back out* — actually hold up under their own scrutiny later.
- ⚠ **Device-variance check, not yet run against the other three offers' bumps** (Astro Force /
  03's / 04's) since 06 isn't in `00m-VARIANCE-PASS.md` yet. On its face: 02's bump is a clearing
  ritual against inherited weight, this is a noticing practice against the buyer's own habitual
  spend — different mechanism, different device, should be fine, but confirm properly once 06 is no
  longer a candidate offer.
- **No capitals, no exclamation stacks**, matching every other bump in this deck.
- **Price is a proposal, not a decision** — flagged the same way `{{PRICE}}` is flagged on `06-C1`.
  Unlike the main price, a fixed odd-cents bump price is unlikely to be controversial, so a specific
  number is proposed here rather than left as a merge field — easy to change if the operator wants a
  different number.
