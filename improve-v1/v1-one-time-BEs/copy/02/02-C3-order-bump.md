## Build notes

- **Rule 1 — adjacent, never identical.** Sight and weight are two different problems, so the bump
  cannot cannibalise the reading. The line that does the work is *"the spread will show me what is
  coming; it will not lift what is already sitting on me"* — which is also, word for word, the
  brief's stated limit for 02 and the hinge into 03.
- **Rule 2 — a bump is self-performed; an upsell is something Evelyn does or ships.** Astro Force is
  text. She performs nothing and posts nothing. This is what keeps it from competing with U1, which
  is a stone she charges and mails — a protection *practice* here and a protection *object* there
  are different products; two charged objects would not be.
- **Rule 4 — it protects the main product.** A woman who thinks her luck is broken reads a good
  spread and disbelieves it. Clearing the weight first is what makes the twelve land.
- ⚠ **One working, once — and that is a variance decision, not a style one.** 02's own free gift is
  28 nights of nightly noticing (`02-P3`). A nightly bump next to a nightly gift would read as the
  same product sold twice inside a single checkout. It also keeps clear of 04's **Still Cup**, which
  *is* a nightly rite, and of 03's **Unburdening**, which burns.
- ⚠ **Nothing is burned and nothing is written here.** 03's bump burns what it cost her; 05's is a
  written self-audit. Salt and a threshold is 02's alone. `02-C4` must not drift into fire or paper.
- **"Astro Force" is kept from the source** (§4a names it), and the astrological framing is an
  asset rather than a mismatch: the reading is cards, the clearing is the hour and the sky. Adjacent
  practices for adjacent problems. If it ever needs defending, that is the defence.
- **No capitals, no exclamation stacks**, unlike the source's *"Remove Negative KARMA forever!"*.
  Same four beats, Evelyn's list, her register.
- ⚠ **`02-C6` is still open** — whether this box ships pre-checked. Negative-option exposure under
  FTC and card-network rules. Needed before the page ships, not before this copy is approved. The
  copy above works either way; it is written as an opt-in and reads as one.
