## The copy

# Astro Force

### The clearing you do once

%FIRSTNAME%,

Read this through before you start. It takes four minutes to read and about twenty to do, and it is
meant to be done tonight.

---

## What this actually is

There is a difference between what is coming toward you and what is already sitting on you, and
almost nobody separates them.

A reading handles the first. It cannot touch the second.

What I mean by karma is not a cosmic scoring system that has judged you and found you wanting. I
mean something plainer and heavier: the accumulated residue of every choice made in a hurry, every
room you left angry, every arrangement you stayed in past the point you knew. None of it individually
matters. All of it together has weight, and weight is what makes a good season fail to land.

You will know if you are carrying it. It feels like this: things go well and then quietly come
apart, in a way you could almost have predicted, often enough that you have privately concluded that
this is simply how it goes for you in particular.

That is not luck and it is not your character. It is load. And load can be set down.

---

## What you need

Ordinary table salt. A small bowl. That is everything.

Not sea salt, not blessed salt, not anything you have to order. The salt in your kitchen is
correct — it has always been correct, and anyone selling you a special one is selling you salt.

---

## When

**The hour before you sleep, on the first night the sky is fully dark before you begin.**

That is the astro part and it is not decorative. This is a clearing that works downward and outward,
and it wants to be done when the day's traffic has stopped — when nobody is arriving, nothing is
being decided, and the house has gone quiet. In practical terms: late, after everyone is in, with
your phone in another room.

If tonight is chaotic, do it tomorrow. Do not do it distracted. A distracted pass is not a shorter
version of this; it is a different thing that does nothing.

---

## The pass

**1. Start at the far end.**

Go to the room furthest from your front door and begin there. Bowl of salt in your left hand if
you're right-handed, the other way round if you're not — the point is that your working hand stays
free.

**2. A pinch in each corner. Every room.**

Not a line, not a circle. A pinch of salt in each of the four corners of the floor, dropped, not
placed. Then move to the next room, working steadily toward the front door. Cupboards and bathrooms
count. Anywhere you sleep counts twice — do those corners slowly.

**3. Say the same thing in every room.**

Out loud, quietly, once per room:

> **"What was finished here is finished. What is still mine, I keep."**

That is the whole of the words. Say it plainly, the way you'd say it to a person. Nothing is served
by intoning.

**4. End at the threshold.**

The last of the salt goes on the floor immediately inside your front door.

Then open the door. Stand in it for a moment — actually stand in it, don't rush this part — and say
it once more, outward:

> **"What was finished here is finished. What is still mine, I keep."**

Close the door.

**5. In the morning, sweep it out.**

Sweep every room, working the same direction — far end toward the front door — and put the sweepings
outside. Not down the sink and not in the kitchen bin. Outside the door, or in an outdoor bin, or in
the earth if you have any.

Then wash your hands and your face in cold water, and go about your day without thinking about it.

---

## What happens afterwards

Probably nothing dramatic, %FIRSTNAME%, and I would rather tell you that now than have you waiting
for a thunderclap.

What women report, and what I have found myself, is that the house feels — settled. Fractionally.
The kind of difference you notice in the doorway of a room rather than in your chest.

The real sign comes later and it is not a feeling at all. It is that something you had assumed was
just how things go stops happening. A pattern you had stopped noticing because you had stopped
expecting anything else simply doesn't repeat. You spot it two or three weeks out, and you spot it
looking backwards.

**Which is exactly what your ledger is for.** If you have your twelve, you have the
twenty-eight-night practice with them. Write this night down as your first line.

---

## Do it once

This is not a discipline and it does not need keeping up. One pass, one night, done.

If your circumstances change hard — a move, an ending, someone leaves the house or comes into it —
you may do it again then. Otherwise, once is the whole instruction, and I would think less of any
reader who told you to buy it monthly.

Go and sleep, dear. It's off you.

— Evelyn

## Build notes

- **Rule 2 — self-performed, zero fulfilment.** She does all of it; Evelyn ships nothing and performs
  nothing. This is the line that keeps the bump from cannibalising U1, which is a physical stone
  Evelyn charges and posts. A protection *practice* here and a protection *object* there are
  genuinely different products.
- **Rule 4 — it protects the main product.** Explicitly, at the end: the clearing's confirmation
  signal is routed into the ledger. A buyer who takes both now has the bump feeding the free gift
  feeding the reading, and every one of the three makes the other two more likely to be used.
- ⚠ **One night, once — a variance decision, not a style one.** 02's free gift is 28 nights of
  nightly writing. A nightly bump beside a nightly gift reads as one product sold twice inside a
  single checkout. It also holds clear of 04's **Still Cup** (a nightly rite for the waiting hours),
  03's **Unburdening** (burning), and 05's **Old Cords** (a written self-audit). Salt, threshold,
  spoken, swept — 02's alone. ⛔ Nothing is burned and nothing is written.
- **"Do it once" is a deliberate refusal to upsell.** *"I would think less of any reader who told
  you to buy it monthly"* costs a recurring revenue line and buys the credibility that makes the
  reading land. It is also the only sentence in the deck where Evelyn criticises the category she
  works in, and it should stay rare.
- **Everything is procurable at 11pm.** Table salt, a bowl, a door. If the buyer has to obtain
  anything, *"you can start it tonight"* becomes false and the bump's fourth beat fails.
- **The expectation is deliberately damped.** *"Probably nothing dramatic"* is inoculation: a bump
  that promises a felt event at £10 gets refunded, and one that promises a settled house and a
  pattern that stops repeating gets believed. It also matches the reading's own timing inoculation.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 2**, salutation and mid-sentence.
- ⚠ **The salt is ordinary and the copy says so.** Naming the upsell that isn't happening
  (*"anyone selling you a special one is selling you salt"*) is worth more than the margin on it,
  and it is the same move as *"do it once."* Do not let a later pass add a product here.
