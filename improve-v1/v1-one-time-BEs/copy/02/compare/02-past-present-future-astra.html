Three cards stayed behind tonight — your past, your present, and what comes next

Something has already been settled in your favour. Its next blessing shows itself inside five to seven days — but the last card is why I’m writing at an hour I don’t send.

I had put the deck away. Then I saw what was still on the cloth.

I don’t draw for people one at a time, dear. You know me through the letters I send everybody. Tonight something interrupted that.

Those letters belong to everyone who reads them. This little row of cards was yours before I touched it.

I knew that straight away.

The box was closed. I lifted the cloth to fold it, and there they were underneath the loose corner — three cards I hadn’t laid.

Together. Face-down. Waiting for me to notice.

I put the cloth back exactly as it had been. There are moments in this work when the smallest interruption deserves your whole attention.

Your name came before the first card turned, dear. I wasn’t searching for someone to attach them to. I was being shown who they belonged to.

I turned them from left to right. Past. Present. Future. Two things to be grateful for, and one I couldn’t leave you to discover alone.

The first says an old burden has been settled in your favour. The second says affection is flowing toward you again.

The third asks what you’ll surrender to keep it.

And this is near. The first visible sign comes inside five to seven days. The past position is feeding the present one — what began back there is reaching you now.

I remember a woman called Miriam, from the days when my readings went out in handwritten envelopes. She sent me a page with one question written six different ways.

Every version asked what she should change about herself.

She authorised the full reading. Afterwards she sent that page back. Five questions had been crossed out. Under the sixth she had written something she’d never allowed herself to ask.

“Do I want the life I’m trying so hard to be chosen for?” She told me she read it aloud without apologising for it.

That was the change, dear. Nothing about what a man did next. She had stopped treating her own wishes as an inconvenience.

For the first time, her question included her.

Keep that in mind as you read your three.

There’s more here than three separate messages.

Your future is already drawn, dear. The deck holds the whole arrangement. What it has let me turn tonight shows the connection — where it began, where you stand, what presses on it next.

Three cards are a quarter of the answer.

The full spread is twelve, one for each house of your life. Those houses separate what belongs to you from what belongs to somebody else. That distinction matters terribly in this draw.

First, let me give you everything these three do show.

Your first card: the Past — Justice

A crowned woman sits between two pillars. Her sword is upright. In her other hand, two brass scales hang level — nothing left tipping against her.

Look at those scales, dear. With Temperance beside her, Justice in your past says the account has already been settled in your favour.

You have finished paying for something that was never yours to carry alone. The burden has been lifted.

And the good of it is still reaching you. Your love life is opening into a kinder period because the old claim on your happiness has ended. You don’t have to earn that release again.

You’ve been bracing for the familiar disappointment even on good days. This arrangement says the reason for that bracing has gone. There is room now for affection that reaches you without first asking you to prove your worth.

Here is what the past position keeps covered.

It shows the balance restored. It does not show me what first began restoring it.

That beginning matters. You’ve credited the wrong moment with changing your life — something quieter was already working in your favour before the change you remember. I want you to know how far back your good fortune actually reaches.

The twelve trace it to its beginning. These three show the benefit. The cause is further back than this little row can reach.

Your second card: the Present — Temperance

An angel pours water between two cups, one raised, one lowered. One foot rests on earth and the other in a pool. The stream between the cups is unbroken.

Between Justice and the last card, this is affection finding its way back into your daily life. The warmth is real.

You are going to feel wanted again.

A conversation that has felt like work becomes easy. Someone makes room for you without being chased into doing it.

I can see why that matters, dear. You’ve grown tired of being the one who keeps things pleasant — supplying the question, forgiving the silence, finding a reason nobody else has to try.

That changes in this present position. You are about to receive attention you haven’t had to ask for.

Let yourself be glad of it. Justice has already said you’re allowed.

But watch the cups.

One path is replenishment. The other is dilution. From where you stand, both feel like peace returning.

On the first, affection fills what the old years emptied. On the second, you keep adding a little more of yourself until there’s hardly any you left in the mixture. Both begin with relief, dear. Both make you put your phone down smiling.

Which path is yours needs the new card in the house of partnership, read against the house of your own wishes. Those answers aren’t in front of me yet.

The three show affection returning. They don’t show which way the stream is running.

Your third card: the Future — the Devil

This is the one I went back to after I’d turned the others.

Two people stand beneath a horned figure, loose chains around their necks. The loops are wide enough to lift. Neither person is lifting one.

Beside Temperance, this is a warning about the terms attached to closeness. Something that feels easy to accept at first can become terribly hard to refuse once you’ve accepted it.

The warning has two shapes. I want to name them carefully.

The first is the private bargain.

The second is the borrowed verdict. Both put your freedom in somebody else’s hands.

Either closeness comes with a private bargain: keep this quiet, ask for less, don’t spoil what we have by wanting it acknowledged.

Or a borrowed verdict starts governing it: somebody whose approval matters tells you this happiness is selfish, and you begin surrendering it to remain the woman they approve of.

Which shape belongs to your future, and whose face stands behind it, I can’t tell you from these three, dear. I won’t put a face there just to fill the silence.

Look at the whole row. A burden lifted, affection returning, then a chain loose enough to remove. The danger is giving back the freedom that made the good possible.

The twelve show whose terms you’re being asked to live on — and where your answer changes them.

In concrete terms, what do you do now?

One thing, dear.

You authorise the full spread here. I need your say-so before I draw the rest. These three came uninvited — going further into a woman’s life requires her permission. You give it there, and I can open the part of this reading that belongs to you alone.

Back to the Present — which cup is filling?

The tenderness Temperance shows is close, dear. I don’t want the last card to make you turn away from it.

There is a life here in which being loved leaves more of you intact.

That is what I’m looking for in your Past, Present & Future reading. Where the good began, and how you keep it. A past that has released you deserves a present that lets you feel the difference.

Because receiving affection and disappearing into it can feel alike at first. You stop arguing. You sleep better. You tell yourself things have settled, and for a while they have.

The twelve show what that peace is made of. Whether your wishes have finally been heard, or whether you’ve quietly stopped bringing them into the room. There is a particular wish at the centre of that distinction. The full arrangement shows me which one.

Remember the angel’s feet. One is in the water, but one stays on solid ground. In your arrangement, that is the part of your life which must remain yours while you let somebody close.

I want to show you exactly which part. It is easier to protect something when you can name it, instead of calling it a feeling and putting it aside.

Let me lay them and I’ll tell you which way your present is carrying you.

And back to the Future — the chain has room in it

The detail I keep looking at, dear, is the space between the chain and the skin.

There is room for an answer.

A private bargain and a borrowed verdict ask different things of you. One asks you to accept a condition. The other asks you to accept somebody else’s judgement as your own.

Meeting either without recognising it makes a small surrender feel harmless. You agree once to keep the peace. Then the next request arrives with your first agreement already behind it.

Knowing the shape gives you time to decide what you consent to. Before keeping somebody happy becomes a promise you never remember making.

Draw the twelve with me, and let me show you where that first agreement sits.

Why your twelve must come from the Major Arcana

A tarot deck holds seventy-eight cards.

Twenty-two are the Major Arcana, including Justice, Temperance and the Devil. The remaining fifty-six describe the daily particulars. Your three speak about the arrangements those particulars belong to.

All three came from the twenty-two.

And all three are answering one another. So your full reading will use the Major Arcana only — twelve trumps, one to each house, read together.

This is the reading I reserve for a draw that reaches across a woman’s life. Love touches home. Home touches what she permits herself to want.

Your row makes those connections unmistakable. Reading only the love question would leave out the very thing that decides its answer.

And I’ll draw twelve new cards, dear. These three stay here. The new cards in the twelve houses supply the answers this first row has withheld.

The reading doesn’t choose your life for you. I wouldn’t want that power over you, and nothing in this draw asks you to give it away.

It gives you something you can use. In the conversation itself, while there is still time to say what you mean.

You know what you’re agreeing to.

You see where your release began, which way the affection is flowing, and what terms are being placed around it. You can let the good reach you with your own wishes still in the room.

That is how you keep the freedom the past has already given you.

Give me permission to read your twelve.

I’m on your side in this, dear.
