Past — Strength

A woman holds a lion’s jaws gently. Above her head is the sign of infinity. There’s no struggle in her face. The effort is there all the same.

Here, in your Past, Strength names the effort you learned to hide.

You carried more than anyone acknowledged, and you made it look easier than it was.

You felt hurt and made yourself speak gently. You needed something and told yourself it could come after everything else. When saying you were tired changed nothing, you stopped saying it.

And underneath the effort was something harder to admit. You wanted to be noticed without having to explain how much it took. To be known for more than what you could keep doing.

You kept carrying that too, dear.

This card records how you held yourself together. Every time you swallowed what you needed to say, you asked a little more of yourself. You’d learned to call that being strong.

There’s one detail I can’t give you from this card: which promise you made to yourself kept you carrying after you knew it was too much.

Strength shows the holding. The full spread identifies the promise behind it. I can’t name that promise from your Past card alone.
