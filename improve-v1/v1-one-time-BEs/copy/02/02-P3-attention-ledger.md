## The copy

<!-- TRANSITION · §6c. The gift is a second ACT, not a bonus paragraph -->

### And now the second part, %FIRSTNAME% — this one is yours to keep

When you have read your twelve properly — all of them, more than once — there is a second thing I
want to give you, and it costs you nothing but a minute a night.

I said in my letter that the difference is not whether things happen to you. It is whether they
happen to you from behind.

This is the practice that makes the difference. It is the only one I give away.

---

### What it is

Twenty-eight nights. One line a night. That is the whole of it.

Each night before you sleep, you write down **one thing you noticed that day that was different
from the day before.**

Not how you felt. Not what you hope. What you *noticed*. Something someone said that they don't
usually say. A message that came at an odd hour. A silence where there is normally noise. The way a
room went when you walked into it.

One line. Ordinary handwriting, ordinary paper, no ceremony. If nothing was different, write
*nothing was different* — that is a real entry and you will want it later.

The reason is simple and it is not mystical, dear. Your twelfth house is holding something unsaid,
and things like that are never invisible. They are only ever *unrecorded*. A woman notices eleven
small wrong notes across a fortnight, forgets ten of them, and is genuinely astonished by the
eleventh. She was told eleven times. She just had nowhere to put it.

The ledger is somewhere to put it.

---

### The four turns

**Nights 1 to 7 — the first turn.**
Just notice. Don't interpret, don't join anything up, and above all don't act on it. This week you
are only training the eye. Write the line and close the book.

⚠ Your reading said the first sign shows itself inside five to seven days. It lands in this turn.
You will not necessarily know it when it arrives — that is exactly why you are writing it down.

**Nights 8 to 14 — the second turn.**
Same one line, and add a second only when you genuinely have one. By now you will start wanting to
explain what you're writing. Resist it for one more week. An explanation written the same night is
almost always the explanation you already believed that morning.

**Night 15 — the read-back. This is the night the practice pays.**
Do not write anything first. Sit down and read your fourteen lines in one sitting, in order, start
to finish.

Something will come up off the page that was not visible on any single night. It always does. That
is the whole reason for the fourteen — not one of those lines is worth much alone and together they
are a pattern.

Then write your line for the fifteenth night, and write underneath it what you saw in the fourteen.

**Nights 15 to 21 — the third turn.**
Now you know what you have been noticing, so notice *that*. Your attention has something to point
at, and you will find you catch things on the day now instead of three days late.

**Nights 22 to 28 — the fourth turn.**
Keep writing, and this week let yourself act on what you have found. Ask the question. Decline the
thing. Say the sentence you have been keeping. You have three weeks of evidence behind you and you
are no longer guessing.

On the twenty-eighth night, read the whole ledger through.

---

### The two love weeks

**In the first two weeks, and again in the fourth**, pay particular attention to the people you
come across.

Your fifth house has something already in motion toward you, %FIRSTNAME%, and the Chariot said it
is not a stranger. That means it arrives as someone you already know behaving slightly differently
— which is precisely the kind of thing the eye skips and the ledger catches.

Someone you know may reappear in these weeks. Someone in the frame may step forward in it. Write it
down on the night, in the ordinary way, even when it seems far too small to mean anything.

Especially then. The ones that matter almost always look too small on the night.

---

### If nothing happens

Then you run the twenty-eight again.

I mean that plainly and not as a consolation. Some women's first cycle is all noise — four weeks of
*nothing was different* — and their second cycle is the one that shows the shape. The practice is
not a spell that either works or doesn't. It is an eye being trained, and eyes take a while.

The second twenty-eight is easier than the first, and it is the one where the read-back on the
fifteenth night stops you cold.

---

### And that is the gift

Keep the book. Not for me — I don't want to see it, and I'd rather you wrote freely than wrote
something you'd be willing to show a reader.

Keep it because in eight weeks you are going to want to know whether you saw it coming.

You did. It will be on the page in your own handwriting, three weeks before it happened, in a line
you thought was too small to matter.

That is what it is to stop finding out last.

## Build notes

- **The structure is the source's; the instruction is not.** Four 7-night cycles, a love window in
  weeks 1–2 and 4, and an explicit repeat-if-nothing line — all preserved, because that is what
  makes a free gift retain rather than decorate. Only the *action* changed: from gambling to
  noticing.
- **It is retention, and it is a window** (§6c). Twenty-eight nights gives her something to do for
  four weeks, and the next offer lands inside that window while she is still in daily contact with
  the practice. The fifteenth-night read-back is the natural high point to land near.
- **The read-back is the mechanism, not decoration.** A gratitude-journal instruction would be a
  nice gesture; *read fourteen entries in one sitting* produces an actual experience — pattern
  visible in aggregate and invisible night by night. It is also true, which matters more here than
  it usually does, because this is the asset a sceptical buyer judges the whole offer by.
- **It closes the product's own loop.** `02-P1`'s twelfth house tells her something is being kept
  from her and forbids her to go looking for it. Without the ledger, that instruction leaves her
  with nothing to do. With it, she has a discipline that is the *opposite* of searching — passive,
  recorded, patient — and it protects her from the failure mode where a woman goes through a
  partner's phone. The gift is the answer to the reading's hardest card.
- ⚠ **Do not add divination to this.** No candles, no moon phases, no drawing a card a night.
  Evelyn writes daily but she does not draw for individuals, and a daily-draw instruction here would
  contradict `02-E2`'s load-bearing origin beat *and* invent a one-card-daily programme that does
  not exist.
- ⚠ **Device variance.** 28 nights in four sevens is 02's alone. 03's free gift is nine nights, 05's
  sealing practice is nine, and 05's Four Watches are four *parts of one night* rather than four
  weeks — watch that pairing at the corpus pass. 02's own bump (`02-C4`) is deliberately a **single**
  night's working so the offer isn't selling two nightly practices in one checkout.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 2**, both mid-sentence, keeping the product's overall cadence.
