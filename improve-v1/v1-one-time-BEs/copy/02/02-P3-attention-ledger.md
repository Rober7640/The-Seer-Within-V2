## The copy

### And now the second part — this one is yours to keep

When you have finished with the twelve, there is a small practice you can take from them. It asks
for one plain observation each night and no promise that a pattern must appear.

Its purpose is to keep what happened separate from the story you first told yourself about it.

---

### What it is

Twenty-eight nights. Four turns of seven. One line a night.

Each night before you sleep, you write down **one thing you noticed that day that was different
from the day before.**

Not what you hoped would happen and not what the reading told you to expect. Record the observable
thing: a changed plan, an unexpected message, an offer, a refusal, a moment of relief, a silence
where you expected an answer.

Ordinary handwriting, ordinary paper, no ceremony. If nothing was different, write *nothing was
different*. That line counts. The page is a memory aid, not evidence that the reading must be true.

---

### The four turns

**Nights 1 to 7 — the first turn.**
Just notice. Don't interpret, don't join anything up, and above all don't act on it. This week you
are only training the eye. Write the line and close the book.

**Nights 8 to 14 — the second turn.**
Keep the same one line. You may already have an explanation for what you are recording. Leave that
explanation out of the entry. Facts are easier to inspect when the first theory is not written over
them.

**Night 15 — the read-back. This is the night the practice pays.**
Do not write anything first. Sit down and read your fourteen lines in one sitting, in order, start
to finish.

Mark what repeated, what contradicted your first explanation, and what remained ordinary. The page
may contain a pattern or fourteen unrelated observations. Do not force one result into the other.

Then write your line for the fifteenth night.

**Nights 15 to 21 — the third turn.**
Continue the same way. Where you formed an explanation at the read-back, give equal attention to
evidence that would disprove it. Do not monitor another person or collect a case against them.

**Nights 22 to 28 — the fourth turn.**
Keep writing without turning the ledger into an instruction. A repeated observation can be worth
considering; it is not, by itself, a reason to confront, accuse, spend, leave, or make a formal
decision.

On the twenty-eighth night, read the whole ledger through.

---

### If nothing happens

Then nothing failed. The page says that these twenty-eight nights did not form a reliable pattern.
Let that result stand.

You can stop there. If the practice itself helped you remember clearly, you may begin a fresh page
and run it again. Start without carrying the old explanation forward and without asking the next
page to confirm the first.

---

### And that is the gift

Keep the book. Not for me — I don't want to see it, and I'd rather you wrote freely than wrote
something you'd be willing to show a reader.

Keep it because memory changes when a story becomes important. The page lets you return to what you
actually noticed before deciding what it meant.

The ledger does not owe this reading a result. It gives your own observation somewhere steady to
stand.

## Build notes

- **The structure is the source's; the instruction is not.** Four 7-night cycles, one line nightly,
  and the night-15 read-back remain because the booking page and delivery nudge promise them. The
  love-window surveillance and repeat-until-confirmed logic were removed after the Phase 3 blind
  review. A fresh cycle is optional and begins without carrying an old explanation forward.
- **It is retention, and it is a window** (§6c). Twenty-eight nights gives her something to do for
  four weeks, and the next offer lands inside that window while she is still in daily contact with
  the practice. The fifteenth-night read-back is the natural high point to land near.
- **The read-back is the mechanism, not decoration.** Reading fourteen entries together can reveal
  repetition, contradiction, or no reliable pattern. All three are valid outcomes. The gift may
  improve recall, but it must never be presented as proof that the reading came true.
- **It does not close a prediction loop.** The ledger records ordinary observations and explicitly
  forbids monitoring another person, building a case, or treating the page as sufficient grounds
  for a consequential decision.
- ⚠ **Do not add divination to this.** No candles, no moon phases, no drawing a card a night.
  Evelyn writes daily but she does not draw for individuals, and a daily-draw instruction here would
  contradict `02-E2`'s load-bearing origin beat *and* invent a one-card-daily programme that does
  not exist.
- ⚠ **Device variance.** 28 nights in four sevens is 02's alone. 03's free gift is nine nights, 05's
  sealing practice is nine, and 05's Four Watches are four *parts of one night* rather than four
  weeks — watch that pairing at the corpus pass. 02's own bump (`02-C4`) is deliberately a **single**
  night's working so the offer isn't selling two nightly practices in one checkout.
- ⚠ **No merge tokens** — removed 2026-08-13 with D7 (one PDF for everyone). One took "dear"; the other reads better without. The gift renders inside `02-P1`, so it follows the same rule.
