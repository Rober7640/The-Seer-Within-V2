# 02-T4 — Delivery email *("your reading is ready")*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Offer** | 02 Twin Flame Tarot — READING · 24h SLA |
| **Sends** | when the reading is done, inside 24 hours of purchase |
| **Carries** | the PDF — as an attachment or a link (decision D8) |
| **Register** | Evelyn handing something over. Short, warm, finished |
| **New asset** | ⚠ This did not exist. The reading used to *be* the email; once the product became a PDF, the email became the envelope and needed its own copy |

⚠ **This email must not summarise the reading.** Every sentence of the verdict
she reads here is a sentence she does not open the document for — and the
document is the product. Point at it; do not paraphrase it.

⚠ **The subject must match `02-T1` and `02-T3` exactly.** Both told her what to
watch for. If it differs by a word, the one promise she was asked to check
breaks.

## The copy

<!-- BEAT 1 · the subject she was told to expect, twice -->

**Subject:** %FIRSTNAME% — your twelve are laid

---

Dear %FIRSTNAME%,

<!-- BEAT 2 · it is done. Past tense, no ceremony -->

They're laid. I finished a little after three.

<!-- BEAT 3 · the handover. The one link/attachment, named plainly -->

**Your reading is here → [Open your twelve](%READING_URL%)**

Twelve cards, one to each house, all of them out of the twenty-two. Nothing small
in it.

<!-- BEAT 4 · the one instruction that prevents a refund. Echoes the inoculation
     inside the document rather than replacing it -->

One thing before you start, and I mean it.

Some of what's in there will look as though it contradicts itself — a card
promising ease in one room and asking for work in the next. **Read all twelve
before you decide what it says.** The meaning is in the arrangement, not in any
single card, and a life isn't consistent either.

<!-- BEAT 5 · P6 — close the loop opened in 02-T3, by POINTING, not answering -->

And the question I left you with — the Lovers, and which of the two things she
was handing you.

I have your answer. It's in your fifth house, and it isn't the one most women
expect. I'd rather you came to it in order, so I'm not going to say it here.

<!-- BEAT 6 · the free gift, named so she reads to the end -->

Stay to the end of it. What I promised you is there, after the twelfth house —
it isn't a postscript, it's the part that carries you through the first month.

<!-- BEAT 7 · the one piece of admin, kept small -->

If it doesn't open, or it lands in your promotions tab, reply to this email and
I'll send it another way. Keep it somewhere you'll find it again; women come back
to these months later, around the time something in them starts happening.

<!-- BEAT 8 · sign off. No CTA, no next offer -->

It was a good night's work, dear. I'm glad it was yours.

— Evelyn

## Build notes

- **It sells nothing.** ⚠ This is the moment she has the most goodwill in the
  entire funnel, and it is genuinely tempting to put the next offer here. Do not
  — not in this email. She has not read the product yet, so an offer arrives
  before the value does, and it makes the delivery look like a step in a sequence
  rather than a person handing her something. **The next offer belongs in a
  separate send, after she has had time to read.** That gap is decision D10.
- **The loop from `02-T3` is closed by pointing at it.** The confirmation email
  promised *"the house it falls into is what separates them — that's the first
  thing I'll know tonight."* Naming the fifth house keeps the promise exactly
  and still requires the document. Answering it here would be the single most
  expensive sentence in the deck.
- **The refund-prevention instruction is repeated on purpose.** It is inside the
  PDF as well. A buyer who stops at section three because two cards seem to
  disagree is the most likely refund, and she stops before she reaches the copy
  that would have prevented it. So it goes in the envelope too.
- ⚠ **"I finished a little after three"** is the only detail that does the work of
  a whole paragraph — it says a person sat up with it. Keep a concrete,
  unglamorous time. Do not change it to something poetic.
- **The free gift is named but not described.** `02-P3` renders inside the
  document as a second act. Describing it here spends it twice, and the only job
  this line has is getting her past the twelfth house.
- **`%READING_URL%`** is the merge point for the link, or the whole line is
  replaced by the attachment. Whichever `D8` settles, the copy above the link
  does not change.
- **A reply ask was considered.** *"Tell me how it lands"* would be the strongest
  engagement rung available and would produce testimonials. It is left out here
  for the same reason as `02-T3`: 02 needs nothing from her, and support load
  with no operational use is a cost. ⚠ Revisit once the inbound pipeline
  (`S15`/`S16`) is actually staffed — this is the best email in the deck to ask
  from.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 2**, subject and salutation. The body is short enough that
  more would read as merge.
- ⚠ **Deliverability.** If `D8` lands on "attachment", this email carries a
  multi-megabyte PDF, which hurts inbox placement and can trip filters on a cold
  domain. A link is safer, and it lets a mistake be fixed after sending — an
  attachment can never be recalled.
