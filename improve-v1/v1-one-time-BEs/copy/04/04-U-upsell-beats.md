# 04-U2b — Stone selection, tied to the tea symbols

⚠ **This is 04's own asset and it is the reason U2 converts better here than anywhere.** The buyer
has just been given three good symbols by name. The stone selection reads them back to her, so
choosing a stone feels like continuing her reading rather than picking merchandise.

> Now — which of your three good symbols do you want it charged to?

> All three came up in your cup, {firstName}. They do different work and you'll know which one you
> need.

**Quick replies:**

| Text | Value |
|---|---|
| The bridge — I want him to cross | `bridge` |
| The lighthouse — I want to be steady | `lighthouse` |
| The butterfly — I want the change to hold | `butterfly` |

**`bridge`**
> The bridge. Of course.

> Then it gets charged to being *crossed toward* — which is a different intention from being
> attractive, and a much better one.

> A bridge doesn't pull anybody over it. It just makes the crossing possible and then it waits,
> perfectly still, and that stillness is the whole of why it works.

> Wear it on your right wrist. The right is your sending hand — what goes out from you. This one
> broadcasts *the way across is open* without you having to say it in week two.

**`lighthouse`**
> The lighthouse — and I'd have guessed that one from your cup.

> It sat opposite your handle, which is as far from you as a symbol gets. You've had a fixed point
> in your life this whole time and you've been navigating by him instead.

> Charged to the lighthouse, the stone holds *you* steady rather than reaching for him. It's the
> quietest of the three and it's the one that changes the most.

> Right wrist. What it sends out is a woman who is not waiting, and that is the single most
> attractive thing in this entire situation.

**`butterfly`**
> The butterfly. The highest thing in your cup.

> That one's not about him at all, {firstName}, and choosing it tells me something good about where
> you are.

> Charged to the butterfly, the stone holds the change that's already happening in you — so that
> whatever he does across those twenty-one days, the woman at the end of them is the one who came
> out of it, not the one who went in.

> Right wrist. It broadcasts the change rather than the wanting.

# 04-U1a — U1 opening beats

✅ **U1 RUNS after 04** *(operator, 2026-08-04 — full ladder everywhere)*. An earlier draft
recommended suppressing it and left five thin beats behind. It ships, so it was rebuilt.

⚠ **The risk beat is NOT a returning threat.** V1's frame — *old shadows try to return* — has no
purchase here; 04's buyer isn't afraid of something coming back, she's afraid of doing the wrong
thing on the wrong day. The real danger to her twenty-one days is already named in `04-P1`'s third
expectation: **somebody close to her is going to talk her out of it.** That is a specific, true,
recognisable threat, it is derived from the product rather than invented, and it does not require
inventing a rival.

## `UPSELL_CONFIRMATION` — 3 messages

> It's done, {firstName}. Your cup goes on the table tonight and your twenty-one days come with it.

> You'll have the whole sequence by morning — which week to be quiet, which week the bridge is for,
> and how old the bird actually is.

> One thing before I go, because it's the thing most likely to cost you this.

## `UPSELL_GAP` — 4 messages

> The sequence works because of the quiet. The first nine days are the ones that do it.

> But nine days of quiet is not nine days of nothing, {firstName}. It's nine days of everything
> reaching you with no reply going out.

> His silence. Your own version of events at two in the morning. And — this is the one — everybody
> else's opinion about what you should be doing instead.

> A woman holding a line she can't explain is the easiest woman in the world to talk out of it.

## `UPSELL_RISK` — 5 messages

> And somebody is going to try. I'd put money on it.

> Not an enemy. Someone who loves you, who has watched you be unhappy for months and wants it over
> — and who is going to tell you, kindly, to stop overthinking and just be honest with him.

> They'll say it around day five. They always do. Day five is when you look like you're suffering
> for no reason.

> The trouble is they're not wrong about the honesty, {firstName} — being honest with him is days
> sixteen to eighteen. It's *in* the sequence. What they're actually proposing is that you do it
> eleven days early.

> And you will not be able to argue back, because the case for waiting is a tea reading, and you
> will hear how that sounds in your own mouth before you finish saying it.

## `UPSELL_QUESTION_1`

> Tell me honestly — is there somebody in your life who already thinks you're being too patient

**Replies:** `Yes, and they've said so` → `yes` · `Probably` → `maybe` · `I haven't told anyone` →
`unsure`

> Let me keep it off you while you work.

**`default`**
> Then you know the shape of what I mean.

> The sequence is sound. What it needs is nine days you don't get talked out of.

## Build notes

- **U2's hinge is the brief's own limit**, stated in `04-P1`'s closing section: leaves give words,
  not removal. Here it becomes *the sequence gives you the words; it doesn't give him anything to
  walk toward.* Same mechanism, sold rather than confessed.
- **The butterfly is the right hook for both paths** because it is the one symbol in her cup that
  is about *her* and already in motion — so the upsell opens on something good and hers, rather than
  on a new fear. 04's buyer has had enough fear.
- ⚠ **The stone goes on the RIGHT wrist here, not the left.** V1's lava stone is left — the
  receiving hand, filtering what comes in. The manifestation bracelet is right — the sending hand.
  `UPSELL2_RITUAL_PATH_A_EXTRA` already depends on that pairing (*"lava stone on your left filtering
  what comes IN, bracelet on your right amplifying what goes OUT"*). Do not swap them.
- **`04-U2b` is the deck's only stone selection driven by the buyer's own reading**, and it is why
  U2 belongs to 04. ⚠ The three options must stay the three *good* symbols. Offering the bird or the
  broken heart would be charging a stone to a fear.
- **The `lighthouse` branch quietly does the most work** — *the single most attractive thing in this
  situation is a woman who is not waiting* is the offer's whole thesis restated as a sales line, and
  it is the branch to watch in the data.
- **U1 ships and was rebuilt for it** *(operator, 2026-08-04)*. ⚠ Its risk beat is **the
  well-meaning friend**, taken from `04-P1`'s third expectation — not a returning threat, which 04
  has no room for. That makes it the only U1 in the deck whose danger is a person who loves the
  buyer, and it is both truer and more specific than the rival V1's version reaches for.
- ⚠ **Day five is named in U1 and in the product and in `04-P3`.** That is deliberate repetition
  inside one offer: predicting the exact day the pressure arrives is what makes it survivable. Keep
  the three consistent — if the sequence's quiet stretch ever changes length, all three move.
