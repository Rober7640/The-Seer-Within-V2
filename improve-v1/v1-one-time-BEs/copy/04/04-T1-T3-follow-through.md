# 04-T3 — Confirmation email + wait-filler

**Subject:** Your cup is turned tonight

---

Dear %FIRSTNAME%,

Thank you for deciding this, and for deciding it quickly. Your leaves were still soft when you did,
which means your dates will be dates and not fortnights.

I'm starting tonight. Your reading will be with you inside **24 hours**, titled *%FIRSTNAME% — your
cup is turned*.

Nothing is needed from you. Not tonight and not tomorrow.

---

### Which is the point, actually

<!-- P5 · register matched to the buyer: ANXIOUS, and likely to act tonight.
     Per P5, hers must argue against acting early. -->

Let me be direct with you about the next twenty-four hours, %FIRSTNAME%, because I know roughly what
they contain.

You have just bought a reading about *when to speak*. Which means that somewhere in the back of your
mind, the fact that you have taken action has already loosened something — and a loosened woman at
ten at night with a phone in her hand is exactly the woman who sends the message.

**Don't send it tonight.**

Not because it's the wrong message. I haven't read your cup yet and I don't know. Because whatever
it is, it will still be sendable on Thursday, and by Thursday you will know whether Thursday is the
week for it.

Twenty-four hours. That's all I'm asking. You have waited weeks already; this is the cheapest wait
you will ever do and it is the only one with a date on the end of it.

---

### And something to hold while you wait

`[IMG — a cup mid-pour, or nothing]`

Here's a piece of the practice I like, and it has nothing to do with your reading.

When I was learning, the woman who taught me made me read forty cups before she let me say a word
about any of them. Forty. I would turn the cup, look, write down what I saw, and hand her the paper,
and she would put it in a drawer without reading it.

Around the thirtieth I got frustrated enough to ask what the drawer was for.

She said: *"You are not learning to read tea. Anybody can learn that in a fortnight. You are
learning to look at something and not say anything about it yet."*

I have thought about that a great deal since, dear, and not only about tea.

Almost everything I have got badly wrong in my life, I got wrong in the gap between noticing
something and responding to it — and the gap is always shorter than it needs to be. Not because
I was stupid. Because the noticing was uncomfortable, and saying something is how you make the
discomfort stop.

Handing it over stops the feeling. It doesn't improve the outcome.

She was, incidentally, a difficult woman who never once told me I'd done well at anything. I miss
her enormously.

---

If it's any use, pass it on. Most people know somebody who could use a drawer.

Your cup is on the table. I'll write in the morning.

— Evelyn

## Build notes

- **P5 — the register is anxiety and the wait-filler argues against acting early**, exactly as the
  framework specifies for 04. 02's donkey is encouragement across a hopeful day; 03's roof is
  inoculation across three angry nights; this one is a story about **not speaking yet**, sent to a
  woman whose entire problem is speaking too soon. Three offers, three parables, three jobs.
- ⚠ **"Don't send it tonight" is the highest-value paragraph in 04's follow-through**, and it is
  placed above the story so it is read. The 24-hour window is the offer's most dangerous stretch:
  she has just acted, the relief of acting is real, and acting is what breaks the sequence she has
  not received yet.
- **The parable earns its length by being about the buyer's exact error** without mentioning him
  once. *The gap between noticing and responding* is the drafted-message problem stated as craft
  lore, and the closing detail — a difficult woman, never praised her, misses her enormously —
  is there to keep it a memory rather than a lesson.
- **The share ask is quiet and specific** — *"most people know somebody who could use a drawer"* —
  matching 02's and 03's engagement rung without their wording.
- ⚠ **The Still Cup ships immediately on `04-T1`**, unlike 03's bump. It is preparation, it was sold
  as *tonight*, and it is the practical form of *don't send it tonight*. The three offers now differ
  deliberately on bump timing: 02 before the product, 04 before the product, 03 after it.
- **Both assets say "you don't need to send me anything."** It is 04's genuine differentiator and it
  removes the last worry a buyer who has seen 03 might carry over.
