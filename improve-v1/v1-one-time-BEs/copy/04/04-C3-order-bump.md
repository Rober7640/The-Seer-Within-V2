## The copy

**☐ Yes — I want something to do with the evenings.**

<!-- RULE 3 · names the MAIN offer's limit -->

The turned cup will tell me which week to speak in. It will not get me through the nine o'clock on
a Tuesday when I have decided to send something anyway.

So yes — send me **the Still Cup** as well.

<!-- BEAT 3 · what it removes, and what it has been costing -->

Four minutes, every evening, in the hour I'd otherwise spend rereading his last message looking for
tone.

That hour is where all of this goes wrong. Not in the daytime — I am fine in the daytime. It goes
wrong between putting my phone down for the fourth time and actually going to bed, and it has cost
me more ground than anything he has done.

<!-- BEAT 4 · ease + immediacy -->

Four lines of instruction and I can begin this evening, before the reading has even arrived. One
cup, four minutes, nothing to buy.

## Build notes

- **This is the best bump in the deck and §4 rule 4 says why: it protects the product.** The single
  likeliest way 04 fails is that she buys a 21-day sequence and breaks it on night three with a
  message sent at 9pm. A bump that raises the odds the main offer works is worth more than one that
  merely sells, and this one is aimed directly at the failure.
- **Rule 1 — adjacent, not identical.** The reading is *what and when*; this is *the hour in
  between*. Genuinely different problems, so no cannibalisation. ⚠ It must never drift into telling
  her what to send — that is the paid reading, and a bump that gave message advice would compete
  with the thing it is attached to.
- **Rule 2 — self-performed.** A cup and four minutes. Evelyn ships nothing.
- ⚠ **It starts tonight, before the reading lands.** That is deliberate: it is the one thing she can
  do in the 24-hour wait, which makes the wait shorter and gets the habit established before the
  sequence begins. `04-T1` must deliver it immediately, the way `02-T1` delivers Astro Force —
  ⚠ **unlike `03-C4`**, which is aftercare and ships with the record.
- **The copy names the hour rather than the emotion.** *"Between putting my phone down for the
  fourth time and actually going to bed"* is more recognisable than any description of anxiety, and
  it is the buyer describing her own evening in her own voice.
- ⚠ **Device variance:** 04's is a **nightly** practice, which is close to 02's 28-night ledger.
  They stay distinct because 02's is *writing what she noticed* over four weeks and this is *four
  minutes with a cup* during the waiting weeks — different action, different duration, different
  purpose. Logged for the corpus pass. Nothing here is written down and nothing is burned.
