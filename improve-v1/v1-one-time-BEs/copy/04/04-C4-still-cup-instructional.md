## The copy

# The Still Cup

### Four minutes, every evening

%FIRSTNAME%,

Do this tonight, before your reading has even arrived, and then every evening for the twenty-one
days.

It is a very small thing and it is aimed at the one hour that has been costing you anything.

---

## The hour

You are fine in the daytime. That is what women tell me and I believe it — there is work, there are
people, there is a version of you that functions perfectly well.

It goes wrong between about nine and bed.

That is when you reread his last message looking for tone. When you check whether he's been online.
When the reasonable, well-argued thing you are going to send starts assembling itself, and by
eleven it is finished and it feels overdue rather than early.

Everything this reading is trying to prevent happens in that hour. Not in the relationship — in the
hour.

So we're going to put something in it.

---

## What you need

A cup, a pot or a kettle, and tea. Any tea. Loose is better, and a bag opened into the cup is
absolutely fine.

Nothing else, ever.

---

## The four minutes

**1. Make it properly, and take the time.**
Warm the pot if you have one. Wait for the water off the boil. This part is not ceremony — it is
that the making has to be long enough to be the thing you are doing, rather than something you do
while looking at your phone.

**Your phone is in another room for this.** That is not a suggestion; it is the entire mechanism.
Everything else here is scaffolding around getting your phone out of your hand at nine o'clock.

**2. Sit down with it and don't drink it yet.**
Hold it. Wait until it is cool enough to drink and you have not drunk it. That will be about two
minutes and it will feel much longer the first three nights.

**3. While you wait, one question.**
Ask yourself: *what did I notice today that I haven't told anybody?*

Don't answer it in words and don't write it down — that is a different practice and it belongs to a
different reading. Just let it arrive and sit with it while the tea cools.

**4. Drink it. Then leave the cup where it is until morning.**

Don't rinse it, don't take it to the sink. Leave it on the table with the leaves in the bottom.

That's the four minutes.

---

## Why the cup stays out

Two reasons, and the second is the real one.

**The first is craft.** Leaves settle overnight into the shape the evening put them in. You are not
reading them — I'd rather you didn't try, and a woman reading her own cup about a man she is
anxious about will find precisely what she is afraid of. But the settling is real and there is
something in doing a thing properly even when nobody is checking.

**The second is that you have to walk past it in the morning.**

A used cup on the table at seven in the morning is a small piece of evidence that you got through
last night. On day five that is worth a great deal. You will have wanted to send something, and you
will not have, and there is the cup.

Rinse it then, and start again that evening.

---

## What this does

It does not make you feel better. I want to be honest about that.

What it does is take the worst hour of your day and put four minutes of structure at the front of
it — and four minutes is enough, because the hour is not driven by feeling. It is driven by *having
your phone in your hand with nothing else happening.* Remove that and most of it doesn't assemble.

Women tell me the first three nights are difficult and the fourth is easy. That has been my
experience of it too.

---

## And when the twenty-one days are up

Stop, or don't. This is the one practice I give away that you may keep if you want it, because it
is only a cup of tea and it asks nothing of you.

But it stops being *for* anything on day twenty-two, and if you find yourself still doing it in
March specifically because of him, that's worth noticing.

— Evelyn

---

## Build notes

- **The mechanism is stated plainly rather than mystified**: *everything else here is scaffolding
  around getting your phone out of your hand at nine o'clock.* That honesty is what makes it work
  for a sceptical buyer and what makes Evelyn credible when she is less literal elsewhere.
- **The morning cup is the best beat in this deliverable** and it is what turns four minutes into a
  streak. Physical evidence of a night survived, encountered without going to look for it. It is the
  same device as `04-P3`'s crossing-off, in a different sense.
- ⚠ **She is told NOT to read her own cup**, and the reason given is true: an anxious woman reading
  for herself finds what she fears. This also protects the paid product — if the bump let her do her
  own tasseography, it would compete with the reading it is attached to (§4 rule 1).
- ⚠ **Nothing is written down here.** The question in step 3 is deliberately left unanswered in
  words: writing what she noticed is **02's attention ledger**, and 04 must not reproduce it. Same
  raw instinct, two different offers, and only one of them writes.
- **Rule 4 — it protects the main product**, more directly than any other bump in the deck. The
  21-day sequence's failure mode is a message sent at nine on day five; this is aimed at nine on
  day five.
- ⚠ **The "keep it if you like" ending is 04's alone.** 02's Astro Force is *once*, 03's water is
  *nine nights then stop*, 03's Unburdening is *never repeat*. This one may continue because it is
  genuinely harmless — but the closing line still names the failure state (*still doing it in March
  because of him*), so it does not become a monument either.
