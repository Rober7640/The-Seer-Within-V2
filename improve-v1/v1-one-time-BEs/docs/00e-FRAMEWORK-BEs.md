# 00e — COMPONENT FRAMEWORK

Reverse-engineered from **02 Twin Flame Tarot**, the only complete specimen in the source
(833 lines; 03/04/05 are 89–187 lines of letter and nothing else). Every component below is
abstracted into beats you fill, plus the rules that make the beat work. Build 03, 04 and 05 by
filling these in — do not re-invent structure per offer.

Companions: [00-FLOW](./00-FLOW-BEs.md) · [00b-BUILD](./00b-BUILD-BEs.md) ·
[00c-ASSETS](./00c-ASSETS-BEs.md)

---

## 0. THE BRIEF — fill this before writing a word

Four fields. Nothing downstream gets written until they're filled, because everything downstream
*derives* from them. Structure without a brief produces copy that is correct and inert — the
components get filled competently with borrowed ideas.

| # | Field | What it is | The test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **THE ONE PROBLEM** | Her situation, in her words | Could she have typed this sentence into the V1 chat? Source it from real `concern` data, don't invent it |
| 2 | **THE BIG IDEA** | The single arresting concept the letter dramatises | One sentence, no jargon, and it **reframes** the problem rather than restating it |
| 3 | **THE ONE PROMISE** | The outcome at the emotional floor | Run So What? three times. Something she'd *feel*, not something we'd *do*. Never falsifiable, never about a third party |
| 4 | **THE MECHANISM** | Why it works, in craft terms | Does it explain the price, the wait AND the limits without marketing language? |

**What derives from what:**

```
  headline                ← big idea
  the free read's threat  ← the enemy inside the problem
  the fork (§1a unit 2)   ← the gap between problem and promise
  statement 6 reason-why  ← mechanism
  SLA justification       ← mechanism
  scarcity                ← mechanism
  wait-filler register    ← the problem's emotional state
  the product's shape     ← mechanism
  the next offer's hook   ← the mechanism's own LIMIT
```

**The mechanism is the field that earns its keep.** A good one explains the price, the wait and
the boundaries all at once. 03's — *three spirits, three offices, one petitioned per night* —
justifies the three-day SLA, the limited slots, and the product's three-verdict structure with a
single idea. If your mechanism only explains one of those, it isn't finished.

### 0a. The four briefs

**The filled briefs live in [00a-BRIEFS-BEs.md](./00a-BRIEFS-BEs.md) — the single source of
truth.** They are not duplicated here: two copies drift, and the brief is the one thing in this
system that must not.

That file carries, per offer: the four fields, the verbatim source lines grounding each, what the
mechanism explains (price / wait / limits), where we reframed the source and why, and the limit
that hands off to the next offer.

## 0b. Then: which archetype is this offer?

The four offers are **not** the same shape. Two archetypes, and the archetype determines how
every component below gets filled. Get this wrong and the components fight each other.

| | **READING offer** | **ACT offer** |
|---|---|---|
| Offers | **02** Twin Flame · **04** Tea Reading | **03** Judgement Day · **05** Hex Her |
| Free unit | a *partial* read | a *diagnostic* read |
| Paid unit | the **complete read** — same mechanic, more of it | an **intervention performed on their behalf** |
| Core verb | *reveal* | *do* |
| Ask framing | "give me permission to draw the rest" | "give me permission to act" |
| Price model | fixed ($35) or ladder ($35→67) | pay-what-you-want, anchor-collapsed |
| Needs a reply? | **No** | **Yes** — they must name the target |
| Scarcity | soft or none | hard — limited slots, "each ritual takes N days" |
| SLA justifies | preparation time | the ritual's own duration |

**Why the price models differ, and it isn't arbitrary:** a *product* has a price; a *personal
act of service* is priced by what you can contribute. The source prices readings and asks for
donations on hexes. Keep that — a fixed price on a hex reads as a transaction on someone's life,
and a donation ask on a reading reads as charity.

**The tell for which archetype you're writing:** if the buyer could receive the deliverable
without you knowing anything new about them, it's a READING. If you need them to tell you a
name, it's an ACT.

## 1. Component: the ESL email

**Driven by:** big idea → headline · problem → the free read · promise → the deck · mechanism → the authority flourish

The whole product lives in the body. 17 beats in 02's v1. First ask arrives at ~60% — **nothing
is asked until the free value is completely delivered.**

| # | Beat | Job | 02's instance | Rule for 03/04/05 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero image | Prove the act physically happened | photo of a 3-card spread on cloth | Photograph the *mechanic*, not a stock mystic. 04 = the cup. 03/05 = the altar mid-ritual |
| 2 | Headline | Name the unsolicited act + who it was for | "%FIRSTNAME%, For An Unusual Reason I've Drawn Three Tarot Cards For You" | `{NAME} + [unusual reason] + [act already done] + for you` |
| 3 | Deck | Payoff + timeframe | "A MASSIVE Change In Your Love Life… FIRST Appearance In Just 5-7 Days" | Always attach a *window*. Vague futures don't sell |
| 4 | Salutation | Intimacy | "%FIRSTNAME% -" then "Madame Delacroix here." | Name, dash, persona names herself. Never "Dear customer" |
| 5 | Urgency opener | Justify the interruption | "I write to you with urgency." | One line. The letter must feel *sent*, not scheduled |
| 6 | Origin story | Why now, and why unbidden | woke from a dream; "my cards SPOKE to me ASKING to be picked" | **The act must be uninvited.** Nobody asked. That's what makes it a gift, not a pitch |
| 7 | Stakes preview | Pre-frame the good and the threat | "don't miss the LUCK… DEFINITELY don't ignore the warning of the THIRD DRAW" | Announce the shape before delivering it. Two goods, one threat |
| 8 | Precedent | One named prior case | Jane, DECADES ago, found her twin flame | **One** name, distant in time, outsized outcome. Not testimonials |
| 9 | Withhold + condition | Establish incompleteness early | "I'll reveal this in a moment. But… you need a complete spread" | Plant the paid thing *before* the free thing is delivered |
| 10 | **The free read** | Deliver real value | 3 cards | See §1a below — this is the engine |
| 11 | First CTA | The ask, after full delivery | line 117, ~60% in | **Never before the free read completes** |
| 12 | Re-read | Second pass on cards 2 and 3 | "let's take another look at the Lovers…" | Deepen, don't repeat. The re-read is where the threat gets specific |
| 13 | Concrete ask | Convert desire to instruction | "In concrete terms, what do you have to do after this draw?" | Answer the question they now have: *what do I do?* |
| 14 | Authority flourish | Justify the price | Major Arcana; "22 Major, 56 minor"; "something I reserve for my protégés" | One piece of genuine craft knowledge + one scarcity of access |
| 15 | Final CTA | "Follow me HERE" | | |
| 16 | Transformation promise | | "transform the rest of your life… the key to all sorts of mysteries" | |
| 17 | Sign-off | | "Your devoted friend, Madame Delacroix" | Relationship word, not a role |

### 1a. The free read — the engine

02 delivers **three units: two blessings and one threat.** This is the load-bearing structure.

```
  UNIT 1  BLESSING          the World    "luck will favor you", end of misfortune
  UNIT 2  BLESSING + FORK   the Lovers   two options — but which is right?  ← creates a question
  UNIT 3  THREAT            the Tower    an ex returns · a lover pulls away ← creates fear
```

- **Unit 2 must contain a fork.** It hands them a question the free read structurally cannot
  answer. That question is what the paid product answers. Without the fork there's no reason to buy.
- **Unit 3 names a person.** Not "obstacles" — an ex, a rival, a lover pulling away. 04 does this
  too ("a bird taking flight… he might even be looking at another woman"). Abstract threats don't
  convert; a person does.
- **Every unit ends incomplete** — "I could provide you with further details if you accept…"

04's seven tea symbols already match this (4 bad → 3 good). 03 and 05 run a compressed version:
03's is a single aura sensing, 05's is three Grand Etteilla cards. **ACT offers can run a thinner
free read** because the paid thing isn't more reading — it's action.

### 1b. CTA rules

- **Six links in v1**, all to the same URL, spread across the final 40%.
- **Every CTA is a sentence, not a button.** "you must draw 12 new cards here" · "allow me to
  read your full 12 cards" · "Follow me HERE". The link is embedded in an *instruction*.
- **The verb is never "buy".** It is *permission*, *allow*, *request*, *follow*, *book*.
  The buyer isn't purchasing; they're authorising work.
- Track each position separately — knowing which of the six converts is how the next letter gets written.

### 1c. Component: the v2 follow-up email

02 ships a **second letter** (`02:199-375`) — "I've Drawn Three MORE Tarot Cards For You" — sent
to non-buyers. 03, 04 and 05 have none. This is the most-missing asset in the deck.

**It is not a new letter. It is the same skeleton with a fresh draw.** What changes:

| Beat | v1 | v2 | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero image | 3-card spread | **the same image reused** | Don't reshoot |
| Headline | "For An Unusual Reason I've Drawn Three Tarot Cards" | "I've Drawn Three **MORE** Tarot Cards" | The word MORE does the work |
| Urgency opener | "I write to you with urgency" | "I write to you with **more news from the psychic realm**" | Softer — urgency can't be spent twice |
| Origin story | a dream; the cards "SPOKE to me ASKING to be picked" | **compressed to one line** — "Today I started a FREE tarot drawing for you" | The relationship is established; re-justifying is weak |
| Precedent (Jane) | present | **dropped entirely** | Proof is a first-contact device |
| The free read | World · Lovers · Tower | Star · Emperor · Moon | **New units, identical roles** |
| Stakes / withhold / authority / close | | **verbatim** | Reuse |

**The three roles carry over exactly** — this is what makes v2 cheap to write:

```
  BLESSING        v1 the World   → v2 the Star
  BLESSING+FORK   v1 the Lovers  → v2 the Emperor  ("a decision to be made")
  THREAT          v1 the Tower   → v2 the Moon
```

⚠ **The threat escalates in intimacy, not volume.** v1's Tower is external and hypothetical —
"an ex coming back", "a lover pulling away". v2's Moon is present-tense and closer — "a
relationship in turmoil", "a lover **keeping secrets and giving you false signals**." Second
contact earns a more intimate accusation, not a louder one.

**To build v2 for 03/04/05:** keep the skeleton, swap the three units, compress the origin,
drop the precedent, escalate the threat inward. It's roughly a third of the work of v1.

## 2. Component: the subject-line bank

**Driven by:** big idea + the problem's sharpest phrase

02 ships 7; 03 has 2, 04 has 1, 05 has **zero**. Minimum 5 per offer.

Patterns observed in 02, all reusable:

| Pattern | 02's instance |
|---|---|
| Name + a question about the near future | "%FIRSTNAME% - a chance encounter next week?" |
| Name + an intimate, unprompted event | "%FIRSTNAME% - I had a dream about you" |
| Name + threat | "%FIRSTNAME% - your love is in danger" |
| Bare deadline, no name | "Your last chance to prevent it…" |
| Name + invitation to look | "%FIRSTNAME% - peek into your future" |

⚠ **Override from our own data:** the proven format on this list is **emoji + first-name FIRST +
curiosity** (30–40% opens). Quiet, literary, name-at-the-end subjects fall to ~22%. Follow our
data, not the source's typography.

## 3. Component: the booking page

**Driven by:** mechanism → statement 5 (SLA), statement 6 (reason-why), the scarcity line · big idea → statement 2, the permission the buyer grants · promise → the request paragraph

Six agreement statements, ordered cheapest → most expensive commitment, **price last**.

| # | Statement's job | 02's instance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Affirm the reader was right** (not merely accept the diagnosis) — see P8 | "you're entirely right… the message from the 3 tarot cards makes me want to find out much more" |
| 2 | Grant permission | "I give permission to [persona] to draw my destiny" |
| 3 | Accept its weight | "done by a professional and its instructions should not be taken lightly" |
| 4 | Accept scarcity | "a once in a lifetime opportunity… might never present itself again" |
| 5 | Accept the effort + SLA | "an intense time-consuming service… minimum of 24 hours" |
| 6 | **Accept the price** | "I agree to pay a modest one-time sum of $35.00" |

Then, still in the buyer's voice:

7. **The request** — "[Persona], you are now in possession of my complete spread. I therefore
   request that you produce MY COMPLETE READING…" — and it ends on **the promise**, in her voice:
   *"…so that I stop being caught off guard by my own life."*
8. **The free gift, received gratefully** — "I understand that you will also send me free of
   charge MY PERSONAL HOROSCOPE… Thank you for this wonderful FREE GIFT."

### The three rules that make this page work

1. **Everything is first person, in the buyer's voice.** They aren't reading a sales page; they
   are signing a statement they appear to have authored. That is the entire commitment device.
2. **The price is buried in statement 6.** Five agreements happen before money is mentioned, and
   by then agreeing is a habit. Do not move it up.
3. **The page never says "buy."** Booking, permission, request, produce.

Per-offer fill: **READING offers** use "draw / produce my reading." **ACT offers** swap statement 2
to authorise an intervention ("I give permission to cast"), and statement 5 cites the ritual's
own duration rather than preparation time.

### Patches from the 03 smoke test (2026-08-03)

**P1 — ACT offers need a NINTH element: the intake instruction, before payment.**
An ACT offer cannot be fulfilled without the buyer's reply. If the page doesn't instruct it, you
end up holding paid orders you cannot fill. Place it directly above the button, in the buyer's
voice like everything else:

> *"After I confirm I will reply to your email with their first name, how they came into my life,
> and what they did. I understand Evelyn cannot begin without it."*

**P2 — statement 6 needs a REASON-WHY whenever the price isn't a fixed number.**
The ladder works because each statement is a claim you assent to. *"I will contribute what I am
able"* asserts nothing — no proposition, so the rung collapses exactly where the device is meant
to be strongest. Supply a reason and the reason becomes the thing agreed to:

> *"Yes, I understand why Evelyn sets no price for this. A working done for money owes something
> to the money — this one will owe nothing to anyone but me. I will give what I am able, and it
> will not change what she does."*

The final clause is load-bearing: at a blank amount field the buyer's live question is *how much,
and will less get me less?* Answer it there or they abandon.

**P3 — the CTA should complete a sentence the buyer just read.** The page is their first person
throughout; an administrative button label steps outside it. If the request paragraph says *"take
this off my hands"*, the button says **TAKE THIS OFF MY HANDS** — still a permission verb, now
also the payoff.

**P8 — statement 1 affirms that the READER was right, not that the buyer has a problem.**
Discovered by working 02's booking page forward from the brief and comparing: the derivation
produced *"the three cards have not left my mind"* — accurate, and weaker. The source has
*"you're **entirely right** and I completely agree with you."*

The difference is who the sentence is about. Describing herself is self-report; affirming the
reader's accuracy opens a small relationship debt, and every statement after it is repayment. It
also costs nothing — she's agreeing to something she already believes, which is why it works as
the cheapest rung on the ladder.

> ✗ "The three cards you drew have not left my mind."
> ✓ "You were right, Evelyn. You read it correctly, and I want to see the rest of what you saw."

**P9 — the free gift is thanked for BEFORE it is received.**
The source has the buyer write *"Thank you, [persona], for this wonderful FREE GIFT that you are
giving me."* Gratitude expressed in advance is a commitment device: she has now performed the
transaction's emotional half before paying its financial half. Never describe the gift neutrally
on this page — have her receive it.

**P4 — ACT scarcity copy creates a code dependency.** *"When the week is spoken for, this page
closes"* requires `S8`, the capacity cap. Build it or don't write the line.

### 3a. Ladder offers — the ascent needs a reason

Only 04 uses this. A price that rises daily is counterintuitive, and the source never explains it,
so it reads as a penalty for hesitating. Hopkins' rule applies: never claim, explain why.

The reason must come from the mechanic, not from marketing. For 04 the cup literally dries — a
later reading is harder work, so it costs more. That turns the ladder from a squeeze into a
consequence of craft, and makes today's price feel like a courtesy rather than a trap.

⚠ **Copy/code coupling:** statement 6 states *today's* price, resolved server-side per `S9`. The
copy carries a merge field, never a hard-coded number.

## 4. Component: the order bump

**Driven by:** a problem *adjacent* to the brief's — never the same one, or it cannibalises

**EVERY offer carries a bump.** 02 is the only one the source supplies; 03, 04 and 05 need one
written. Four beats:

1. **Title = YES + benefit + permanence** — "Yes! I Want To Remove Negative KARMA forever!"
2. **Named mechanism** — "my POWERFUL Astro Force ritual". A mechanism with a name is a product;
   without one it's a promise.
3. **What it removes, and what that's been costing them** — "DISPEL any negative KARMA you built
   up through your life that's PREVENTING you from having any good luck and brings MISFORTUNE"
4. **Ease + immediacy** — "a simple and clear instructional you can start tonight"

**Rule 1 — adjacent, not identical.** Karma-clearing sits beside a tarot reading without competing
with it. Derive the adjacency from the brief: if the offer answers the brief's problem, the bump
answers the one standing next to it. 02 = *sight* (what's coming) vs *weight* (what's already
sitting on you).

**Rule 2 — a bump is a self-performed instructional. An upsell is something the persona does or
ships.** This is what keeps a bump from cannibalising U1/U2. 02's bump is explicitly *"a simple
and clear instructional you can start tonight"* — text, zero fulfilment, performed by the buyer.
U1 is a stone Evelyn charges and posts. A protection *practice* on the booking page and a
protection *object* in the upsell are different products; a second charged object would not be.

**Rule 3 — the bump's first line names the main offer's limit.** *"The spread will show me what is
coming. It will not lift what is already sitting on me."* This does three jobs at once: it
justifies the bump, it makes the main offer more credible by bounding it, and it satisfies §8's
rule that every product names its own limit.

**Rule 4 — the best bumps protect the main product.** 04's stops her sending a rash message that
would break the 21-day sequence she just bought. A bump that increases the odds the main offer
works is worth more than one that merely sells.

### 4a. The four bumps

| Offer | Adjacent problem | Bump | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **02** Twin Flame | *sight* → the weight already there | **Astro Force** — clears accumulated karma | $12.77 |
| **03** Judgement Day | the ledger closes → *she still replays it* | **The Unburdening** — write what it cost her, burn that (never the name) | $12.77 |
| **04** The Turn | what to send → *what she does in the hours between* | **The Still Cup** — a nightly rite for the waiting hours | $12.77 |
| **05** Cut the Cord | this cord → *the older ones still tied* | **The Old Cords** — a self-audit of what else she's still attached to | $12.77 |

⚠ 03's bump must have her burn **what it cost her**, never the target's name. Burning a named
person is both a compliance problem and a different product from the one we're selling.

⚠ A fixed-price bump on a pay-what-you-want offer is coherent and should stay: the *working* is
priced by what she can give; the *instructional* is a defined product with a defined price. Say
so if it ever needs defending.

## 5. Component: the confirmation email

**Driven by:** the problem's emotional state → the wait-filler's register and length

Sent immediately on purchase. **It sells nothing.** Its job is to make the wait feel like service.

1. Subject: "Your [offer] Has Been Confirmed"
2. Thanks framed as *their good judgment* — "thank you for making the right decision and
   listening to my advice"
3. Work has already begun — "I'll get started immediately"
4. Restate the SLA
5. **The wait-filler** — 02 tells a parable about a donkey in a well. Unrelated to the offer,
   genuinely readable, explicitly framed as shareable: *"I hope you will share it with others
   too."*
6. Sign-off

**The wait-filler is the component most likely to be skipped and shouldn't be.** It converts dead
time into a gift, and asking them to share it is an engagement action — Lesson 1's ladder is
open → read → click → **reply** → buy. Each offer needs its own: a parable, a piece of craft
lore, an origin story. Never a discount.

**P5 — match the buyer's emotional register, and scale to the SLA.** 02's buyer is hopeful across
24h, so a donkey-in-a-well story lands. 03's is *angry* across three days, so its parable must do
quiet inoculation — *don't spend these nights watching for their downfall*. 04's is anxious and
likely to text him something rash tonight, so hers must argue against acting early. Three days
earns a parable; 24 hours may only earn a paragraph.

**P6 — open a loop before the wait.** The confirmation email is the last contact until delivery.
One line saying the product will answer something you can't answer yet turns delay into anticipation.

## 6. Component: the product (the delivered reading)

**Driven by:** mechanism → the unit structure and their count · promise → what the conclusion pays off

02's is `02:501-829`. Architecture:

```
  1  Cosmic preamble        the forces aligned; you chose at an auspicious moment
  2  The promise block      a WINDOW (next two months) × a DOMAIN (money + love)
  3  The condition          "requires your active participation"
  4  Spread framing         why this many units, why these
  5  INOCULATION #1         "may at times appear enigmatic or seemingly contradictory"
  6  12 × unit entries      see §6a
  7  The free gift          delivered inside the same email, as a second act
  8  Conclusion + INOCULATION #2 and #3
  9  Sign-off
```

### 6a. The unit entry — repeated 12 times

**Header:** `[cosmic agent] has guided your [choice|selection] to [NAME], [one-line character]`

Rotate the opener; 02 uses eight variants ("The celestial wisdom has guided…", "The cosmos has
guided your selection…", "Your Choice fell on…", "Your choice has led you to…"). Note 02's
variety *decays* toward the end — three consecutive entries close with "Here's what it
signifies." Don't inherit the fatigue.

⚠ **The agency device:** it is always "**YOUR** choice", "**your** selection" — though the buyer
chose nothing. Retroactively manufactured participation, softened by a cosmic guide so they get
agency and destiny at once. Keep this exactly.

**Body — 8 to 14 short paragraphs, in this arc:**

| | Move | Example (The Magician) |
|---|---|---|
| a | Verdict + condition | "heralds the dawn of a new life chapter… But to manifest this, it is crucial to adopt the right mindset" |
| b | The promise | "filled with gratification and joy" |
| c | The instruction | "seize the initiative, breaking free from the stagnant routines" |
| d | **The third-party threat** | "beware of flatterers and deceivers… some may seek to exploit your newfound abundance" |
| e | Close on compliance | "clarify your objectives and maintain focus… you shall accelerate your progress" |

⚠ **Move (d) appears in SIX of the twelve cards — and is absent from the final three.**
(Corrected 2026-08-03: Magician, Chariot, Justice, Strength, Temperance, Hierophant carry it;
Judgment, High Priestess and Death do not. The pre-sell decays exactly where recall is strongest —
fix that when building, and make the last entries *narrow* from plural strangers to one person.)
It is not decoration. "Be careful who you place
your trust in." "Resist the influence of individuals who may have a harmful impact." "Some may
try to profit from your positive financial situation." **The product is quietly pre-selling the
next offer** — by the time Judgement Day arrives asking who wronged them, the reading has told
them a dozen times that someone has. Build this into 03, 04 and 05's products too.

**`%FIRSTNAME%` cadence:** ~2 per unit, ~24 across the product, always placed **mid-sentence**.
Mid-sentence reads as intimacy; sentence-initial reads as mail-merge.

### 6b. Hard rules for the units

1. **Free units and paid units must not overlap.** 02's free emails use World, Lovers, Tower,
   Star, Emperor, Moon. The paid 12 are Magician, High Priestess, Hierophant, Chariot, Strength,
   Wheel of Fortune, Justice, Hanged Man, Death, Temperance, Devil, Judgment — **zero overlap**,
   which is what earns the phrase "12 **new** cards". (18 of 22 Major Arcana used; Fool, Empress,
   Hermit and Sun are spare.)
2. **The count needs a reason.** Twelve isn't arbitrary — it's "The Zodiac Spread", one per house.
   04 needs its own justification for however many symbols the paid reading adds.
3. **Every unit is conditionally positive.** "Favourable, *provided you*…" This is what makes the
   reading unfalsifiable: good outcome proves the reading, bad outcome proves non-compliance.

### 6c. The free gift

Not a bonus paragraph — a **second act with its own schedule**:

- Transition: "after thoroughly reading and analyzing your [reading], you can proceed to the
  second part"
- **Dated instructions**: week 1 do X · wait for the visualisation period to pass · then two
  familiar and two new · repeat the seven-day cycle
- A love window: "during the first two, and fourth weeks… among them is a hidden gem. Someone you
  know might reappear"

**Why it matters structurally:** it gives them something to *do* for weeks, which is retention —
and it opens a window during which the next offer lands. The gift is the bridge to the next sale.

### 6d. The three inoculations

Each shifts responsibility for failure onto the buyer. All three are required.

1. **Before the reading** — "may at times appear enigmatic or seemingly contradictory, but fear not"
2. **Timing** — "don't be surprised if your Reading's predictions take some time to manifest"
3. **Compliance** — "this depends on how quickly you absorb and, most importantly, *apply* the
   guidance you've received"

## 7. Component: the thank-you page

**Driven by:** promise → the one line about her, not about us

Shortest component. Five beats, no selling.

1. "Dearest," / name
2. Thanks framed as their good decision — "for making the right decision and following my advice"
3. Work begins now — "I'll get started immediately"
4. **Tell them exactly what to look for** — "it will be titled with your name and: Your Great
   Complete Tarot Reading" (a named subject line is a deliverability and open-rate device)
5. Sign-off

**P7 — the thank-you page's job changes by archetype.**

- **READING offer:** a receipt. Confirm, name the delivery subject line, state the SLA, stop.
- **ACT offer:** an *intake gate*. Push them straight back to their inbox to reply, and put that
  instruction **above** the delivery promise — it's the only thing on the page with a real
  deadline, because the work cannot start without it.

## 7a. Using the copy skill alongside this framework

`direct-response-copy` is useful for *mechanics* — Schwartz's awareness levels, Hopkins'
reason-why, Collier's "enter the conversation already in their head", the So What? chain. Not for
*voice*: its register is a smart friend explaining something; Evelyn is a practitioner writing to
someone who has been wronged.

**The governing rule: the specimen wins.** 02 is proven copy for this audience at this awareness
level. Where the skill and 02 disagree, follow 02 unless there's reason to think 02 was wrong.
Where the skill fills a gap 02 never faced — PWYW, ladder pricing — it's genuinely additive.

**The common failure is over-application.** Booking-page visitors are Schwartz **Level 5, Most
Aware**: they read the letter and clicked. Headlines, pain quantification, social proof and
disqualification are Level 2–3 machinery; on a confirmation step they're friction. The skill's own
Schwartz section says it — *most aware = short*.

## 8. Cross-cutting devices — the things that make all of it work

| Device | Where it lives | Why |

## 10. The product for 03, 04 and 05 — answered

02 is the only offer whose product exists. These are the replication briefs for the other three.

### 03 — "The Record of Judgement"

**Report *plus* forward ledger.** A pure record of past work is falsifiable — three days later
they hold a receipt and no result, and day four is a refund. 02 is unfalsifiable because it is
forward-looking and conditional; the hex report must be too.

**Reframed from justice-on-them to closure-for-you** — the ledger is closed, the debt moves off
the buyer. Same payoff, no claim about a real person.

⚠ **Updated 2026-08-04** for the mechanism rebuild ([00a §03](./00a-BRIEFS-BEs.md)). The three
offices survive; the spirits do not. Beat 4's inoculation had to move off *"the spirits do not
answer as you expect"* onto something the new mechanism can carry.

```
  1  It is done                     first line answers the only question they have
  2  The Entry read back            their intake, itemised as she gave it — highest-satisfaction beat
  3  The Three Nights               dated; what was on the table; concrete nouns only
  4  Inoculation #1                 a closed account does not feel like a win. It feels like nothing
  5  Three Verdicts                 the Entry · the Transfer · the Closing
                                    → three motions give the count a reason (§6b rule 2)
  6  The Ledger of Signs            days 1-9 private · 10-30 visible · by the third moon
  7  Your part — the Sealing        three duties, chief among them silence
  8  What the Closing did not reach → pre-sells the U1 protection ward
  9  Free gift: Nine Nights of Water
 10  Inoculations #2 and #3
 11  Sign-off + "do not write to ask if it worked; the ledger will tell you"
```

⚠ **Beat 8 was *The Returning Current*** — struck energy returns on the striker. That premise
required a strike. Nothing is struck now, so the U1 hand-off is re-derived: closing an account
settles what was owed and does not repair what it cost her to carry, which is the same shape and
survives the reframe. **Do not reinstate the backlash framing** — it re-imports the "you attacked
someone" reading the whole reframe exists to remove.

⚠ **Replace the three testimonials, don't gender-swap them.** *"Her deceitful ways were EXPOSED…
she was exiled in shame"* promises reputational harm to a real person. Rewrite as *buyer-change*
precedents: what shifted for Marta, never what happened to her rival.

### 04 — "The Turn"

**The paid product adds POSITION, not symbols.** Tasseography is read by zones: the handle is the
querent, near the handle means soon, opposite means a stranger, rim is days, base is what's
buried. **Position is timing.**

The free letter read what the leaves *were*. Evelyn *"had not yet turned the cup."* Turning it
converts the **same seven symbols** into a dated 21-day sequence, in which each leaf's physical
shape dictates the *form* of what she says that day. Every instruction is derived from a leaf
property, never prescribed as advice — which is how it stays a tea reading rather than dating
advice.

- **Self-diagnosis replaces personalisation.** Three named cup patterns — *The Turned Handle*
  (he replies, but flat) · *The Divided Rim* (hot and cold) · *The Sunken Leaf* (silence). She
  picks. 04 gets no reply, so this buys precision with zero data.
- **Relative days, never calendar dates** — "the first evening after you read this."
- ⚠ **Banned vocabulary:** the free letter's own closing advice (*"foster open communication,
  seek guidance, embrace growth"*). Restating it is the fastest refund in the deck.

### 05 — the cord report *(**Cut the Cord**)*

✅ **Named _Cut the Cord_** (2026-08-03). "Hex Her" was indefensible as a product name, and its
promise — *"Her feelings towards the man you choose will change. And she will NOT pursue him
anymore"* — is a stated outcome about a named, non-consenting person and must never ship.

**The object is the cord, not the woman.** Cut at the buyer's end, sold on what it frees in
*her*. The rival appears only as an unnamed third presence. Governing test for every line:
**write it as if she will read it** — buyers forward things.

```
  1  Personal top-block         the only bespoke part, from their reply
  2  The hour it began          receipt of intake; work started, not scheduled
  3  What I did not do          the ethical frame delivered as craft superiority, early
  4  The naming and the release the name anchored the thread, then was released
  5  The Four Watches           Dusk · Midnight · Third Watch · Dawn — the proof-of-work
  6  What was severed           where it attached (his attention, not his heart)
  7  What returns to you        affirmative payoff
  8  The Nine Nights            dated sealing practice
  9  What to expect + inoculations
 10  The residual               one thing the sever couldn't reach → the next offer
```

**The best refund-prevention line in the deck** — the flare inoculation:

> In the first two or three days you may see her push harder, not softer. Do not read it as
> failure. A thread that has been cut still swings once before it falls.

### Rules that apply to all three products

1. **≥3 buyer-specific callbacks, exactly one verbatim.** A product with zero facts about the
   buyer is this category's documented failure mode — *"could have applied to anyone"*,
   *"the tone ChatGPT might use"* are the most-quoted complaints, and it is also the construction
   prosecuted in the Maria Duval case. This is a lookup + merge, **not** the n8n generator.
2. **No calendar deadlines.** A date turns an unfalsifiable service into a falsifiable claim with
   a refund date attached. Behaviour triggers only.
3. **Report a difficulty that was overcome.** Clean successes read as script.
4. **Name your own limit at the end** — that limit is the next offer.
