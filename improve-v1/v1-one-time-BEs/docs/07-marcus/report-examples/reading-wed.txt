[2 · The step — the one thing · Page of Cups]

A young man in a blue tunic printed with pink flowers. He holds a gold cup up at chest height. A fish has put its head out over the rim and is looking straight at him. Behind him the sea is moving in short waves.

He's the one carrying the cup. Fourteen years and Pages still make me laugh, because he's not smooth about it. A fish is staring up out of his own drink and he goes ahead anyway.

So the step is small and it is yours. One sentence, today, with both of your nos inside it. Close to: I said I was busy and I wasn't, so ask me again, or I'll ask you.

Some women tell me they would sooner write it than say it, and that still counts. What the card doesn't hold is the version where you wait to be asked again. The cup is up and moving before anybody has agreed to anything.

[3 · What's in the way · Eight of Swords]

A woman standing in shallow water. A red cloth is wound round her from shoulder to ankle and a second is tied over her eyes. Eight swords stand upright in the mud, four one side, four the other. Behind her, up a slope, a castle.

Count the swords between her and the edge of the card. None. Every one is behind her shoulder line and she is barefoot on open mud.

The thing in your way is a matter of size. From behind that cloth, one drink after work measures the same as nine years did. There's no way to check the difference without moving.

The rope is worth a look too. It's wound round her arms and knotted to nothing, so she could walk. She hasn't, and the card gives me no count of the years.

Which makes speed the trick today. Say it before you have measured it, because the measuring is the broken part.

[4 · What it asks of you · Strength]

A woman in a white dress, a chain of flowers at her waist and more in her hair. Both her hands are on a lion, one under its jaw and one on its brow. Above her head, a small figure of eight lying on its side.

Look at where her hands are. On the mouth. No cage in the picture, no rope, and her face is not straining.

You have to say the true half out loud, that you weren't busy. Some women tell me they would rather do almost anything than take back a no to the face that got it. Those are the most expensive ten seconds in the instruction.

She is not driving the lion off. Her hands stay on while it's still a lion. The fear comes into the corridor with you at full size, and the saying takes less time than the deciding has.

[5 · What changes if you take it · Ace of Cups]

[2.1 · The thing as it is · Two of Wands]

A man stands on top of a stone wall in a heavy coat. He holds a small globe in his right hand and looks out over a bay and a grey coastline. One staff is in his left hand. The other is strapped to the wall with an iron bracket.

He's not in a bad way up there. Good coat, the whole coast in view, the world small enough to sit in one palm.

[2.2 · What's holding it there · The Devil]

A horned figure with bat's wings crouched on a black half-block. Below it a man and a woman stand chained by the neck to a ring at its base. Each has small horns now, and a tail. The chains hang loose.
