# Etsy, read in a real browser — the written-reading market

⛔ **Why this file exists.** `04-marketplace.md` collected only per-minute phone and live-chat
platforms, because Etsy and Fiverr return **403 to server-side fetches**. The operator caught it:
*"what u surfaced is psychic per min call questions. what questions do people put into written
readings? those on etsy kinda?"* Etsy loads perfectly in a normal browser. This was read that way
on 2026-09-05, from `etsy.com/search` and shop review pages.

⚠ **Etsy reviews are near-useless as VOC.** Read across a bestselling shop, they run *"Great
reading"* / *"Thank you helen for accurate reading"* / *"Fast response.Excellent."* Buyers of a
written reading do not narrate what they asked, which is the opposite of the per-minute platforms
where they do. ⭐ **The value on Etsy is in the LISTING TITLES**, which sellers keyword-stuff to
match what buyers type into the search box. They are the closest available proxy for her question.

⭐ **"Situationship" and "no contact" are native market vocabulary.** So is **"POI"**
(`04-marketplace.md`). The undefined relationship is the mass-market case, not the boyfriend.

## 2 · ⭐ The unit of sale is THE NUMBER OF QUESTIONS

Etsy's own left-hand search filters include **"1 Question"** and **"5 Question"** as facets, and
titles sell *1 Question · 3 Question · 5 Question · Unlimited Question*.

⭐ **This is independent market validation of `07-C5`** — the ladder the operator locked, where
$35/$57/$87 buys one, two or three of her questions. The market already prices this way and the
buyer already understands the unit. It is not an idea we invented and have to teach.

## 3 · 🔴 Two things 07 is on the wrong side of

### "No AI" is the market's loudest claim
*No AI · Real Person No AI · Real Reader · no AI* appear in title after title, from multiple shops.
The one-star review found in a bestselling shop is the whole risk in her words:

> *"Not a fan of this type of reading, it Felt AI generated Even the voice note was animated through Ai not my cup of tea,"*

⛔ 07's reading is machine-assisted and says so in its disclosure. **The market is actively
competing on the opposite claim.** `04-marketplace.md` independently found her tell for AI is
*"too smooth, too bold"* — which is an argument for the roughness in Marcus's voice, not against it.

### Speed is the delivery promise, and it is brutal
*Same Hour* and *Same Day* dominate. 07 promises 24 hours and sells a $12.77 bump to go faster.
⚠ On this market that bump is table stakes sold as an upgrade.

## 4 · Price — 07 sits above the market

Observed on one search page (SGD, ~1.28 to the USD): **SGD 0.63 – 110**, clustered **5–30**
(≈ **USD 4–23**). Heavy discounting is normal — 50%, 60%, 75% off is the default presentation.

⛔ **07's $35 entry is at or above the top of this range, and its $87 rung is far beyond it.**
That is not fatal — this is the commodity end and 07 is not competing here — but any claim that
$35 is cheap for a written reading is false, and the booking page must not make it.

## 5 · The positioning that recurs

**"No sugar coating" / "Honest Answer Not The One You Want."** Multiple shops. Marcus's register
is already this. ⭐ But `04-marketplace.md`'s finding is the sharper one: buyers praise the
*pairing* — *"Bluntly honest answers. She was empathetic but genuine."* Marcus currently buys the
blunt half only, which is exactly the operator's complaint about the emails.

## 6 · Limits

- One search page and one shop's four review pages. Not a survey.
- Etsy Singapore, so prices are SGD and the result mix may be localised.
- ⛔ Buyer **questions** are not recoverable from Etsy at all — the reviews do not contain them.
  Listing titles are a proxy for the search string, not a transcript of what she typed in the box.
  The only real source for that is a seller's intake form, which is not public.

# Part 2 · What actually sells — read from `q=tarot+reading`, most relevant, 2026-09-05

~60 listings on one page, badged *Bestseller* or *Popular now* where Etsy says so.

## 7 · 🔴 THE FINDING: nobody sells a named spread

Across the whole page, the number of listings selling a **named spread** is effectively zero. Card
structure appears only three times, and always as a spec line rather than the product:
*"3 Card Spread"* · *"4 Card Personalized PDF"* · *"Past Present Future"*.

⛔ **07's entire mechanism is seven named spreads** — The Weight, The Two Doors, The Undertow. On
this evidence the market has no appetite for a spread as a *thing to buy*. It buys a **topic**, a
**question count**, or a **speed**.

⭐ This does not kill the mechanism — 07's spreads are the free daily's content, not the thing being
sold, and a named spread is a real differentiator nobody else has. But it does mean **the booking
page must not sell "The Two Doors."** It sells *an answer to your question*, and the spread is how
the answer gets made. The distinction is the whole difference between a product and a process.

**Sold by format** — and this is the sharper taxonomy:

2. ⭐ **Blind reading — "No Questions Asked."** A major category in its own right, repeatedly

   Know Right Now"* · *"Blind Tarot Reading"* · *"No Questions Reading"*.
3. **Speed.** *Same Hour* is the default promise.
4. **Brutality.** *"Extra Detailed Brutal Tarot Reading, No Sugar Coating, Shocking Truth"* —
   bestseller AND popular-now on the same shop.
5. **No AI · No Tools · Real Person · Real Reader.**

## 9 · ⭐ The blind reading, and what it tells us about 07

*"No questions asked"* is one of the largest categories on the page. A whole segment does **not
want to have to articulate the question** — either because she cannot, or because naming it is the
part that costs her something.

⭐ **07 already sells both halves and has never noticed.** The free daily email IS a blind
reading — Marcus cuts before light, before he knows who opens it. The paid product is the
question-specific one. That is precisely the market's two categories, in sequence, with the free
one qualifying her for the paid one.

⚠ The risk in the same fact: a woman who buys blind readings is buying *not having to say it*. The
booking page asks her to type the hardest sentence in her week into a box. That is a real
conversion cost and no page copy currently acknowledges it.

## 10 · 🔴 The market sells exactly what Marcus refuses

*"Exact Thoughts"* · *"True Feelings & Telepathy"* · *"What They Wish They Could Say"* ·
*"Hidden Intentions"* · *"How Do They Really Feel About Me"* — mind-reading of a third party is a
top-selling category.

⛔ 07's hardest rule is **Marcus reads the cards, not the man.** So the single most saleable love
product on this market is the one thing he will not do.

Two ways to read that, and the operator should pick one deliberately:
- **A weakness** — refusing the biggest category costs volume.
- ⭐ **The differentiator** — every one of those listings is making a claim it cannot keep, and
  `04-marketplace.md` shows the resulting complaints (*the date moved*, *it fit anyone*). Marcus
  refusing it **out loud**, in the letter, is a trust move no competitor on this page can copy, and
  it is already in his voice.

## 11 · What this changes

| | |
|---|---|
| The booking page | ⛔ Stop selling the spread's name. Sell the answer; the spread is the method |
| The daily email | ⭐ Name the free letter as what the market calls a **blind reading** — it already is one |
| The paid product | Keep the question-count ladder. It is the market's own unit (§2) |
| Positioning | Decide deliberately on §10 — refuse the mind-reading category loudly, or lose it quietly |
