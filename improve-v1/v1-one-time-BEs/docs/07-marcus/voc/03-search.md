# VOC 03 — Search behaviour and public Q&A

**Territory:** how she phrases it when nobody is watching. Search boxes, public Q&A question
titles (Quora, JustAnswer psychic/relationship categories), and published keyword commentary.

**Why these strings:** a Quora or JustAnswer question *title* is not a forum post. It is the
sentence she typed into a box before she knew anyone would read it. No audience, no editing,
no apostrophes. That is the closest published artefact to her typing alone at 1am.

**Collected:** 2026-09-05, via web search. All strings below are **verbatim** — typos, missing
apostrophes, run-ons and stray punctuation preserved exactly. Names replaced with `[his name]`.

⛔ **Not measured:** nothing here is a proven search-volume figure. See §6 for what is *claimed*
by third parties and why it is not evidence.

## 2. Subject-shape analysis — does she ask about him, or about herself?

**Short answer: she asks about HIM, and she asks it as a WHY. She only becomes the subject when
she has given up on getting an answer about him.**

Four grammatical shapes turned up, and they are not interchangeable. They map to four different
emotional states, and the emails should be written to a specific one, not to "love" in general.

### Shape A — "he" as subject, verb of withholding. **Dominant.**

He is the actor. She is the object the action is being done to (or withheld from). The verb is
almost always negative-continuous: *doesn't*, *won't*, *hasn't*, *never*, *keeps*.

- `Why does he text me to check in, but doesn't ask me out?`
- `Why does he call and text every day but doesn't make plans to meet up?`
- `Why does he text everyday but rarely wants to spend time with me?`
- `Why does my boyfriend keep disappearing for months and then coming back?`
- `Why does he disappear for a week or 2, and then comes back and tries to talk to me again acting like nothing happened?`
- `Why has my ex moved on so fast after years of being together with me?`
- `Why hasn't my boyfriend introduced me to his parents?`
- `My boyfriend hasn't texted me in like two days. Should I text him first? I am always the one who texts first`
- `My boyfriend hasn't texted me in two days, but he's been watching my stories and he's been active on social, what could this possibly mean ?`
- `What does it mean if a married man says he will leave his wife for you but never actually does?`
- `Why would a guy block me? He told me he misses me then I messaged miss him too then when I woke up in the morning I saw he's blocked me.`

⭐ **The grammar of Shape A is the whole insight.** She is not asking *what he feels*. She is
asking *why the two halves of his behaviour don't match*. Nearly every one of these is a
**"X but not Y"** sentence. He texts BUT doesn't ask her out. He says he loves her BUT hasn't
told his parents. He comes back BUT disappears again. She has already collected the evidence.
She wants the contradiction explained.

**For Marcus:** the reading should name the contradiction, not the man. "He is consistent — just
not about you" is a Shape A answer. "He loves you deep down" is not; it answers a question she
did not ask.

### Shape B — "we / us" as subject. **Rare, and only after a breakup.**

- `Will we ever get back together and when?`
- `My ex and I broke up 6 months ago and tarot says it's the end of our relationship. Is there any way to salvage it ?`
- `Do you have psychics on here? Will I get back together with my ex boyfriend?!`

"We" is almost never used about a live relationship. It appears once the relationship is
already gone and she is asking about a reunion. **"Us" is past tense in this data.**

### Shape C — "I" as subject, but he is still the topic. **The second-largest group.**

She has turned the question on herself *only because* she cannot get an answer about him. Note
that these are all self-blame framings.

- `Why do I seem to attract emotionally unavailable men?`
- `Why am I attracted to toxic and emotionally unavailable men even though I have a good father?`
- `It's been 3 years and I still think about my ex 24/7. Why?`
- `Is it normal to still be thinking of my ex after 3 years?`
- `My boyfriend of almost 5 years and I just broke up. He has already moved on within a week. How did he move on so fast, and why am I struggling?`

⭐ Look at the last one. **Both halves in one sentence** — his speed and her struggle, joined by
"and". That is the exact hinge the emails should live on.

### Shape D — "I" as subject, he is absent entirely. **The loneliness and purpose bucket.**

- `I'm 40 years old and I'm single and I might be single forever. Will I find that person for me?`
- `Will I find love at 45?`
- `I'm scared that no one is ever going to stay with me. Is this normal?`
- `Why do I feel stuck repeating the same routine everyday while everyone around me seems to be moving ahead in life?`
- `My life is stuck in a cycle that repeats every year. How do I escape?`
- `I feel like I do the same things every day. I'm stuck in a boring routine. How do I change this?`
- `When will I be permanently be financially stable?`

**Notice:** even here the comparison is social — *everyone around me seems to be moving ahead*,
*I feel like I've been left behind*, *I feel like no one sees me*. Loneliness in this data is
never solitude. It is **being the one left behind while others move**.

### The shape ranking, for copy purposes

| Rank | Shape | Write it as |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A — "he does X but not Y"** | The email names the contradiction she has already noticed |
| 2 | **C — "why do I always…"** | The email removes the self-blame before answering |
| 3 | **D — "will I ever…"** | The email answers the *left behind* feeling, not the singleness |
| 4 | **B — "will we…"** | Only for ex/reunion sends; do not use for live relationships |

## 4. Asked *of tarot* vs asked as a general search

Kept separate deliberately. They are not the same person-moment and should not feed the same
email beat.

**Three shapes only, in the paid-question set:**

| Shape | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **WHEN** | `When will he propose?` `When am i going to find my true love` | Timing is what she pays for. Almost never asked as a plain search. |

⭐ **The single biggest difference from a general search:** when she pays, the question gets
*shorter* and asks for a **date**. The story falls away. `When will he propose?` is the same
woman as #3 in the fifteen — the two-year, no-family-introduction one — but she has stripped
out the evidence because she thinks the reader does not need it.

⛔ **Copy implication:** do not write the email as though she needs to supply the story. Write it
as though Marcus already has it, and ask her only for the one detail he could not know.

- `How do I know what a psychic medium tells you to be true? I have been talking to one over the last couple of years, and the outcome stays the same with no change`
- `Two readers have informed me that he's not my Mr. Right & I don't know whether I should trust because I live with him a lot. I'm in pain.`

These two are the **fatigue** signal for a recurring daily programme. She has been reading for
years and the outcome does not move. Any daily send has to be different from the last one or it
joins that pile.

## 5. What this means for the Marcus daily emails

Six rules the strings support directly.

1. **Open on a contradiction, not a feeling.** Shape A is "he does X but not Y". The most human
   line in the haul is `He texts me every day and says that he likes me, but still doesn't ask
   me out.` It is a fact-pair, not an emotion. The email earns her by naming the pair.

2. **Put a number in it.** `3 years`, `8 months`, `two days`, `3:06 am`. She dates everything.
   A generic "for a long time" is the sound of a stranger.

3. **Answer `Am I overthinking?` before anything else.** It is the permission underneath most of
   these questions and it appears verbatim.

4. **Never open the door for a full story.** In the paid set she strips the story out. Marcus
   should present the pattern and ask for the one detail he cannot know — not for a recap.

5. **She is auditing you.** `Multiple psychics have told me my ex is coming back. Am I supposed
   to believe this?` and `the outcome stays the same with no change`. Fatigue is real and
   published. A recurring daily send has to move, not repeat.

6. **Loneliness is being left behind, not being alone.** `everyone around me seems to be moving
   ahead in life`, `I feel like I've been left behind`, `I feel like no one sees me`. Write the
   comparison, not the solitude.

## 7. ⚠ SENSITIVE — flagged content

Handle deliberately. Do not lift these into copy without a decision.

### ⚠ Bereavement / contacting the dead
Present and searched heavily. Consistent with the known bereavement cross-cut in the ad work.

- `As a recent widow, how do I know if my husband's spirit is near me (e.g., what signs should I look for)?`
- `What signs have you received from your deceased husband?`
- `When my husband died I felt his spirit around me for the first 2 to 3 days and then after that I havent felt his presants dose anyone know why?`
- `I was talking to a psychic and was told I would see someone again in time. But how could that be, that certain someone has been passed for two years?`
- `Why do certain spirits not come through during a psychic medium session? I went hoping my dad, who passed 9 months ago, would come through today but he didn't. Kind of disappointed.`

**Note:** this is a *different product* from a daily tarot spread. Marcus is not a medium. Flagged
so the daily deck does not drift into it by accident.

### ⚠ Money desperation
Real and specific. Ad-policy exposure on financial-status targeting is a known constraint.

- `What can I do to overcome the debt I am already drowning into? It's a bank loan and I am four months late.`
- `What should someone do if they're drowning in financial debt and have more money going out than coming in?`
- `What are some legal ways to become financially stable and get out of debt without resorting to stealing or scamming others?`
- `When will I be permanently be financially stable?`
- `Why am I drowning in debt?`

⛔ `four months late` on a bank loan is a hardship disclosure. Do not build an email beat on it.

### ⚠ Self-harm adjacency / severe distress
Present at the edge of the loneliness bucket. **Do not use in copy at all.** Recorded only so the
boundary of the loneliness territory is visible.

- `At what point is it acceptable to quit your job when you feel it impacting your mental health? I dread going in and often wish harm befalls me so I don't need to attend.`
- `What do you do when you feel like no one would care if you were gone tomorrow? I'm scared I will have this weird view of the world forever and that I will always feel alone even if I'm surrounded by people.`
- `I'm 40. All my life I've tried, but literally nobody likes me. I'm actually a really good person. Should I just completely give up on others now?`

**Line to hold:** `I feel like I've been left behind` is usable. `no one would care if you were
gone tomorrow` is not. The daily emails work the first and must never touch the second.

### ⚠ Reading dependency
The programme is recurring, so this is a live risk and worth naming.

- `I feel as if I met my twin flame but I am in separation now. I am getting addicted to tarot readings and psychic readings. how can I stop this addiction?`
- `Is watching tarot card readings a form of 'chasing' in twin flame connections?`
- `In separation from twin flame, I have been seeing a lot of angel numbers and every tarot reader I see says we are coming into reunion. Psychics tell me the same thing.`

### ⚠ Health
Not a theme in this haul. The 3am-waking cluster is spiritual-framed but has an obvious medical
reading (sleep disruption); every advice source in the results pivoted to it. Keep the daily
emails on the *meaning* framing and out of any sleep-health claim.

## 8. Method and limits

- **Collected** 2026-09-05 via web search. Strings are search-result question *titles* from
  Quora, JustAnswer (general / relationship / psychic categories), plus the recurring-question
  claims on practitioner blogs.
- **Verbatim rule held.** Nothing was tidied. `the feelings doesn't go away`, `when/how will i me
  my soulmate`, `dose anyone know why`, `his presants`, `I'm scared` — all as found.
- **Names removed.** No name appeared in the harvested strings other than a single already-public
  placeholder pair on a JustAnswer title; that string was not used.
- **What this haul does not contain:** real Google autocomplete or People-Also-Ask boxes. Those
  cannot be pulled through search-result text. The Q&A titles are the substitute and they are
  arguably better — a PAA box is Google's paraphrase, a Quora title is her sentence.
- **Skew to be aware of:** Quora titles are lightly self-edited (she knows *someone* will answer,
  even if not who). JustAnswer titles are the least edited in the set, because she is paying and
  in a hurry — which is why §4a reads so much blunter than §4b. That difference is a finding,
  not noise.
