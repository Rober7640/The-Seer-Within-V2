## The chain

**What we found.** 09 mined *"what did she type?"*, which returns **situations**, and ranked them by
revenue per 1,000 **inside a theme** — conditional on her already having that concern. That is the
right metric for an ad, where you buy the audience. It is the wrong one for one daily letter to one
undifferentiated 76,000-person list, where **reach multiplies everything before the index touches
it.** Re-scored as reach × index, all thirty land under 3% of the list. *"The marriage ended years
ago"* is 0.25% — about 190 women, and 75,810 wasted sends.

⭐ **And the design rules already knew.** `07-spread-design-rules.md` §3 tests every free position
with: *"Marcus could write this passage honestly in a mass email to 76,000 women who have told him
nothing."* That test was applied to the **card** and never to the **topic**. The letters passed it
and the roster failed it.

**What it means.** The unit is not a situation she matches, it is a **frame she enters** — a door
broad enough that most of the list can walk through it on any morning, and specific enough that
walking through it produces a finding. ⭐ This is what the product already is: under 07-C5 the
letter is broad and **her question makes it specific.** The daily never had to name her situation.
It had to open a door.

⚠ **Broad is not the opposite of love.** The list is love-heavy because Meta delivered love. A love
*frame* — the ask, the silence, the thing she keeps seeing — reaches it. A love *situation* — two
men, the man from 35 years ago, the child who stopped calling — does not. What died is the
situation, not the subject.

⛔ **And the biggest arm in the database is the proof.** *"Is there someone else"* has the largest
reach we can measure (n=1,759, 2.71%) and earns **below baseline** (idx 0.96) — plus it carries the
one real harm case in the bank. Reach alone is not the answer either. Reach × index is, and every
frame below is picked on both.

**What we do.** Ten frames, each run three times in thirty days, one spread key each.
Seven of the ten spreads already exist and are untouched. Three of batch 1's six letters survive.
**Two of the six die.** Phase 2 shrinks from *design thirty spreads* to *design one and re-point two*.

## ⛔ The fourth gate

The plan's three gates were: **1** she already has a word for it · **2** a spread can answer it
without guessing at a stranger's future decisions · **3** it survives being asked again next month.

> ### **4 · Could most of the list find themselves in this, on any given morning, without having to match a life-situation?**

Applied to the old thirty it fails **all but nine of them.** It is the only gate that would have
caught the roster before six letters were written.

**Two traps it must not walk into:**

| Trap | The rule |
|---|---|
| ⛔ Ranking by database reach | Our pull is a love funnel. *"What is blocking my money"* measures **0.24%** in it and is plainly broad — 09 itself says money's fall is *"Meta moving delivery off money, not demand leaving."* **Reach comes from Etsy rank and plain universality. Our index says only how well a frame converts once she is inside it.** No DB share appears as a ranking column below, on purpose |
| ⛔ Broad becoming vague | *"Love"* is broad and worthless. *"What is blocking your money"* is broad **and** names a thing, promises a finding, and takes anyone. **The test is the answer-sentence:** if you cannot write the sentence a happy buyer repeats to a friend, it is vague, not broad. Every frame below carries one. Two candidates were cut for failing exactly this |

## ⭐ How many, how often — the recommendation

> ## **Ten frames. Three sends each. One spread key per frame. Thirty days.**

**Yes, frames repeat, and the repeat is the point.** Three reasons, in order of weight.

**1 · Thirty one-offs are not a test.** Phase 0.5 ranks by revenue per 1,000 sends per spread. With
one send per spread every arm is a single draw. 09's own concentration check killed candidates
where one buyer held 22% of a theme's revenue — at n=1 send, a single $87 order with both upsells
can be most of an arm. Three sends triples the exposure per arm and, more importantly, buys the
thing a one-off can never buy: ⭐ **a consistency check.** A frame that earns on all three mornings
is a frame. A frame that earns on one is a letter, or a card.

**2 · The readout was already built for it.** `scripts/07-readout.mjs:169` — *"Roll up per spread.
A spread may run on more than one date; sends add up across them."* **Zero code changes.** Ten
frames × three dates aggregate on their own.

**3 · Re-asking is her own behaviour, not our compromise.** `01-forums`: *"She never frames
re-asking as a failure of the cards. She frames it as the cards insisting."* `02-readers`: the same
client, the same question, three times in five days — *"Uh, he texted me."* A programme that comes
back to the same door matches what she already does. And it is the product's own claim: **the same
spread, cut again on a different morning, gives a different reading.** Running each frame once
quietly concedes that it wouldn't.

⛔ **The condition on all of that.** `03-search` §4a is the fatigue signal — *"the outcome stays the
same with no change."* **A repeat must change the finding, not the wording.** Rule: each of a
frame's three sends opens on a different free-card angle and ends on a different act. Frame 3
(money) is the one most at risk and needs its three cuts named before it is written.

### Why not 7 × 4, and why not 30 × 1

| Shape | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **30 × 1** | ⛔ Unmeasurable, and it contradicts the product's own promise |
| **7 × 4** | ⛔ Tempting — every frame locks to a weekday, *"Friday is the money one"*. But then **frame is perfectly confounded with weekday**, and weekday moves opens and orders on its own. You could never tell whether money earns or Friday earns |
| ⭐ **10 × 3** | 10 and 7 are coprime, so **every frame lands on three different weekdays and three different opening shapes.** The frame is measured clean of the shape, and the shape is measured across ten frames. Every frame is seen once by day 10, three times by day 30 |

**The cost of 10 × 3, stated:** twenty fewer topics tested this cycle. That is the trade, and it is
worth it — thirty tested topics that cannot be told apart is zero tested topics. Cycle 2 promotes
from the bench.

## Method, briefly

- **Nothing was queried.** No DB read this pass. Every index, n, top1 and trend above is lifted
  verbatim from `09-thirty-named-things.md`, which did the read-only pull with the 25006 canary.
- **Reach was judged from Etsy rank, Etsy market pages and plain universality** — never from our own
  share of rows, per the stated trap. The 09 shares were computed to confirm they are uninformative
  (money 0.24%, the thing you keep seeing 0.32%, everything-goes-to-others 0.31%) and are then not
  used to rank anything.
- **Etsy was checked live 2026-09-06 via search snippets**, which is weaker than 08's browser
  session — it returns listing titles and market-page existence, not badges. New listings are quoted
  as titles only, and the three that carry the ranking (#1 blind, #2 window, #4 brutal, #10 money)
  all come from 08's badged read, not from this pass.
- **The survivor list came from reading all 30 spreads' positions**, not from the registry metadata.
  ⭐ **Twelve of the thirty have a free card that names no situation** — they were already
  frame-shaped, and that single column decided almost everything. Seven of the twelve are the
  untouched survivors; five went to the bench because a *better* frame already covered their
  territory (`is-it-too-late`, `can-i-trust-anyone-again`, `how-long-can-this-go-on`,
  `the-money-somebody-else-is-holding`) or because the index was below baseline. Two more
  (`hes-gone-quiet`, `should-i-stay-or-go`) reach gate 4 with a pronoun change.
- ⛔ **Nothing was edited.** `scripts/07-spreads.json`, every letter and `07-30-DAY-PLAN.md` are
  untouched. This file proposes.
