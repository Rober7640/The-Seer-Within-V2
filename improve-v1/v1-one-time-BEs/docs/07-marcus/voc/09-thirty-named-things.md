## The chain

**What we found.** Etsy is a read of **supply** — and the supply is heaviest exactly where our own
buyers pay the **least**. Ranked against 64,931 real `concern` rows weighted by money, the themes
about **her position inside the thing** — *am I asking too much · the marriage that ended years ago ·
the man who came back after 35 years · is he who he says he is* — earn **1.4× to 1.9× the baseline**.
The themes about **his future** — *will he come back (1.05×) · is he the one (0.89×) · who is coming
towards you (0.77×)* — earn **at or below it**, and those four are most of the Etsy page.

**What it means.** Follow the Etsy ranking for **what to name**, because it is proof the words are
shoppable. Do not follow it for **what to expect** — the crowded categories are crowded, not rich.
Two thirds of the thirty came off our own buyers and are things nobody on Etsy sells.

⭐ **The single best find:** *"I asked her if I was calling too much because I didn't want to be a
burden."* One reviewer's line in `04-marketplace.md`. Our own buyers say it 468 times and it earns
**$84.17 per 1,000** against a **$53.28** baseline, on 59 separate buyers with no single buyer above
4% of the money. Nobody on Etsy sells it.

⚠ **And the finding that costs us something.** *"She doubts herself as the reader"* is the strongest
conclusion in `01-forums.md`. Measured, it is the **worst-earning theme in the entire pull** —
$19.41 per 1,000, index **0.36**. It is the frame Marcus writes inside. It is not a product.

**What we do.** Build the thirty below in rank order, in batches of six, and read the money at five
sends. The three gates are stated per row; every row states which one it barely cleared.

## Method, briefly

- **DB read is READ-ONLY and proved it.** `BEGIN TRANSACTION READ ONLY`, then a no-op UPDATE
  canary that must be rejected with **25006** or the script hard-exits — the pattern from
  `scripts/bump-take-rate.mjs:15-46`. Canary printed `rejected (25006)` on every run. Scripts live in
  the session scratchpad, not the repo. Nothing was written.
- **Themes were found by reading, not counting.** 350 concerns pulled and read in full: 130 from
  **paying buyers only**, 130 random, 90 long-form. The regex arms exist only to put a number on a
  theme the reading had already found.
- **Paid signal** = `main_paid_at IS NOT NULL OR (purchased AND upsell_offered)`. The bare
  `purchased` flag is written before payment and overstates by 2.5×.
- **Four dirty arms were caught and fixed**, which changed the ranking: bare `used` was matching
  *"conf**used**"* (inflated #12 by 10×); bare `blocked`/`stuck` was conflating a money block, being
  blocked on WhatsApp, and *"he's stuck overseas"*; `child-gone` and `love-block` collapsed once
  tightened. **Five candidates failed the concentration check** and were demoted or cut — see the
  rejected list.
- **Ad-independence.** Raw counts reflect Meta's past delivery, so every row carries its share in the
  first half of the window against the second. A theme that holds or rises across eight months of
  changing ad mix is a theme; one that halves alongside its whole bucket (#8, #14) is a delivery
  artefact and is noted as such rather than demoted for it.
- **Not verified:** Etsy sales figures do not exist on a search page, so its ranking is supply
  weighted by badge, never demand. The `03-search.md` volume claims remain unverified and none was
  used. Every quote above is copied from the bank or from a DB row read in this pass.
