# Etsy, consolidated and ranked — what the market actually sells

**Read 2026-09-05 in a real browser** (Etsy 403s server-side fetches). Three result pages:
`tarot reading` · `personalized written tarot reading email pdf` · `lucky numbers psychic reading`.
≈130 listings.

⚠ **What the ranking is, and is not.** Etsy shows no sales figures on a search page. The signals
available are its own **Bestseller** and **Popular now** badges, and the fact that *most relevant*
ordering is partly sales-driven. So this ranks by **how many listings sell the thing, weighted by
how many carry a badge.** ⛔ It is a read of supply, not a measure of demand. Sellers cluster where
they believe money is — usually right, sometimes a herd.

## What the ranking says

**1 · The two biggest sellers are both things Marcus already does and never claims.**
*Blind* is the top category by badge density, and the free daily **is** a blind reading — he cuts
before light, before he knows who opens it. *What's coming in a named window* is second, and a
spread laid this morning is exactly that. ⭐ Neither needs a new product. Both need a name.

**2 · The third-biggest category is the one he refuses**, and it is the whole love market:
his thoughts, his feelings, what he wishes he could say. ⛔ Marcus reads the cards, not the man. The
compliant version is *what is being **done*** — the cup still held out, the feet that have not
moved. That conversion is the offer's hardest and most valuable move.

**3 · "Brutal, no sugar coating" is the highest badge density on the board** — five listings, five
badged. Marcus's register is already this, and it is the one thing he does not have to change.
⚠ But `04-marketplace.md` found buyers praise the **pairing**: *"Bluntly honest answers. She was
empathetic but genuine."* He currently buys the blunt half only.

**4 · Nobody sells a spread.** Card structure appears three times in ~130 listings, never as the
product. ⛔ A named spread is not a thing anyone shops for.

**5 · The market's nouns are things in HER LIFE, not things in the painting.**
*A block on your money. A message never sent. Someone coming towards you. Where you stand.*
⛔ *"The thing in your arms"* is a metaphor from card art and is not a product.
