## 2 · The frame — and why it is the one to get right

**"What's coming in the next seven days?"** — **Etsy rank #2** of ~130 listings, 7 of 10 badged.
It is one of only two frames in the whole roster that excludes nobody: *everyone has a next week.*

⭐ **This is a FRAME, not a situation.** She does not have to match a life-circumstance to enter it.
That is the entire reason it was chosen, and it is what the letter must protect: write it so a
widow, a woman in a new relationship and a woman with no romance at all can all find themselves in
it. ⛔ If your letter only works for a woman waiting on a man, you have written the wrong letter.

## 3 · The spread — `whats-coming-in-the-next-seven-days` · 1 free + 5 paid = 6

**Her question:** *What's coming in the next seven days?*

**Shape:** *A week is not a list of dates — it is one thing already moving, where it came from, the
form it takes when it surfaces, the loud thing that is not hers, what it asks of her, and what
waiting it out takes.*

| # | | Position | `job` — what it must FIND |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **FREE** | What's already moving | What is already in motion. The thing that started before this morning and is still moving, not something due to arrive |
| 2 | paid | Where it really started | Where the thing in motion actually began. Further back than this week, and not where she has been dating it from |
| 3 | paid | How it shows up | The small ordinary form it takes when it reaches the surface. What it will look like, not when it lands |
| 4 | paid | The loud one that isn't yours | The loud thing she is braced for, and whose problem it actually is. The one not hers to handle this week |
| 5 | paid | What the week asks of you | The one thing the week asks of her, and what it costs. An act she can start, not a decision to wait for |
| 6 | paid | What waiting it out takes | What a week spent waiting for the other thing takes from her. What she does not get back |

⛔ **The letter reads position 1 only.** Name positions 2–6 and leave them face down. ⭐ Note there
are **five** paid here, not six — the profile's examples say "six face down". Say five.

## 4 · 🔴 THE TRAP IN THIS FRAME — read twice

A letter titled *what's coming* is one sentence away from being a **prediction with a date on it**,
and that is the single most repeated complaint in the entire buyer pull — *"they will give you a
time line and then keep changing it"*. It is the scam signature she actively scans for.

- ⛔ **No day, no date, no window, no "by the weekend", no "within seven days".**
- ⛔ **No event forecast.** Not *"a message arrives"*, not *"someone from your past returns"*.
- ✅ **Answer with what is already in MOTION** — position 1's job says it outright: *"not something
  due to arrive."* The week is read as a thing already underway, not a thing scheduled.
- ✅ Position 3's own disqualifier is the model: *"what it will look like, not when it lands."*

⭐ The refusal in beat 10 writes itself here, and it should be specific: he will not put a day on it,
and he will say why.
