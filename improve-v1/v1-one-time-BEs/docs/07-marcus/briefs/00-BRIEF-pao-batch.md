## 1 · Four of Cups → "Am I asking for too much?"

**Reach.** The strongest evidence in the whole bank — flat over eight months, and a working reader
names *"am I too much, or not enough?"* as the question underneath all the others. Cut loose from a
man it takes a mother, a boss, an adult child.

**The card.** A young man sitting under a tree on a green mound, arms and legs crossed, looking down
at three gold cups standing in the grass in front of him. From a small cloud on the left, a hand
holds out a fourth cup. He is not looking at it.

**The signal.** A hand is holding something out and he has not looked at it. Nothing has been
refused — refusing takes a decision. It is the card of an offer that has simply not been examined.

**The position (the problem).** ⛔ Different sentence from the signal. The problem is not that people
do not offer. It is that she settled what she is allowed before anybody said no, so an offer arrives
and gets counted against a limit she set herself years ago.

**Banks.** A3 · B2 · C3 · D3 · E2 · F1 (popular reading: boredom, apathy) · **G2**

## 2 · Eight of Pentacles → "What's coming in the next seven days"

**Reach.** Etsy #2, seven of ten listings badged, and everybody has a next seven days.

**The card.** A young man on a bench with a hammer and chisel, cutting a pentacle held on his knee.
Six finished ones are fixed to a post beside him and one leans at his feet. A town in the distance
behind him and he has his back to it.

**The signal.** Six done, one in his hands, one on the floor. He is making the week one piece at a
time and looking only at the piece he is holding. It is the card of a stretch of days that gets
built rather than delivered.

**The position (the problem).** The problem is that she is braced for what the week will do to her,
when most of the week is made out of things she does. Waiting has become the plan.

⛔ **This letter must refuse the forecast the frame invites.** No dates, no predictions, no
"by Friday". The refusal is the integrity beat and it is the reason the letter can be trusted.

**Banks.** A4 · B4 · C2 · D4 · E3 · F3 (no correction) · **G4** — Marcus changed his own mind about
this card

## 3 · The High Priestess → "Just tell me what I need to know"

**Reach.** Etsy #1 and the highest badge density on the board. It names no situation at all, so
nobody is excluded by construction — and Marcus's daily already is a blind cut.

**The card.** A veiled woman on a stone seat between one black pillar and one white one. A crescent
moon at her feet, a cross on her breast, and a scroll in her lap marked TORA with most of it hidden
under her cloak. A curtain of pomegranates hangs behind her.

**The signal.** The scroll is half covered. She is holding something written and showing an inch of
it. It is the card of a thing known and deliberately not produced.

**The position (the problem).** ⛔ The brave read, and the one this frame needs. The problem is that
she would rather he guessed than name it herself, because naming it commits her to it. Asking a
stranger to tell her what she needs to know is the most polite way there is of not saying it.

⛔ **Do not insult her for asking.** The blind cut is a real thing he offers and he is not going to
pretend otherwise. What he refuses is pretending it costs her nothing.

**Banks.** A2 · B5 (no close) · C4 · D2 · E4 · F2 (popular reading: secrets, intuition) · **G5**
(nothing — the reading is the proof)
