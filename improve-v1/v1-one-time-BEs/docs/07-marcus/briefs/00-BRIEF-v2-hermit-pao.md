## The free position — yours to read

**1 · The problem.** The job, verbatim: *What is actually in the way, named as one thing she could
point at. Not the version she would give if somebody asked her, and not a feeling.*

⛔ **The two readings must be different sentences.** The signal above says she carries it alone.
The position must say something the signal does not — what is in the way is the carrying, not the
thing carried. Cover the position name and your second reading should stop making sense.
