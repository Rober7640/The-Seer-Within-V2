# 07-CONCEPT-MAP — the seven days, sold as concepts

**Tests the operator's proposal:** *each weekday sells a concept that already has demand; the spread
becomes the METHOD, not the product.* Scored against `docs/07-marcus/voc/` (3,724 lines, collected
2026-09-05), the position contract in [`07-P1`](../../copy/07-marcus/07-P1-the-seven-spreads.md) and
the voice rules in [`marcus-voice-profile.md`](../../copy/07-marcus/marcus-voice-profile.md).

⛔ Nothing in `copy/07-marcus/daily/` was touched. No letters written. Nothing committed.

---

# The decision chain

**1 · The proposal survives. All seven days keep their spread.** Not one spread had to be
rebuilt to carry a concept the market wants. That is the finding, and it was not the expected one —
the seven spreads were written in-house against a thesis, and they land on the market's own topic
list almost square.

**2 · Six of seven concepts change, and five of the six changes are a RESTATEMENT, not a swap.**
The concepts are broadly right; the *wording* of four of them either can't be delivered by the
positions or breaks Marcus's rule about reading the man. Restating them fixes both at once.

**3 · Wednesday's gap has an answer, and it may be the best day on the board.** The Small
Instruction's six positions describe one small reversible act and everything true about taking it.
The market has a product for exactly that — *"The Unsent Message Reading"* — and 01-forums has an
11-post group for it named *"should I text him?"*, which it calls *"the purest daily-email trigger
in the set."* Wednesday sells **the message she hasn't sent**.

**4 · Saturday is where the money and the risk are in the same sentence.** The market's biggest
love category is mind-reading — *Exact Thoughts · True Feelings & Telepathy · What They Wish They
Could Say · Hidden Intentions* — and Marcus refuses all of it. But 03-search's dominant question
shape is not *what does he feel*, it is **"he does X but not Y"**: eleven verbatim strings, all
fact-pairs. ⭐ **That is the same demand with the mind-reading removed.** Saturday sells the
contradiction, not the man, and no competitor on the Etsy page can copy it because they have all
promised his thoughts instead.

**5 · Monday is the one day still selling an in-house thesis, and it goes last.** *The one who
carries everyone* is real — eight verbatim instances across four files — but it has **zero listing
titles and zero search strings.** Nobody types it into a box. It is a feeling she recognises when
named, not a thing she shops for. That is exactly the weakness the operator identified in 07, so
Monday is ranked seventh on demand and written last.

**6 · Sunday has the clearest product category and the sharpest scam risk.** *"Whats Coming In
Next 7 Days"* is *"a whole product category"* on Etsy. It is also a rolling weekly forecast, and
`04-marketplace.md §2.1(a)` names the moved date as **the most repeated complaint in the entire
pull**. Ship it only with the no-date rule below locked.

**7 · The booking page needs less surgery than the Etsy finding implied.** It already sells the
answer, not the spread's name — the tiers are *The Spread / The Pattern / The Table*, which are
question shapes, not spreads. What it lacks is the **concept echo**: she clicks out of a letter
about a situationship and lands on *"The other six are still face down."* The mechanism greets her.
Her question doesn't. Detail in §4.

# 2 · Each day, on the five tests

| Test | Verdict |

| **4 · Cannibalisation** | ✅ None. |

⛔ **The reason it ranks last, stated plainly:** on the operator's own test — *a concept that arrives
carrying existing demand* — Monday fails. It is the one day still selling an in-house thesis.

## Sunday · The Zodiac → **What's coming in the next seven days**

| Test | Verdict |
|---|---|

| **3 · Compliance** | ⚠ House 7 is *the other person* and is free-block copy. It must read as **his side of the table**, never his mind. |

⛔ **The Sunday rule, and it is not optional.** The week ahead reads **what is already in motion and
what she will be asked for**. Never a dated event, never *he will contact you by*. The market has
already published the defence and it costs nothing: *"I DO NOT answer questions concerning: … Time
Frame predictions (Because of free will)."* ⭐ Marcus saying that in his own voice on a **forecast
day** is stronger than saying it anywhere else.

# 3 · ⭐ The ranked shortlist — what to commission

**What separates strong from marginal, in one line:** a concept is strong when **she already has a
word for it**, the spread's positions answer it **without touching a third party's mind**, and it
**survives being asked again next week**. All three, or it waits.

| # | Day | Concept | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Tue** | Situationship — the thing with no name on it | Best-evidenced demand on the board: *"Where Is This Going"* is the commonest love framing on the Etsy page, *situationship* is native vocabulary in three files, 17 forum posts. One framing change and it ships. |
| **2** | **Thu** | No contact — why I keep going back | *No contact* is a market keyword and the ex is the biggest forum group (29 posts). The positions fit with **nothing rewritten**, and the weekly re-ask is documented buyer behaviour, not a hope. |
| **3** | **Sat** | He does X but he still hasn't done Y | The market's biggest category, converted into the one thing Marcus can honestly do. Highest ceiling, hardest to write, and the offer's single best trust proof. |
| **4** | **Wed** | The message I haven't sent | Best spread fit and best recurring engine of the seven, and the market already named the product. ⚠ A $35 day — volume, not the top rung. |
| | | *— cut line: 1–4 justify a full letter now —* | |
| **5** | **Fri** | What it has already cost me | Enormous verbatim support and a perfect fit, but no listing sells sunk cost. She feels it; she doesn't shop for it. Needs the letter to name it before it can sell. |
| **6** | **Sun** | What's coming in the next seven days | A real product category and a natural Sunday — held below the line only because a rolling forecast is the exact scam signature she scans for. **Commission it after the no-date rule is agreed, not before.** |
| **7** | **Mon** | I'm always the one who reaches first | True, compliant, well-evidenced as a feeling — and the only day with zero listing titles and zero search strings. It is a naming email, not a demand email. |

⚠ **The nuance on Monday, because it changes when you'd write it and not whether.** Ranked 7th on
**demand**, not on quality. `marcus-voice-profile.md §1.3` calls shame-removal *"the commercial
engine, not a kindness — the funnel only earns when she types her question into a box. Shame is what
stops her typing."* Monday is the shame-removal day. If a later cycle shows the intake box is where
buyers drop, Monday jumps the queue.

# 4 · What this changes about the booking page

⭐ **Less than the Etsy finding implied.** [`07-C1`](../../copy/07-marcus/07-C1-the-booking-page.md)
already sells the answer rather than the spread — the rungs are *The Spread / The Pattern / The
Table*, which are **question shapes**, and the question-count ladder in `07-C5` is the market's own
unit (Etsy runs *1 Question* / *5 Question* as search facets). Four changes, in order of value.

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | ⭐ **Add the concept echo at beat 4.** She clicks out of a letter about a situationship and the first thing she reads is *"The other six are still face down."* The mechanism greets her; her question doesn't. Beat 4 already varies per `?c=`, and the link already carries `&s=<spread>` — so the day's concept line is a token, not new plumbing. | The lander's job is to echo what she arrived carrying. This is the one beat where the concept map reaches the page. |
| **2** | ⭐ **Make the beat-5 placeholder the concept's own sentence, in her words.** Tuesday: *"He says we're good but there's no name on it and it's been eight months — is this going anywhere."* Thursday: *"I blocked him three months ago and I'm still checking."* | 07-etsy §9: *"a woman who buys blind readings is buying not having to say it. The booking page asks her to type the hardest sentence in her week into a box. That is a real conversion cost and no page copy currently acknowledges it."* A concept-shaped placeholder lets her **confirm** instead of **confess**. |
| **3** | ✅ **Keep `{{SPREAD}}` in the masthead dateline. Never let it become the headline or a tier name.** | 07-etsy §7: the spread is a differentiator nobody else has, but the market has no appetite for a spread *as a thing to buy*. As a dateline it is proof of work; as a headline it is a product nobody searches for. The page is currently on the right side of this — the note is a guardrail, not a fix. |
| **4** | ⚠ **Do not let any new copy call $35 cheap.** | 07-etsy §4: the observed Etsy band is ≈USD 4–23 and heavy discounting is the default presentation. $35 is at or above the top of it. The no-clock advantage is the honest defence and it is still unclaimed by anyone in the sample. |

⭐ **One thing the map validated rather than changed.** Thursday's concept *(why do I keep going
back)* routes to **The Pattern**, and Thursday has no Pattern rung. Saturday's *(what he's doing)*
routes to **The Table**, and Saturday has no Table rung. Beat 9 already explains both refusals —
*"Today's cut IS that question … charging you again for them would be charging you twice."* The
concept map is not an overlay on the page; **the page already anticipated it.** That is the
strongest single piece of evidence that the day→concept assignment is right.

# 5 · Guardrails, in one place

⛔ **Rejected and not to be revived** — per the brief: **lucky numbers** (numerology, the
numerologist's persona; lottery-adjacent, and readers refuse it near-universally — *"I do not
predict the Lotto Numbers"*), and any **twin flame** framing anywhere in 07.

| Rule | Applies to |
|---|---|
| ⛔ Twin flame · soulmate · *the one* · destiny · divine timing · runner/chaser · reunion · separation | **All seven.** Sharpest on Thu and Sun |
| ⛔ No lucky, fortunate or auspicious numbers | Sun especially — twelve numbered houses invite it |
| ⛔ No dated prediction, no *by [date]*, no *he will contact you within* | **All seven.** Sun is where it will be tested |
| ⛔ No claim about a third party's thoughts, feelings or intentions — only what the spread shows is being **done** | Sat, Tue, Sun (house 7) |
| ⛔ The balance is never an instruction to leave or stay | Fri, and Tue's fork |
| ⛔ Never answer *will he come back* | Thu |
| ⛔ Never say what he does when he receives the message | Wed |
| ⚠ Health · pregnancy · legal · bereavement · debt hardship · self-harm — refuse by name, never answer | **All seven** |
