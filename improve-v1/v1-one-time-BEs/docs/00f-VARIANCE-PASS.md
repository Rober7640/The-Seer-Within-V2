# 00f — Corpus-wide device-variance pass

Run 2026-08-04 across **02, 03 and 04** (36 copy assets). ⚠ **05 is not written**, so this pass is
three-quarters of the check the rule asks for — anything 05 introduces must be re-run against all
four. Re-run with `node scripts/copy-check.cjs`.

**The rule:** *any device used in 2+ offers must vary.* Not a compliance rule — a quality one. The
deck sells the same woman up to four times and she will spot the trick the second time.

## Collisions found and fixed

The scan caught real repetition the per-offer audits could not see. All fixed in 03/04; **02 is the
ratified pilot and was left alone.**

1. ⚠ **The abandon nudges were nearly one email in three costumes.** Four separate lines identical
   across all three: *"and then I'll leave it alone"* · *"there's also something I didn't put in the
   letter, because I don't like leading with a gift"* · *"I'd far rather you kept your money than
   spent it on a night you weren't sure about"* · *"I won't write to you about this again"*. A woman
   who abandons two checkouts would have received the same email twice. All rewritten in 03 and 04.
2. **The +1h nudge's core sentence was verbatim in 03 and 04** — *"If it was a decision, close this
   and I'll say no more."* The *device* (decision-or-interruption) repeats deliberately across all
   three; the wording must not.
3. **Two offers shared a +24h subject line** — *"one more thing and then I'll stop"*. 04's changed.
4. **Upsell beats:** *"But here's what most women don't understand"*, *"I can tell you know what I
   mean"*, and the `someone` bucket's *"clear the line between you and {personName}"* were shared
   02↔03. The `purpose` bucket collided twice — the second time on a line introduced by the first fix.
5. **Bump ease-line** *"a simple, clear instructional and I can start it tonight"* appeared in all
   three (it is §4a beat 4's example, taken too literally).
6. **Booking-page statement 1** was *"Yes — you were right, Evelyn. You read it correctly"* in all
   three. P8 mandates the *move*, not the words. Now: *you were right* / *you read me correctly* /
   *you had it right*.
7. **Thank-you deliverability line, under-button microcopy, and three letter-close constructions**
   were shared 02↔04 — both being 24h READING offers, they had drifted into the same shapes.

## Accepted, not fixed

- **"ledger"** — 02's free gift vs 03's big idea. Accepted deliberately at the blocker stage; the two
  senses are kept distinct (02's is a record she keeps, 03's is a debt that gets closed) and 03's
  mechanism rebuild made the distinction sharper, not weaker. ⛔ Do not re-flag.
- **The decision-or-interruption device** in all three +1h nudges. Four sentences of mechanics with
  no argument in them; inventing worse ones twice to avoid a rhyme costs more than the rhyme.
  Wording now differs in all three.
- **Four framework-mandated statement stems** on the booking pages — *"I give Evelyn Cross permission
  to…"*, *"this is done by a professional…"*. These are §3's specified ladder, and the buyer is
  signing a standard form; varying boilerplate for its own sake would read as inconsistency rather
  than freshness.
- **Beat 6's shape** — all three origins are *she was doing something ordinary and it interrupted
  her*. That is the framework's own beat and it is unavoidable; the mechanics are wholly distinct.
