# 00a — THE BRIEFS

**Single source of truth.** Nothing gets written for any offer until its brief is open on the
desk. Every headline, statement, price justification, wait, limit and next-offer hook derives
from these four fields.

Extracted from the source sales letters (01–05), then reframed where compliance or evidence
required it. Both versions are recorded, with the source lines that ground them — so nothing here
is invented, and every change is traceable.

Method: [00e-FRAMEWORK §0](./00e-FRAMEWORK-BEs.md) · Assets: [00c-ASSETS](./00c-ASSETS-BEs.md)

---

## The four fields

| Field | Test |
|---|---|
| **THE ONE PROBLEM** | Could she have typed this sentence into the V1 chat? Source it from real `concern` data |
| **THE BIG IDEA** | One sentence, no jargon. It must **reframe** the problem, not restate it |
| **THE ONE PROMISE** | Run So What? three times. Something she'd *feel*. Never falsifiable, never about a third party |
| **THE MECHANISM** | Must explain the price, the wait **and** the limits. If it only explains one, it isn't finished |

**Derivation map** — what each field is responsible for downstream:

```
  headline · subject lines           ← big idea
  the free read's threat             ← the enemy inside the problem
  the fork (free read, unit 2)       ← the gap between problem and promise
  booking statement 2                ← big idea, in her voice
  booking statements 4, 5, 6         ← mechanism (scarcity · SLA · price)
  the order bump                     ← the problem standing NEXT to the brief's
  wait-filler register and length    ← the problem's emotional state
  the product's unit shape and count ← mechanism
  the next offer's hook              ← the mechanism's own LIMIT
```

## 03 — Judgement Day *(ACT · pay-what-you-want, anchored $300→$250 · 3 days · needs reply)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **PROBLEM** | "Someone did this to me and nothing ever happened to them." |
| **BIG IDEA** | The debt was never yours. It has been sitting on you because nobody closed the ledger. |
| **PROMISE** | You put it down. You sleep. |
| **MECHANISM** | **The Three Nights of Accounting.** A debt is closed in three motions and they cannot be combined: night one the **Entry** — it is written down properly for the first time, named and itemised; night two the **Transfer** — the weight is moved off her name onto the page where it belongs; night three the **Closing** — the line is ruled under the account and the page sealed so it cannot be reopened. One account held at a time. |

**Grounding lines:** *"something tells me you have unfinished business. That someone has wronged
you… and maybe a score needs to be settled"* · *"in this modern world, people take advantage of
that"* · *"each ritual takes between 2 to 5 days for me to prepare and perform"*

⚠ **REFRAMED FROM THE SOURCE — twice.**

*Source big idea:* an ancient hex rains karmic justice on your enemies.
*Source promise:* their deceit exposed, exiled in shame, your ex crawling back.
*Why changed:* a stated outcome about a real, named, non-consenting person. Hits Stripe's UDAAP
entry for outrageous claims and deceptive testimonials, and it is the most exposed sentence class
in the deck. **The reframe keeps the payoff and drops the claim** — closure for her, not
punishment for them.

⚠ **MECHANISM REBUILT IN EVELYN'S OWN TRADITION** *(operator, 2026-08-04)*. The source petitions
Baron Samedi, Kalfu and Legba. Evelyn Cross is a card reader writing to a list that has had months
of her doing exactly that; a tarot reader who abruptly works Haitian lwa is a visible register
break to anyone who knows either practice, and it is not hers to work. **The three offices are
kept and re-derived** — open, turn, seal becomes Entry, Transfer, Closing — so nothing downstream
of this field loses its reason-why.

*The offer keeps the name **Judgement Day***: judgement here is the weighing and closing of an
account, which is what the word meant before it meant a hex. The product keeps **The Record of
Judgement**, which is now literally what it is — a ledger page.

**The mechanism explains:** the three-night SLA (three motions, one a night, and a debt entered and
closed the same night is not closed) · the scarcity (one account open at a time — she cannot hold
two people's debts side by side without confusing whose is whose) · **the pricing** (a debt settled
with money has been *bought*, not closed — which is the strongest pay-what-you-want reason-why in
the deck and much stronger than the source's *"it's not about the money for me"*) · the product's
three-verdict structure.

**Its limit → next offer:** Samedi settles debts. He does not mend hearts. When the interference
falls away he comes back confused, and the wrong word from her in those first days undoes it.
*(→ 04)*

## The deck read as one argument

The four briefs are not independent. Each promise is the next problem's precondition, and each
mechanism's limit is the next offer's opening.

```
  02  can't see what's coming   →  a spread shows, it doesn't lift
  03  nobody closed the ledger  →  Samedi settles debts, not hearts
  04  don't know what to send   →  leaves give words, not removal
  05  part of him is elsewhere  →  one thing the cut couldn't reach
```

⚠ **Every device that appears in more than one offer must vary per offer.** The deck sells the
same person up to four times. Two pay-what-you-want reason-whys, two wait-filler parables, two
scarcity justifications — a buyer who takes 03 and 05 sees both. Derive each from its own
mechanism and they differ automatically; copy-paste and they don't.

## Standing constraints

- **Women-only for v1.** Copy ships unmirrored; 04 and 05 are held for she-target and
  unknown-target buyers. 02 and 03 go to everyone.
- **Static product for MVP**, plus three echo slots (one verbatim) merged from V1 Supabase data.
  A product with zero facts about the buyer is this category's documented failure mode.
- **Read the spread, not the card.** A card's meaning comes from the draw it sits in and what it's
  paired with. Nothing a persona has published about the same card on another day constrains a new
  reading. *(operator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Predictions are stated, not hedged.** No "tends to", no "may indicate". This is a spiritual
  product and its buyers came for a reader with a spine. Windows and dates are allowed — the
  source's *five to seven days* is back in 02.
