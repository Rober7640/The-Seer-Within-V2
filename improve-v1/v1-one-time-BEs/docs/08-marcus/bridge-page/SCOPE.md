# 08 Marcus — post-purchase bridge page

Status: local page implemented for review, 2026-09-10.

## Place in the funnel

**AWeber email → booking page with bump and payment → bridge page → audio Upsell 1 → thank-you.**

The bridge appears only after the main $35 payment is confirmed. It is not an order bump, checkout,
receipt, or second sales page.

## Job of the page

1. Confirm that the written reading is secured and being prepared.
2. Repeat the buyer’s delivery email and the correct 24-hour or 12-hour deadline.
3. State that nothing on the next page can cancel or delay the written order.
4. Create a clean transition to one optional way to receive the same reading.
5. Send the buyer to Upsell 1 with one uncomplicated **Continue** button.

The bridge should be short. It must not explain the whole audio offer, display the audio price, ask
for the buyer’s details again, or require another decision. Upsell 1 owns the audio sales argument.

## Local copy

**Order confirmed**

> Your personal reading is now being prepared.

> Thank you, [First name]. I have your question, your saved cards, and the personal card connected
> to your name.

> Your written reading is secured. It will be prepared for [delivery email] and delivered within
> [24/12] hours of payment. Nothing on the next page changes or delays that order.

> Before you view your receipt, I want to show you one optional way to receive the same reading.

Button: **Continue**

## Acceptance checks

- Direct visits require a valid paid-order reference; no order is inferred from browser copy.
- Refresh preserves the order and keeps the page between payment and Upsell 1.
- Name, delivery email, and deadline come from the verified saved order.
- Visiting or leaving the bridge does not trigger payment, fulfillment, or the audio purchase.
- The page works at mobile and desktop widths without hiding the primary action.
