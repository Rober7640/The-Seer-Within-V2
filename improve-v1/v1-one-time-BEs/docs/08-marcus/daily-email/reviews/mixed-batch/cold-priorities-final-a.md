Remaining actual ambiguity: in “With no next task being performed, there's room to ask whether it needs to be yours,” I do not settle whether the person currently doing no task is the pictured knight or me taking a pause. I take “it” to mean my next task, but the sentence shifts from the figure’s inactivity to my responsibility without explicitly locating that task. The surrounding context identifies the opening movement and the dancing figure, so those earlier uncertainties are resolved. The other flexible words do not leave the letter’s meaning unresolved on this pass. The birth-date purpose and personal-card method are not explained, but the request and claim themselves are understandable.
