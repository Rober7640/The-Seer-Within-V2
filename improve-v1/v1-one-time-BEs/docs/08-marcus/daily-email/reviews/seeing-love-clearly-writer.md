# Writer notes — seeing-love-clearly

Status: writer draft only; not cold-read, approved, illustrated, built, or sent.

- Subject: What are you not seeing in love?
- Spread: Six Questions, adapted in-house; six positions; positions 1–2 turned.
- Free discovery: Distinguish what you hope love might become from what you are experiencing; you can ask for clarity while remaining open to connection.
- Personal continuation: What hope deserves time, what needs closer attention, what needs asking, and the reader's next step.
- Audience: Does not require a current relationship. Hope can inflate a promising connection or make a real beginning look disappointing.
- Distinction from commitment sibling: Focuses on the reader's interpretation and expectations; no claims about a man's intentions or failure to commit. Some shared potential/present territory is intrinsic to this approved topic.

## Build anchors

- Opening: `When you think about love, how much of what you see is happening now—and how much is what you hope will happen?`
- Reading setup: `The first of this morning's six Rider–Waite cards gives that question a picture.`
- Crosshead: `That's the two I can turn.`
- Close start: `The next question is which hope deserves more time, and which uncertainty needs an answer.`
- CTA: `[Explore what needs your attention in love](BOOKING).`
- Use normal body/letter close treatment, no administrative heading.
