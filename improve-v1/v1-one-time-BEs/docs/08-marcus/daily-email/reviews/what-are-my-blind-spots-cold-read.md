## Second pass: remaining shared friction

The second three-reader pass confirmed that the main answer was understandable. At least two readers still paused at the third position label, at loose references in the seven-card preview, and at why three cards could be interpreted before the name was supplied.

Repairs:

- Changed “what you’ve worked out” to “what you have concluded.”
- Explained that the first three cards describe a broadly recognizable pattern and the remaining seven locate that pattern in the recipient’s life.
- Listed the seven remaining areas in parallel, concrete language.
- Explained the name-to-number-to-personal-card method and why the personal card becomes the lens for the remaining positions.
- Tightened the opening, Tree of Life description, Moon evidence, and Two of Swords explanation to reduce pronoun and metaphor load.
