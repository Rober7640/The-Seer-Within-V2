MORNING EDITION SIX CARDS · ONE QUESTION
 

%FIRSTNAME%,

Marcus. Six cards on one question this morning: what is my higher calling. Two up, four face down. Rider–Waite.

 
THE TABLE THIS MORNING — six cards on one question. Two of them are looking back at you. Four of them are not. Rider–Waite–Smith deck, 1909.
 
 
 
One — what you keep coming back to. The Star.
 
FIG. I · POSITION ONE
Turned. The Star.

A woman kneeling at the edge of a pool with nothing on. Two jugs. She’s pouring one into the water and one onto the ground beside it. Above her, one big eight-pointed star and seven smaller ones. A bird in a tree behind her shoulder.

Now look at how she’s kneeling. One foot is in the water. The other knee is on the land. She hasn’t got in.

Look at the top of it. THE STAR, spelled out. Some cards in this deck have a name on them instead of a number, and those are the heavy ones. Whatever you keep going back to isn’t a passing interest. It’s a source. You go, and you kneel at it, and you put one foot in.

You’ve been doing that for years.

 
 
 
Two — what you’ve been calling it instead. The Seven of Pentacles.
 
FIG. II · POSITION TWO
Turned. The Seven of Pentacles.

A man leaning on a long-handled hoe, both hands folded over the top of it, looking at a bush. Seven gold discs growing on it. He’s stopped working to look at them.

He’s calling it patience.

That’s what you’ve called it too. Not ready yet. Needs more time. Wrong year, wrong money, wrong everything — and every one of those has been true, and not one of them is why.

Now look at the two together. Position one has a name on it. Position two is a seven. The thing you keep returning to is bigger than the reason you keep giving it.

 

That’s the two I can turn.

The other four carry the weight.

 
 

The third is what this has already cost you. It has. Whether or not you’ve added it up.

The fourth is what you’d have to put down — and it sits further in than it looks.

The fifth is who you’d have to stop being. That’s the real one.

The sixth is where it starts. Not someday. Where.

And you’ve been responsible, %FIRSTNAME%. You’ve kept it alive by not risking it. You’ve protected it from failing by never letting it be tested, and that is how a sensible person looks after something precious.

Keeping it alive isn’t the same as living it.

NOTICE TO READERS
 

I can’t turn those four yet.

On their own they don’t say much. They only mean something once I know which card is yours.

Everyone has one. Your name gives it to me — first and last, nothing else.

And it never changes. Same name, same card, every time I lay them.

Tell me your name here. That’s all that page asks you for.

So this morning, one line: write down what you’d do with one free Tuesday. Not a free year. A Tuesday.

Then read your line back and see whether you’d need anyone’s permission to do it.

P.S. One foot in the water for years is still one foot in the water.
