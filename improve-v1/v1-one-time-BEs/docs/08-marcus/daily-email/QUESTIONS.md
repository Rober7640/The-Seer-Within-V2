# 08 · THE NAMED THINGS

**A well, not a schedule.** Nothing here is a rotation, a ranking or a backlog. The operator names
the morning's question. This is what he draws from when he wants one.

**Source.** Fifty questions from a working medium's list —
<https://www.sherylwagnermedium.com/blog/questions-to-ask-tarot-cards>. Grouped below by her
categories, kept in her wording. Two of them arrive stapled into one bullet, so fifty bullets give
fifty-one questions.

**Her craft rule, worth keeping.** Open-ended beats yes/no. Over-specific questions limit the answer.
Her own example: *"Is this person my soulmate?"* is a poor question, *"What is this relationship
teaching me right now?"* is a good one.

⭐ **Register: hers is allowed, his is not.** SHAPE.md voice rule 4 bans *energy, journey, universe,
soul, superpower* **in Marcus's own sentences**. It does not touch the question she brings. The deck
line quotes the named thing as she asked it. *"What my soul is experiencing"* is a perfectly good
morning — Marcus just answers it with a man leaning on a hoe. ⛔ Do not reject a question for
sounding new-age, and do not sand its register off.

**Two mornings are already written.** [`why-they-go-quiet`](letters-02/why-they-go-quiet.md) and
[`what-is-my-higher-calling`](letters-02/what-is-my-higher-calling.md). Overlaps are flagged in the
notes.

## The well

**Short form** is the same thing in five words or fewer, because the deck line has to hold it:
*"Marcus. The Horseshoe this morning, on one question: how I'm learning to love."* The note column
is empty unless something needs saying.

⚠ **What this list is short on.** It is a medium's self-work list, so it is nearly all her, alone.
There is no equivalent of *why he goes quiet* anywhere in the fifty — the closest is *saying how I
feel*. The daily's reader has a man on her mind most mornings. This well does not feed that on its
own.

## Rejected, and why

Nine questions dropped. The gate each one failed, so nobody re-proposes them.

| The question | Gate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What is a sign that I am in the most loving state possible? | 5 | Six positions would give six signs. That is a list, not a spread. The positions are not distinct |
| What do I need to know about my mind, body, and spirit? | 5 | Three named things stapled together, so no position belongs to one question. The body third also walks into health |
| What is affecting me most from my past, present, and future? | 5 | This is a three-card spread with a question written on it, and SHAPE.md bans *past / present / future* from a letter anyway |
| What part of my work can I outsource to someone else or ask for support? | 3 | Needs her actual job. Marcus cannot say which task. *What I can call in* is the half that survives |
| How should I move forward in initiating this relationship? | 2 | The answer only means anything as a prediction of what he does back. *Saying how I feel* is the readable half |
| How will I know if my feelings are reciprocated? | 2 | Straight prediction of another person's feeling. A promise he cannot keep |
| What phase is this relationship growing into? | 2 | A joint future is his decision as much as hers. He can read what she brings to it, not where it goes |
| What is the past life connection we have together? | 1 | One fact about two people, and every position is a gloss on the same fact. It is the source page's own bad question — *is this person my soulmate* — in older clothes |
| How can I express a greater love and support for my friend? | 3 | Needs what the friend actually wants, and he has no access to her. *Where the line should be* covers the same friendship from her side |

## What makes a good one

1. **It holds three or more different positions, not one answer repeated.** *What did it teach me*
   holds four. *What is a sign that I am loving* holds one, six times.
2. **She is the subject, even when there is a man in it.** The moment an honest answer needs him to
   do or decide something, it is gone. *What draws me to him* stays. *What phase are we entering*
   does not.
3. **Open beats yes / no — her page's own rule.** And over-specific beats nothing: a question that
   needs her name, her job or her dates cannot be laid for a stranger.
4. **Her register is welcome, his is not.** *Soul*, *energy*, *superpower*, *block* can stand in the
   question. Marcus answers in things you could photograph. That gap is the reading.
5. **There has to be something to photograph inside it.** *A home*, *a hoe*, *one free Tuesday*. A
   question made only of concepts — *my most empowering step toward my future goals* — gives back
   only concepts.
6. **A picture has to be able to answer it.** The closing act is cut, so the reading is all she
   gets, and the answer reaches her as one odd detail in a drawing she can go and look at — *"her
   feet aren't tied"*. If you cannot imagine a card carrying the answer, it is not a morning yet.
   ⚠ `PAID-READING.md` §2 still says the morning ends with a line she writes. It is stale; the
   close now says *"Nothing else is on that page."*
7. ⚠ **Two questions about the same thing are one morning.** This well has forty-two rows and about
   twenty distinct named things. Read the notes before scheduling two of them in a week.
