## Product fit

Sources: [paid reading scope](../../paid-reading/SCOPE.md), [booking scope](../../booking-page/SCOPE.md), [email craft](../SHAPE.md), and project `.claude/skills/marcus-daily/SKILL.md`.

Current product is a $35 written personal continuation, delivered as a PDF through a signed link. Paid positions are drawn separately for the buyer and interpreted with the personal card from the full birth name and Life Path from date of birth. Free positions are not reread. No situation or question box is collected; do not promise comparison of two named job offers or knowledge of a partner's thoughts. These categories fit as reflective readings; exact positions remain subject to downstream spread selection.

Keep the buyer desire explicit. A career reading can explore fit and next steps, but cannot identify a guaranteed job or hiring date. Purpose can explore meaningful direction, but cannot establish a fixed destiny. Love blockages can explore the reader's experience and choices, but cannot promise a spell, removal of a curse, guaranteed partner, or someone else's future actions. The listing's spell bundle is not transferred into Marcus's offer.

Marketplace questions such as “Will he return?” or “What does he secretly feel?” would require a separate product decision. They are not silently reclassified as love blockages in this slate.

## Editorial priority

1. Career direction: recognizable offer, more than one listing, fresh against the six logged drafts. Audience interest in work is unverified.
2. Love blockages: recognizable offer and broad relationship relevance; exact category already drafted, so avoid treating new wording as a new category.
3. Life purpose: clear packaging and broad reach beyond employment; higher calling already drafted, so use a distinct discovery or rework the existing asset deliberately.

This is a production recommendation, not a sales ranking. Explore all three before allocating around winners.
