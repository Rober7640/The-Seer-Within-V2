## Audience assumptions and overlap

Career: reader has an interest in work, returning to work, self-employment or a future vocational direction. This does not cover every subscriber. Do not assume she has a job offer or wants to quit. CAR-01 has adjacent choice territory to `the-risk-i-wont-regret`; keep the concrete question about working life, not bravery in general. CAR-02 must develop advancement versus desired work, rather than replay CAR-01's competence versus fit.

Love: romantic closeness matters to the reader. No ex, current partner or dating-app scenario is required. LOV-01 substantially overlaps `whats-blocking-love`, including its protection/withdrawal territory; consider revising that existing draft after checking its stale claims and close. LOV-02 has adjacent territory to `what-it-taught-me`; avoid turning it into a retrospective breakup lesson. Neither is evidence that the reader caused a relationship problem.

Purpose: reader wants meaning; employment status and a particular hobby are not required. Both share a category with `what-is-my-higher-calling`. PUR-01 is about recognizing useful strengths; PUR-02 is about where purpose can be expressed. Avoid repeating the existing persistent-interest/waiting-for-certainty discovery or its Star/Seven of Pentacles details.

## Test hypotheses

- MAR-CAREER: a personally useful direction may motivate buying. Compare the two angles within the same paid scope after initial category exploration.
- MAR-LOVE: understanding an obstacle to closeness may motivate buying. Test protection versus unreciprocated effort without narrowing to a specific relationship status.
- MAR-PURPOSE: understanding personal gifts and meaningful contribution may motivate buying. Test overlooked ability versus purpose beyond employment.

No subject is a declared winner. First subject is the proposed starting execution, second is reserved for a later controlled subject test. Do not vary both subject and body and call the result a subject test.
