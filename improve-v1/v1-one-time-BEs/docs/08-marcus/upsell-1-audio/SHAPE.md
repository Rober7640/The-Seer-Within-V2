# 08 · The shape of audio Upsell 1

Craft authority. **Latest decision: A is selected as reusable copy across daily questions; [COPY.md](COPY.md) is the selected text.** The earlier requirement to rewrite the argument for a specific question is superseded for this standard upsell. Joel accepted the warning-led argument. [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) owns product facts; [WRITING-BRIEF.md](WRITING-BRIEF.md) owns the common inputs and style assignments. The old softer examples are superseded.

## Goal

Make the reader see why jumping to the final answer can leave her without the understanding needed to use it, then explain why hearing the report in sequence is worth considering. She already owns the full written interpretation.

## Movement

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Transition | The bridge page confirms the verified written purchase. Upsell 1 opens with a short warning rather than repeating the receipt. |
| Warning | State plainly: do not skip straight to the answer. Establish the mistake early, not after several reflective paragraphs. |
| Consequence | Explain what skipping loses: why the advice fits this interpretation and how to use it. No vague “deeper meaning” claim. |
| Concrete example | Explain what any reader misses by jumping ahead: why the advice follows from the cards and what to consider before using it. Do not hardcode healing, support, relationships, or a particular spread. |
| Audio mechanism | A spoken version walks through the explanation in order before the conclusion; she can pause or replay. This is the reason for the format, not a detached list of features. |
| Offer stack | State what she receives: the complete reading narrated in order, the same saved spread and personal-card interpretation, private playback, pause/replay, and the inherited 24-hour or 12-hour deadline. |
| Objection | Make clear that the written reading is already complete and continues whether she buys audio or not. Explain the format advantage without weakening the original purchase. |
| Close | Show the additional price once beside the offer, then use one benefit-led accept CTA and one plain-language decline. The accept action purchases Upsell 1 directly; do not add another review page. |

These are jobs, not compulsory paragraph slots. Each style may change rhythm and emphasis, but must keep the warning, consequence, example and reason to buy unmistakable. Do not make all three versions share identical openings or closes.

## Voice and accuracy

- Firm about the habit; respectful toward the buyer. No shame or threats.
- Explain connections in familiar words. No riddles, unexplained metaphors or customer-facing “lens” jargon.
- No unsupported “everybody/most buyers do this” statistics or invented observations from Marcus’s customers. Address the temptation directly.
- No claim audio forces attention, guarantees understanding/healing, or contains withheld answers.
- The whole written reading remains worthwhile. One natural sentence can establish that; repeated defensive disclaimers weaken the page.
- Do not promise Marcus personally records it or state an audio duration. The approved delivery deadline is the same 24-hour or 12-hour deadline as the written order.
- Do not invent testimonials, measured buyer behavior, scarcity, a discount, a guarantee, or bonuses.
- Use the real question/positions as scope; do not invent a paid card or report conclusion while the report is being prepared.
- No required word count. Cut repetition; keep enough explanation to make the warning fair and the audio offer persuasive.

## Before handing in a draft

- [ ] The warning is clear near the beginning.
- [ ] The consequence is clear and applies across daily questions.
- [ ] The example is plainly illustrative, not a fabricated reading result.
- [ ] Audio solves the stated format/use problem without undermining the written purchase.
- [ ] The offer is concrete: contents, inherited deadline, additional price, accept action and decline path are all visible.
- [ ] The assigned writing style is evident in structure and rhythm, not just the headline.
- [ ] The close finishes the argument without a mechanical summary.
- [ ] The price is an editorial token and accept/decline accurately describe the prototype action.
- [ ] No unsupported narrator, delivery, customer-behavior or outcome claim.
