# Canon entry shape

Each entry is an internal reference reading of 500–800 words. The headings use stable IDs because a
workflow claim must point back to the exact passage that supports it.

## Required qualities

Each entry must:

1. define what the number means in this specific role;
2. describe useful capacities without promising that the person has developed them;
3. show how the same capacity can become rigid, avoidant, controlling, diffuse, or performative
   under pressure;
4. name observable choice, work, communication, or relationship patterns as possibilities;
5. include a growth practice that changes behavior rather than offering encouragement;
6. end with questions that can confirm or disconfirm the interpretation;
7. avoid occupations or life events stated as facts;
8. avoid destiny, diagnosis, moral ranking, and predictions.

## Tone

Write like a careful observer. Prefer a concrete distinction over a flattering adjective. “May
keep refining the plan because a visible mistake feels more costly than a delayed start” is useful.
“Hardworking, loyal, and destined for success” is not.

Use conditional language where a statement could be mistaken for biography. A canon entry may say
what a pattern looks like when present. It may not tell the eventual customer that the pattern is
present before the cards and question support it.

## Word count

The count covers prose under the eight passage headings. It excludes the entry title, headings, and
the final `Word count:` line. The range is a content gate, not a reason to repeat a point.
