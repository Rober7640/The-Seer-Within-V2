## Build notes

- **It sells nothing.** No next offer, no link but the download.
- **It fires when the audio is ready, and only then.** It does not wait for the written delivery to send first, and the written delivery does not wait for it. The copy works in either order: "same as in the written one" is true whether she has opened the written one yet or not. The sign-off names only the audio, so it is true even if the audio lands first.
- **The written reading is never made smaller.** "Same cards, same order, nothing in it that isn't in the written one." No "full", no "complete", no "deeper".
- **One word, one job.** "The written one" is the PDF. "The recording" is the file. "The page" is not used.
- **The card is not named.** The loop opened in the confirmations is closed in the written reading and on the audio, never in an email. Her card gets its one-clause explanation here too — "the one your name points to" — and no more.
- **No length is promised.** We do not know how long the audio runs, so the copy says "while it plays" and never "for the length of it" or a number of minutes.
- **The instruction is repeated on purpose.** "In order" opens and closes beat 5, matching the written delivery's habit.
- **One link.** `%AUDIO_URL%` appears once, in the handover line.
- **The support line never tells her the address she is reading at is wrong.** "If you want it sent to a different address".
- **Subject is a receipt.** Matches the audio confirmation's promised title exactly. No emoji, no urgency.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 2** — salutation and sign-off line. Cap is two for a delivery.
- **Body length:** under 160 words (see track report).
