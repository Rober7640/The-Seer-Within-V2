# Cold read 01 — the four post-purchase emails (draft 1, 2026-09-13)

Method: `cold-read` skill. Text frozen with real values (Karen · "What are my blind spots?" · Tree of
Life · seven · 24 hours · 9:40 tomorrow morning). Three readers, none had seen the brief, the letters,
or each other. Audience line: a woman over 55, on a phone, one pass, first thing in the morning.

Verdict key: BROKEN = a reader wrote *I can't* · AMBIGUOUS = readers read it two ways, or all three
re-read it · CLEAR = all three said the same thing back.

Only failures are listed. Everything not listed was CLEAR for all three.

**Fine as written, all three agreed:** the deadline sentence, the titled-email promise, the read-in-order
instruction (both emails), the audio-comes-separately line, the audio-never-holds-up-the-written line.

**One artefact of the freeze, not the copy:** "(this is a link)" — ignore.

**What every reader could not place, by the end of email 4:** the Tree of Life; how a card becomes
hers. Both are things the daily letter explained, and the emails assume she remembers. She did not.
