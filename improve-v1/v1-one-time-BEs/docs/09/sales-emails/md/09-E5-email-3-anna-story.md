Let me tell you about Anna, dear, and the wish she placed inside.

### She knew whose name to write

Anna had been apart from Daniel for four months.

They still spoke occasionally. A birthday message. A question about something he’d left behind. Just enough contact to keep her wondering.

Every time his name appeared on her phone, she hoped this would be the conversation.

The one where he said he missed her.

When she sat down with her charm, she wrote:

*“I wish for another chance with Daniel.”*

Then she paused.

She remembered their last few months together. How often she’d swallowed what she wanted to say because she was afraid of starting another argument.

Underneath his name, she added:

*“A chance where we both show up.”*

She rolled the paper, placed it inside the capsule, and slipped the bracelet onto her left wrist.

### Then came the call

In this story, Daniel called on a Thursday evening.

Nothing dramatic at first.

“How have you been?”

Usually, Anna would have said, “Fine.”

She would have kept the conversation light. Made him laugh. Left him with the impression that everything was easy.

But as she held the phone, her other hand rested on the bracelet.

She remembered the sentence inside.

*A chance where we both show up.*

“I’ve missed you,” she said. “And there are things I’d want us to talk about if we saw each other.”

There was a pause.

Then Daniel asked whether she was free on Sunday.

### What mattered happened after they sat down

Anna had pictured that first coffee so many times that she’d almost forgotten there would be an actual conversation.

This time, she told him what had hurt.

She listened when he told her where he’d felt shut out.

They stayed longer than either had planned.

Before they left, he asked to see her again—and they chose a day.

Over the following weeks, they kept those plans. Sometimes the conversations were uncomfortable. But Anna stopped pretending she needed less affection than she did.

The result she wanted began to take a shape she could recognise: two people making time, speaking honestly, and deciding to try again.

That is how Anna’s story ends, dear.
