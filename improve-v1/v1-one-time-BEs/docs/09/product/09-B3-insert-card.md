## Front

**Your Heart Cleanser Love Charm**

Before you wear it for the first time:

1. Open the capsule.
2. Write your wish on one of the papers. Keep it short.
3. Roll the paper small enough to fit.
4. Place it inside and close the capsule.
5. Hold the charm in your left palm. Rest your right hand over it and say:
   "I am ready to receive love, too."
6. Put it on your left wrist, your receiving side.

— Evelyn

## Back

**Caring for your charm**

- Put it on and take it off gently. Don't force it.
- Take it off before a bath, a shower or a swim.
- Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth.
- Keep it in its gift box when you're not wearing it.
- The spare papers are for the day you want to write a new wish.
