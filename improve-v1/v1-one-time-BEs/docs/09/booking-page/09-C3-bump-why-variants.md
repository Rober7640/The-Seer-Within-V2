## What changed in all three, and why

1. **"charge" is gone from the label.** In round 1 all three readers of the current wording read "charge my charm" next to "+$11.11" as *billing*, not Reiki. That's true of the label live on the page today, whichever variant is picked. All three now say **"give my charm Reiki"**.
2. **"still ships" became "ships … either way".** Two readers looked back up the page for where the shipping time had been said before. It hadn't: the small print sits under the button.
3. **Each variant gives ONE reason the Reiki helps her.** A: all day. B: the hard mornings. C: without telling anyone her wish.

## Variant B — on the hard mornings, her charm still holds Evelyn's hope

**Why this angle:** names a morning she will recognise (the one where hoping for herself is hard) and says what the Reiki does on that morning. It is about her, never about the charm lacking anything.

> **☐ Yes — have Evelyn give my charm Reiki before it's packed.**
> **+$11.11** · Reiki is a Japanese practice that uses the hands. Evelyn holds my charm and gives it Reiki with one hope for me: that I receive love. Some mornings I'll find it hard to hope for myself. On those mornings, my charm still holds Evelyn's hope. My charm ships within 2 business days either way, so the Reiki adds no wait.

60 words after the price.

**Cold read: ✅ CLEAR (round 4, the text above).** All three readers said back what she gets and gave the same reason in their own words: *"a backup source of hope on hard days"* · *"the charm carries hope for her on the days she can't carry it herself"* · *"on mornings when the reader can't hope for herself, the charm still holds that hope from Evelyn"*. None read it as the charm being weak or unfinished. One noted, correctly, that without the box her charm simply wouldn't hold Evelyn's hope. That is the add-on, not a defect.

| Sentence | What readers stumbled on | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Some mornings I'll find it hard to hope for myself. | 2 of 3 paused on "it" (for a beat, did it mean the charm?) then read it as "hoping feels hard" | CLEAR, small hitch |
| On those mornings, my charm still holds Evelyn's hope. | 2 of 3 noticed "holds" is used for the paper in the capsule higher up. Both settled on "keeps" | CLEAR, small hitch |
| Evelyn holds my charm and gives it Reiki with one hope for me: that I receive love. | 1 re-read the colon to attach the hope | CLEAR |

**How B got here.** Rounds 1–3 were a different reason ("too": the first love in the charm is given to her). It broke: round 1, "holding care" (3 of 3 stumbled); round 3, "I've already practised receiving: I said yes to Evelyn's Reiki" (2 of 3 wrote *I can't*; one said paying is spending, not receiving; one read "receiving" as the parcel arriving). Dropped after three rounds.

## Variant C — Evelyn's help, and her wish stays hers

**Why this angle:** answers the doubt a woman may have before ticking ("does Evelyn need to know what I wish?"). Her Reiki is for love; her own words stay private. Builds on tick 1, "I can keep what I write to myself".

> **☐ Yes — have Evelyn give my charm Reiki before it's packed.**
> **+$11.11** · Reiki is a Japanese practice that uses the hands. Evelyn gives my charm Reiki with one purpose: that I receive love. She never reads my wish. So I get her help to receive love, and my wish stays private. My charm ships within 2 business days either way, so the Reiki adds no wait.

54 words after the price.

**Cold read: ✅ CLEAR (round 4, the text above).** All three readers said back what she gets and why: help toward receiving love, done without anyone reading her wish. None read the charm as lacking anything. Two added that the page states the purpose but not *how* the Reiki gets there. That's the flat claim level the letters use, not a gap in the sentence.

| Sentence | What readers stumbled on | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| She never reads my wish. | 3 of 3 noticed "reads" could mean a psychic *reading* on this site; all 3 settled on the plain meaning. 1 wondered whether the capsule is opened | CLEAR, snag worth knowing |
| My charm ships within 2 business days either way, so the Reiki adds no wait. | 3 of 3 looked at the box to work out "either way"; all landed on ticked / unticked | CLEAR |

**How C got here.** Round 1 said *when* (Reiki before she writes) but not *why*, and "By then her Reiki is in it, and only I know what my wish says" got an *I can't*. Round 2 stated the help outright but "She doesn't need to know my wish to do it" sent all three looking for what "it" meant. Round 3 still had the soft "never needs to see", which two readers said doesn't support "no one but me reads my wish". It's true that she never sees it, so it's now said flat.

## Build notes

- **Wiring any variant is three files, changed together:** the copy block in `09-C3-order-bump.md`,
  `BUMP.label` / `BUMP.note` in `client/src/lib/heartCleanserBooking.ts`, and the two `toBe` strings in
  `heartCleanserBooking.test.ts` (it also checks the `09-C3` doc contains both strings word for word).
  All three variants already pass that test's banned-word regex and keep "2 business days" and
  "adds no wait", so no assertion needs loosening.
- **"Uses the hands" / "holds my charm".** Reiki is a hands-on practice by definition, so this is what
  Reiki is, not an invented step. ⚠ If whoever gives the Reiki works hands-off or at a distance, cut
  "that uses the hands" and "holds my charm" before wiring. One question to the packer settles it.
- **⚠ Overlap with U2 (existing risk, varies by variant).** U2's `HC_WHAT_RECEIVE` says Evelyn will
  "attune each stone to the love you're ready to receive before your bracelet ships". A and B say
  Evelyn puts a hope for love into the charm's pink quartz, which is close to the same act on a
  different bracelet. C doesn't put anything into the stone, so it overlaps least.
- **U1 overlap is lower in all three.** U1 sells "the charged stone". Dropping "charge" from the bump
  label keeps the two apart on the page.
- **"Adds no wait".** Several readers heard "no weight" for a moment, and every one of them corrected
  it straight away. Kept, because the live test asserts the phrase. "Adds no delay" is the fix if it
  is ever wanted (test change included).
- **Rule 2 (must not fight the product).** No variant names a limit or a missing part. The question
  "if she leaves it unticked, what does the page suggest about her charm?" was asked of every reader
  in every round. No reader, in any round, said the charm is weaker, unfinished or "doesn't work"
  without it.
- **"Evelyn" not recognised, round 1.** Every round-1 reader flagged "who is Evelyn?". That was the
  test page, not the copy: it left out the "Evelyn Cross" masthead and the letter she arrived from.
  Added from round 2, and the flag disappeared.
- **Angles considered and not written.** *Last hands on it* (Evelyn's are the last hands before
  hers): it draws attention to the charm being handled by strangers, and nobody has confirmed that
  the person who gives the Reiki also packs it. *Arrives already attuned*: "attune" is U2's word,
  and "rather than a bare stone" says the unticked charm is bare. *"Too" / receiving*: tested three
  rounds and dropped (see Variant B).
- **Banned words.** None of clearing, energy field, our conversation, the AI-tell list or "proven"
  appear in any variant. No outcome about a man or a relationship.
- **The total.** Ticked, it reads 70.11 (from the catalog, never typed). Unticked, 59.00. Unchanged
  by this proposal.
