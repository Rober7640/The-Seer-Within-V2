**2. The upsells own these jobs.**

- **U1:** guards who gets close during the wait. It names "the conversation you promised yourself you'd stop reopening".
- **U2:** the right wrist, reaching out, attraction, signs, and stones attuned before shipping.

**3. The deck's rules.**

- A bump is something she does, not something that ships (02-C3, 06-C3).
- The first line names the charm's limit, as a different job. It never names a missing part.
- The bump protects the charm (00e §4, rules 3–4).
- None of WORKFLOW rule 3's three banned V1 terms. No personal details.
- No salt, fire, water or tea (those belong to 02, 03 and 04).
- No nightly rite (00m: 05 must not add a third).
- Not 00m's banned ease-line.

**4. One fixed second line on every label.** "Sent to my inbox when I pay, not in the parcel". A buyer of a physical item who ticks a box will look for it in the gift box. That line is the refund guard.

## Ruled out, so nobody rebuilds them

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| A book of ready-written example wishes | Fights C1 statement 3, "Nobody writes it for me". Examples also get copied word for word |
| A stand-in bracelet or thread to wear during the wait | Fights E2: "I don't mean any bracelet you happen to have in your jewellery box" |
| Charging or preparing the stones herself | Says the charm arrives incomplete. U2 already attunes stones. Too close to rule 3's banned words |
| The right hand, or noticing signs | U2's job, word for word |
| A 20-minute pause before messaging him | U1's RISK beat names that exact conversation |
| Burning an old wish | 03's device, and a fire-safety claim |
| Printable wish papers | Suggests the box doesn't have enough |
| A "smaller sentence" ladder for when "too" feels untrue | Too close to #1 in different clothes. Keep it as a fallback if #1 is dropped |
| Waiting for a new moon to first wear it | Delays the first wearing, which fights "when it arrives, take a quiet moment" |

## How a chosen bump would reach her (describe, don't build)

Nothing here needs a person or an n8n job. Each step is done once, then the same for every buyer.

1. **Catalog.** Give the `heart-cleanser` row in `shared/backendOffers.ts` a `bump` with **its own product key** (e.g. `heart_cleanser_once_list`; never reuse `closed_purse` or any other). Today there is no bump, and `bump: true` is refused with `bump_unavailable`. Update `09-C1`'s "Order bump: none" line and total line to match. 09 has no chat treatment, so there is no bump turn to write.
2. **Checkout.** The bump rides as line item 2 on the same Stripe Checkout session, as on 02/03/06.
3. **Tag.** In `server/lib/backendCustomerList.ts`, 09's `bumpTag` is undefined today. It would follow the deck pattern, `be-09-bump`, on the shared bump list the other offers use. The webhook's `addBackendCustomer({ …, bumpPurchased })` applies it when she pays. Add the tag; never rename one.
4. **The send.** One AWeber Campaign, triggered by `be-09-bump`, sends one email. Its subject names the item, and its body links to the deliverable: a hosted page on theseerwithin.com, or a PDF/MP3 on S3 under `evelyn/backend-09/`. Built once.
5. **Receipt.** `09-T1` sells nothing. At most one line, "Your [bump] is in your inbox", and only if the order lookup returns the bump flag. Otherwise say nothing.
6. **Check before building.** A refund of the bump alone must not trigger the `charge.refunded` rule that cancels a `be_*` parcel. Confirm no n8n fulfilment filter matches the new key. A hosted link can be forwarded, which is acceptable at this price.
