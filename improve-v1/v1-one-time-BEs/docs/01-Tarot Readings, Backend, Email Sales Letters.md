## Lesson 1: Engagement creates sales.

Opening -> reading -> clicking -> replying -> buying.

If you can get your customers to take an action or participate in an EXPERIENCE, that creates engagement which leads to sales.

It's important that you have a STRONG customer support for this that replies fast to emails, and gets conversations going.

## Lesson 2: Email Sales Letters Straight To Checkout Typically Outperform Email Clicks To Sales Letter

Especially when you have a "superlist" of buyers.

Reason being that everyone who opens the emails sees your sales letter.

Say you have a 30% open rate, and an average of a 5% click rate.

If you link to the sales letter from the email that means only 5% of people see your actual sales letter.

But if you put the whole sales letter in the email, 30% of people see your sales letter, and 5% click to checkout.

With high purchasing intent.

I wouldn't do this in an aware B2B market.

But in mass markets...

In less "aware" markets this is extremely powerful.

Because you can play around with scarcity timers.

And you can use heavy personalisation like I did in the Tarot reading.

Whenever I launch a product via email, I'll make sure to have AT LEAST 1 email sales letter that goes straight to checkout.

## Lesson 3: The more offers you send the more you make

It was a bit of a counterintuitive lesson for me at first.

But the more different offers you send to your list, especially mass market b2c, the more you make.

Buyers buy.

And the more different, fresh, offers you put infront of them, the more they have the opportunity to spend.
