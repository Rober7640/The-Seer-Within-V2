THE SOURCE THE BUYER WILL SEE PRINTED DIRECTLY ABOVE THIS INTERPRETATION
The gallows from which he is suspended forms a Tau cross, while the figure—from the position of the legs—forms a fylfot cross. There is a nimbus about the head of the seeming martyr. It should be noted (1) that the tree of sacrifice is living wood, with leaves thereon; (2) that the face expresses deep entrancement, not suffering; (3) that the figure, as a whole, suggests life in suspension, but life and not death. It is a card of profound significance, but all the significance is veiled. It has been called falsely a card of martyrdom, a card of prudence, a card of the Great Work, a card of duty. I will say very simply on my own part that it expresses the relation, in one of its aspects, between the Divine and the Universe.
PLAIN TRADITIONAL MEANING: a wait you agreed to, paid for in time
DRAWN DETAILS AVAILABLE FOR ACCURATE REFERENCE:
  · a man hanging head-down from a gallows shaped like a T, tied by one ankle
  · the gallows is living wood, small green leaves coming out of it
  · his free leg crossed behind the tied one, making the shape of a figure four
  · his hands out of sight behind his back, elbows out to the sides
  · a ring of yellow light around his head, as wide as his shoulders
  · his face wide awake and somewhere else, no strain in it anywhere
ACCURACY FENCE: the Hanged Man is willing suspension. He tied himself there; the price is time, and what he is paid in is a changed view. Nothing moves while he hangs, and that stillness is the whole mechanism. It has no deadline — it ends when the seeing is done. ⛔ It cannot say the waiting is over, and it cannot give a date.
