I laid these last night, at my own table, with the lamp pulled down close and the rest of the house asleep.

I will tell you what I expected. With the Tower already showing, I would have bet the eleventh would be the loud room — the one I would sit up with. It went down easily and told me what it was on the second look.

It was your eighth I sat with. This whole draw runs back to one question you were asked and never answered, and the windfall in your first is standing behind it. I turned that room three times before I let it stand.

So: a good draw, and a strong one. The next two months happen in your first, your fifth and your eighth — and two months is the window every card here speaks to.

All twelve are trumps because I pulled them that way. A Majors-only twelve is the heaviest reading I do — it costs me a night, and I keep it for the draws that ask for one. Yours asked.

Now the thing you paid for. The World fell in your first house, yourself. The Lovers fell in your fifth, romance. The Tower fell in your eleventh, your circle. That is the answer the letter owed you, and each one is settled in its own room below.

You had to admit something to yourself to send for this, dear — that it had got as far as asking a stranger. Put that down now. Women who are managing perfectly well do not write to me.

And read all twelve before you decide what this says. One room will promise you ease and the next will ask you for work, and it will look as though I contradict myself. I do not. The meaning is in the arrangement — and no life is consistent either.

[1 · yourself · the World]
She is dancing naked inside a green laurel wreath, the violet cloth wound loose about her and tied to nothing — nothing held down, nothing covered for anybody's comfort.

It fell in the first house, dear. That is the door I could not see from three cards: the luck comes through you, through your own person — not through a change of place or of weather.

The first windfall is money with your name on it. A sum somebody releases or hands over, not wages. Judgment reversed in your eighth tells me it has been sitting delayed in another person's hands, awaiting their word. It comes loose.

The second is not money at all. It is being chosen out loud — an offer of time, a place, a door held open, given because it is you. The Lovers in your fifth is where that one lands, and that is why it changes love and not your bank balance.

Both arrive whole, provided you stop paying your half of everything this month.

What would you have to allow, if a thing were given to you with no price on it?

[2 · what you own · the Fool]
The sun behind him is pale and low and wide, sitting just above snow on the peaks. It is early light with no heat in it. Behind him is cold country; he has come out of a hard season and is not looking back at it.

Count what he carries. One stick, one bag, and the bag is small.

That is what you own, dear — and it is the good news of this room. Nothing of yours is sunk into something that would have to be broken up first. You can move without unwinding anything.

The money that comes loose arrives as cash in your hand, no invoice, no explanation attached to it. Then the first decision comes fast, because this card has no patience in it.

Put the first part of it into something you have never tried. Not into the ground behind you.

The white rose is held loose, stem down. He is not gripping it and he is not waving it at anybody.

[3 · what you say, and who you say it to · the Devil]
He crouches on a black half-block, wings out, one hand raised flat and the other lowered, and neither hand is touching anybody. He is presiding, dear. Not gripping.

That is your speech in this room. You keep an arrangement going with your own mouth — the pleasant version, the softened answer, the message rewritten until nobody could take offence at it. And it works. It buys you quiet, and quiet is worth something.

The Hierophant reversed in your fourth tells me where the script came from and why it will not budge. You were taught this way of speaking at home, and you have carried it long past the house it was made for.

The torch in his lowered hand burns pointed at the floor. Your heat goes downward, into the drafts you never send.

Send one of them this week. Unsoftened, to the person it was written for.

[4 · home, and what you came from · the Hierophant (reversed)]

That is what you came from, dear. Not cruelty. Form.

And the blessing in his right hand never reached you. The proper way was there to be taken and it did not happen — so you grew up outside it, and were still marked against it.

This is not news to you. You have noticed you keep house by rules nobody living is enforcing, and you decided you were being dramatic.

The two keys lie crossed on the floor, down at the foot of the pillars. Nobody handed them up.

Leave this room as it stands and you go on paying dues into a house that has stopped keeping accounts.

Keep those keys on the floor in mind. What comes next was handed down to you by nobody either — and that is exactly why it can reach you.

[5 · romance, and what delights you · the Lovers]
The sun sits above the winged figure, wider and higher than anything else drawn there. Both trees, both bodies, the grey mountain between them — all of it lit from a place neither of them owns.

It fell in your fifth. Beginning, then. Not binding.

That settles the two trees. Renewal needs something already in your hands, and the Hanged Man in your seventh is suspended, going nowhere, waiting on nobody's word. There is nothing there to revive. So it is the tree of flames — arrival.

A twin flame, dear. Someone already known to you. The third week of next month. I can date this one room because an arrival is an event, and events land on a day; the other eleven are weather.

He is mid-errand when it happens, going somewhere, not looking for you. He stops. You are used to men whose attention arrives ahead of them.

Arriving and being recognised are two separate events. Brace for the second and the first walks straight past you — so this room hands you the meeting, and wants you plain and unhurried in an ordinary hour that will not announce itself.

[6 · work, health, the daily grind · Temperance]
Yellow flowers are growing right at the lip of the pool, close by the foot that stands in the water. Nothing was planted there. They came up because something has been standing in that spot long enough to feed them.

That is your working week, dear. Not wasted, not squandered on the wrong things.

This card does not accuse you of laziness and it does not say you have mismanaged your strength. It says the opposite. Your energy has been going into a slow mixing you cannot feel while you do it — the old skill and the new one, the old body and the way you live in it now, coming into proportion.

You want proof, and proof is the one thing a season withholds. Keep the same hours this month. Do not test it.

The water crosses between the cups in a curved stream. It does not fall. It is being carried.

[7 · who you are bound to · the Hanged Man]
The ring of yellow light sits close around his head, no wider than his shoulders, and there is nobody else standing inside it with him. It lights him and it lights nothing further. The beam he hangs from is living wood, small green leaves pushing out of it above him.

That is your binding, dear. Still, and still by your own arrangement — you tied that ankle.

The Lovers in your fifth arrives into this stillness, not into an empty room.

You decided a while ago not to force it and not to end it. That was the decision.

[9 · what you believe · the High Priestess (reversed)]

That is how your knowing works, dear. It runs straight down into you, and you could not say where it starts.

Here is the belief you would not defend out loud if somebody asked you for it. That seeing a thing clearly is most of the work. That if you know, you have as good as dealt with it.

[10 · your standing · Strength (reversed)]

[11 · your circle · the Tower]
The tower is set on bare rock, and there is no door drawn on it anywhere — no way in, no way out, nothing to knock at. It was never a place people came and went from. It was a thing that stood.

It fell in your eleventh, so it is not a lover and not a catastrophe. It is your circle, and it is the second shape: somebody still here who has already started leaving.

Already begun, dear, and already visible to the others. They have seen the shortened replies too.

[12 · what is being kept from you · the Sun]

That is what has been kept from you, dear. Not a secret somebody is sitting on. The plain fact that you are already thought well of, in daylight, by people who have never told you.

Two months is when the movement begins, not when all of it is finished. Some of these rooms will have opened inside a fortnight. The sixth is slow because Temperance is slow, and a woman who reads slow as failure stops a few weeks before it comes right.

This week: the draft in your third house. Write it as it actually is, and post it.
