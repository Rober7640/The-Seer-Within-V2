THE SOURCE THE BUYER WILL SEE PRINTED DIRECTLY ABOVE THIS INTERPRETATION
I have presented Typhon in his serpent form. The four Living Creatures of Ezekiel occupy the angles of the card. The symbolic picture stands for the perpetual motion of a fluidic universe and for the flux of human life. The Sphinx is the equilibrium therein. The transliteration of Taro as Rota is inscribed on the wheel, counterchanged with the letters of the Divine Name—to shew that Providence is implied through all. But this is the Divine intention within, and the similar intention without is exemplified by the four Living Creatures. Sometimes the sphinx is represented couchant on a pedestal above, which defrauds the symbolism by stultifying the essential idea of stability amidst movement. Behind the general notion expressed in the symbol there lies the denial of chance and the fatality which is implied therein.
PLAIN TRADITIONAL MEANING: a change that comes from outside and does not ask

DRAFT
The usual objection to this card in a house of reputation is that reputation is not supposed to be luck. Standing gets built: work, record, the slow accumulation of people knowing what you are good for. To put a wheel over that looks like an insult, as though the whole ledger could be spun away or spun up by nothing.

But the picture argues the opposite of a lottery. The commentary printed above is explicit that behind this symbol lies the denial of chance, and of the fatality that chance implies. The letters spaced around the rim, two alphabets counterchanged the whole way round, are there to say that the motion is ordered — not that it is fair, not that it is a verdict, only that it is not random. Order without desert is a strange idea to hold, and it is exactly the idea this house is handing you. Something in your position moves because of a decision or an event outside your reach. It does not consult you first, and it does not arrive as a reward.

Notice that the wheel hangs in open sky with nothing under it. There is no axle in a wall, no crank, no hand at the rim. Whatever moves your standing is not being worked by a person you could go and speak to, and it is not being withheld by one either. That single detail settles a whole category of anxiety: this is not a room about someone's opinion of you hardening or softening in private.

The turn changes your circumstances. It does not change your character, and it does not amend your record. That distinction is where the practical reading lives. If a change in position arrives and you read it as a judgment — the world has finally seen me, or the world has decided against me — you will have misread an event as an assessment. Circumstance moved. What you are good at is where it was.

House six in this spread holds Justice, the week weighed by what it shows rather than what it cost. That matters here, because it means you have one room governed by the record and one governed by the turn, and they run on different clocks. The record accrues by increments and can be checked. The turn does not wait for the record to agree with it. If your position shifts before the ledger looks as though it should, the ledger is not lying; the two are simply not the same instrument.

Visibility is the thing this house asks about, so here is what to watch. A change of standing sometimes announces itself in a title or a public placement, and that kind is hard to miss. The other kind is quieter and easier to overlook: things begin arriving at you first that formerly reached you second-hand. You are included by default in something you previously had to be added to. A question that used to travel around you now stops with you. None of that requires anyone to tell you what they think of you, and you do not need to ask. You only need to notice where things route.

So the tension in this room — earned or given — resolves without splitting the difference. The movement is given, and it is not reversible, and it will not have been arranged by you. What you actually know how to do earns nothing here. It simply decides what still stands once the position has changed under you.
