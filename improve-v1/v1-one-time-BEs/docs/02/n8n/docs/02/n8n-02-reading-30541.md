I laid your twelve last night, late, the lamp on and the house asleep.

Here is what I expected, and what happened. With the Tower already in your first house, I was certain your own room would be the loudest thing on the table. It was not. It was the seventh — who you are bound to. Your first house and your eleventh both refused to finish without it, and I read the rest from there.

It is a good draw, dear, with one genuinely hard room in it. The next stretch of your life happens in the first, the seventh and the eleventh. The twelfth is where you get paid. Two months is the window on all of it.

All twelve are trumps because I pulled them that way for you. A Majors-only twelve is the heaviest night's work I do, and I keep it for the women whose first draw asks for it. Yours asked.

Your three, and their houses. The Tower in the first, which is you. The Lovers in the eleventh, your circle. The World in the twelfth, what is being kept from you. That is the answer the letter owed you. Each is taken in its own room below.

You had to admit you could not ask anyone in your own life about this. That is not a fault in you. It is what comes of being the woman other people bring their trouble to.

Read all twelve before you decide what they say. Two rooms will look as though they argue — one card handing you ease, the next asking you for work. The sense is in the arrangement, and no woman's life is consistent either.
