## Five most consequential faults, ranked

### 1. House 12 manufactures evidence for its required prediction

**Evidence:** “with a particular man, attention and care and good conversation no longer change the temperature for longer than an evening” and “Reassurance is accepted and does not hold” (p. 29). Later: “You read movement away, and movement away is genuinely there” (p. 30).

**Why it matters:** Choosing a present person leaving, and specifying the stage, are required predictive commitments. Describing the buyer's repeated conversations, emotional efforts and experience of failed reassurance as facts is extra supporting biography. The reading calls this “the part you can check” before the reader has recognized any such events. It then gives an otherwise useful explanation that ambiguous replies fit many inner states. The invented observations make that lesson internally unstable: the reader is warned against constructing a story while being handed one as her own experience.

**Fix mechanism:** Preserve the departure branch, closeness/stage and cross-card interpretation. Separate those predictions from customer-provided observations, which this PDF does not establish. Present potential signs as recognizable examples rather than a reported history, and keep the distinction between signal and explanation consistent across the opening of the house, its analysis and the close. No replacement prose is needed to identify the change.

### 2. House 5 adds one-chance pressure to a legitimate timed answer

**Evidence:** “A recognition of this kind lasts about the length of one conversation. It does not repeat” and “then the summons can sound in a room you are not in” (p. 15). The prior justification is “I can be that narrow because a meeting is an occasion with a day attached” (p. 14).

**Why it matters:** The third-week prediction, known man's identity type, activity and manner satisfy the genre contract. None requires an opportunity that vanishes forever after one conversation. Combined with the account of surrendered hours, the addition makes missing the event feel like the buyer's failure to reclaim her evenings. Saying an event is countable also does not explain why the chosen week is warranted; it supplies an unnecessary appearance of proof.

**Fix mechanism:** Retain the required relative week and recognition details. Remove added nonrepeatability and the causal implication that her scheduling can squander the promise. Let the card interpretation carry the timed product answer without the pseudo-justification that all occasions can therefore be dated precisely. Keep the private reflection on hours proportionate and free from a loss threat.

### 3. Reflective distinctions become overbroad instructions about real choices

**Evidence:** “interruption is the only thing here that genuinely undoes work. Consenting to weeks that show you nothing is the cost” (House 6, p. 17). House 7 asks for “giving to a living thing across the stretch where it shows you nothing” (p. 19). House 10's bold takeaway says “only recorded decisions report your actual position” (p. 25).

**Why it matters:** Observation is nominally the requested action, but the rhetoric favors continuing a routine or giving to an unresponsive bond without fresh evidence. That consequence exceeds a metaphor about blending or ripening. The Empress house does not earn its exclusion of a finished tie through an observable distinction. The Justice takeaway similarly converts one useful evidence source into an exclusive measure of standing. Bold emphasis strengthens the overstatement. The gift's later caution against consequential decisions does not make these passages consistent.

**Fix mechanism:** Keep the card's patience and evidence themes while removing exclusivity and the implication that checking or changing course itself spoils progress. Define the scope of each reflective test and make actual observations matter to its application. Bold takeaways should preserve those limits. Do not add a generic disclaimer; correct the local reasoning and instruction.

### 4. Repeated teaching structures make the reader do too much interpretive labor

**Evidence:** “There is speech that transmits” / “There is speech that asks” / “There is speech that commits” (p. 10); “There are several categories, and you are better placed than I am to fill them” (p. 21); “There are at least three ways a working belief can be held” (p. 23). The analogy-management formula also repeats: “The comparison has a limit worth stating” (p. 7) and “the comparison has a limit worth marking” (p. 23).

**Why it matters:** The first taxonomy is interesting; their accumulation becomes predictable. Many houses explain the image, distinguish two or more abstract categories, explain what the interpretation is not, and finish with a small private choice and its price. This is not exact copy repetition, but it is an audible compositional template. The torch analogy on p. 23 is introduced, limited and then corrected by the stationary Hermit, spending attention to restore the original image's meaning. Many bold takeaways are useful, but cannot compensate for the distance to the relevant personal question.

**Fix mechanism:** Give each house one principal interpretive problem. Retain only card details and distinctions necessary to solve it. Remove analogies that require lengthy correction, redundant method announcements and category lists that do not affect the conclusion. Vary house movement through substance, not cosmetic phrasing, while preserving the promised answers and real cross-card reasoning.

### 5. The PDF remains visibly unfinished in typography and pagination

**Evidence:** Literal asterisks appear around “*who*” (p. 18), “*who is withholding*” and “*what kind of thing gets held*” (p. 21), and “*where I am, you also may be*” (p. 23). Page 6 starts with the isolated final line “means the knot is worth looking at before you decide to keep it tied.” Page 8 holds only the end of House 2 and leaves most of the page empty; p. 28 similarly contains only House 11's short ending. House 5's interpretation starts on p. 14 after its entire card/quotation opening on p. 13.

**Why it matters:** The asterisks look like unprocessed source markup. Isolated carryovers and unevenly filled pages make a paid report feel automatically flowed rather than finished. They also exaggerate the reading's length and disconnect the art/quotation from the analysis it supports.

**Fix mechanism:** Render inline emphasis consistently, apply widow/orphan controls, and rebalance house openings and final paragraphs after the prose edit. Inspect all pages again at reading size. Preserve the generous margins and worksheet writing space; the problem is unintended overflow, not whitespace in itself.
