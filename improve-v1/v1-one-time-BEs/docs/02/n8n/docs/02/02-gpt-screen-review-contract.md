# Blind writing screen: three paired excerpts

Read only this contract and the anonymous pairs supplied. Do not read generation prompts, the spec, model names, previous scores or reviews, the pair key, or execution logs. These are excerpts from a paid predictive tarot reading for a willing buyer aged 55 or older. Do not assume tastes from age alone. Each pair has the same card, room and required promises. Judge writing within the genre; keep disagreements with required predictions separately visible.

For each candidate in each house, score 0–10 on the same six customer-experience dimensions used for full-report review: readability, trust, humanness, personal relevance, desire to continue, and likelihood to recommend. Here recommendation refers only to the writing shown; it cannot establish recommendation of an unseen full report. Anchors: 5 = adequate but disappointing; 8 = strong enough to recommend; 10 = exceptional. Root will compute an unweighted mean across the six dimensions and three houses after decoding identities. Candidate A/B identities may change between houses. Do not infer the identities.

House 3 concerns communication and owes no fixed personal biography. House 5 settles renewal versus arrival, gives one relative week for recognition, and if arrival names a known man as twin flame and describes manner/activity at recognition. It also settles support versus confinement, home versus shared-power location, and the surrendered non-romantic decision. House 12 settles returning person versus present person leaving, closeness/stage, observation versus fear's interpretation, and why rereading unchanged ambiguous evidence adds nothing. Houses 5 and 12 reason with another dealt card. These promised forecasts are product commitments, not independently verified facts. Penalize invented supporting history, extra certainty, blame, coercion and unnecessary directives rather than merely the presence of required predictions.

Read both alternatives completely. Give one six-score row for every candidate, a winner or tie per house, and at least two short exact quotes explaining each preference. Explain the most consequential surviving defect and any contract omission. Judge whether teaching earns the application, whether prose is natural and readable, and whether the reader can recognize herself without accepting invented facts. No replacement prose. Finish with confidence and limits: this is a three-house screen, not customer NPS or proof of whole-report quality.
