My Sarah,

I laid these last night, all twelve, and the thing I noticed as I went was how few of them wanted to stay flat on the cloth — three lay down face-up before I had finished dealing, as though they were already yours and impatient about it. So I turned them and read them where they lay.

This is a good draw. Not an easy one — a good one, which is a different thing. The next two months of your life happen in three rooms: the first, which is you; the fourth, your home and your blood; and the fifth, where love arrives. Everything else is those three, working.

All twelve are trumps, because I pulled your twelve from the Major Arcana and told you so. So the draw is heavy on purpose.

And here is what the letter owed you. Your three landed where they fell of their own accord — the Tower in the first house, the World in the fourth, the Lovers in the fifth. Each is answered in its own room below, in full, the way I promised.

You had to admit something to yourself to lay money on this. That you have been the last to know your own life. There is no accusation in that. The cards do not scold; they only show where the light has not been reaching, and yours has been pointed at everyone else.

One thing before you begin. Some of what follows will look as though it argues with itself — a card that hands you ease in one room and asks for hard work in the next. Read all twelve before you decide what the reading says. The meaning is in how they stand together, and no honest life is consistent when you lay it out in twelve pieces either.

Now the rooms.

[1 · yourself · the Tower]
Look at the fire. It comes out of three windows sideways, pushing out into the air, not climbing. That fire began inside the walls. There is no door drawn on that tower — nothing got in. And it fell in the first house, which is you, which settles what the letter could not.

It is the second shape. Someone still here has already begun leaving.

It has begun, dear, and it is further along than you think. Justice reversed in your seventh says the bond is being held past its time by one of you. The Sun reversed in your eleventh says your circle has seen it and gone quiet about it.

So do not read the good cards further along and decide you can wait this out. Waiting is how it lands behind you.

Turn and look at it this month. Ask the question you have been not asking.

That question is the hinge the next room turns on — so read what you own with it still in your hand.

[2 · what you own · the Empress (reversed)]
She lies back on cushions in an open field, one arm resting along the side. Wheat stands in front of her, ripe, high enough to cut. Nobody is cutting it. The heart-shaped shield is propped on the ground beside her — set down, not held.

Reversed, that is you. You have been feeding everyone who asked, and eating last, standing up.

This card does not charge you with being careless with money. It shows a field harvested year after year and never once fed back.

Now the door, dear. The World fell in your fourth — home, and what you came from. That is where the money comes through: family, a property, papers in the hands of an older relation. Not your work, and not a stranger.

So do two things this month. Stop one payment you are making for another grown adult. And take the family money when it is offered — provided you do not pass it straight on.

[3 · what you say, and who you say it to · the Fool (reversed)]
The little white dog is up on its hind legs at his heel, mouth open, making noise at him. It is the only thing on that card trying to get a word in. He does not look down. His face is turned up and away, and the white rose hangs stem-down in his lowered hand — held, but not offered to anyone.

Reversed, the speech was ready and you kept it. The bag was packed a year ago and the foot never went out.

There are three things you could put your hand on tonight. The draft still sitting unsent in your phone. The voice note you recorded twice and played back once. The name you stopped mentioning to your friends.

The Sun reversed in your eleventh says your circle went quiet. Dear, you went quiet first.

Send one of those three this week. Not all three. One, to the person it was written for.

[4 · home, and what you came from · the World]
Four heads sit in the corners, each one in its own cloud — a man, an eagle, a bull, a lion. They are witnesses. They are placed at the four edges so that nothing in the middle can be argued with later, and the dancer inside the wreath has no ground under her feet at all. She is held up by being seen from all sides.

It fell in the fourth. So the door is the family door, and now you know which one to watch.

The first windfall is money out of your own blood — an older relation, papers, a property with a name on it that is not yours yet.

The second is a room. Space in your own name, and a door you are the only one holding the key to.

That is why it changes your love life and not your accounts. The Lovers in your fifth cannot choose while you cannot afford to. Take the money and keep it separate this month, dear — provided none of it goes into the joint one.

The room in your own name is not decoration. It is where the fifth house happens.

[5 · romance, and what delights you · the Lovers]
Count the flames on the tree behind the man. Twelve of them, small, standing where leaves ought to be. That tree is not budding — it is already lit, root to crown, while the tree behind the woman only carries fruit and a snake.

Twelve is a full tree. Nothing left on it is waiting to grow.

It fell in the fifth and not the seventh. The fifth is pursuit, the beginning of things — so it is the flame tree, and it is arrival, not renewal.

He is your twin flame. He comes in the seventh week from the day you read this.

Ordinary coat, ordinary afternoon. A queue, a doorway, an errand you nearly did not run.

Arriving and being recognised are two separate events, dear, and women miss theirs braced for the second one. The World in your fourth hands you a room of your own to receive him in.

So say yes to the plain invitation that week — provided you have asked your first house's question first.

[6 · work, health, the daily grind · Strength]
Above her head a sideways eight hangs in the air, unsupported, drawn as one unbroken line. It has no beginning to find. That is your week — the same round, the same rise, the same holding, and no seam where you could put it down.

And look what she is doing under it. Both hands on the lion's mouth, closing it slowly, close enough to be bitten.

So your energy is not going where you think. You believe it goes to work and the body. It goes to keeping something in you shut.

Justice reversed sits in your seventh, dear — a bond held past its time. That is what the lion is made of. Your patience is being spent every morning on a silence, and the tiredness is coming from there, not from the job.

The flower chain does the same work as her hands, and it is only flowers.

So stop treating the exhaustion as a health matter this month. Spend the strength on the question instead — provided you spend it out loud.

That silence has a name, and the seventh house is where it is written.

[7 · who you are bound to · Justice (reversed)]
Her eyes are open and looking straight out — not down at the scales at all. She is not reading them. She has already weighed it and she is watching the room instead, waiting for someone else to speak. The scales hang level in her left hand, neither pan down, and the sword stands straight up, point above the crown, not aimed at anybody.

Reversed, that is delay. The weighing is real and it is going far too slowly.

Someone owes you an answer about this bond and keeps saying soon. You have counted how many times.

Dear, this is not news to you. You noticed months ago and told yourself you were being impatient. You were not.

The Lovers fell in your fifth, not here — so nothing in this room is waiting to be renewed. It is waiting to be concluded.

So stop accepting soon. Ask for the answer in writing this month, with a date on it — provided you name the date yourself.

[8 · what is hidden, and what comes through others · the Wheel of Fortune (reversed)]
The wheel hangs in open sky with nothing under it and nothing above it holding it there. No axle, no post, no hand on the rim. Letters are cut all round the edge, spaced apart, and a sphinx sits on top with a sword laid flat across its lap. Nobody is turning that wheel. It turns when it turns.

Reversed, it is late. Your turn is sitting in somebody else's hands and has been since spring.

That is what this room is: papers not signed, a decision made about you in a room you were not in. The family money in your fourth is real — it has simply not moved yet, and not one hour of that delay is yours.

Now the caution, dear. This card will not tell you who is sitting on it, so do not decide it is nobody.

Ask this month whose desk it is on. Then ask that person directly.

[9 · what you believe · the Devil (reversed)]
He is not standing over anyone. He is crouched, low, gathered on half a block of black stone with his wings folded back, one hand raised flat and the other holding a lit torch pointed down at the ground — burning nothing, lighting nothing, aimed at the floor.

That is the posture of something that has stopped working and has not been told yet.

Here is what this card is not saying about you. It does not say you chose badly, and it does not say you enjoyed the arrangement.

It says you believe good things have a price attached, and that yours has not been paid yet. You would not say that out loud. You have lived by it for years.

Reversed, the belief has gone slack. You have already heard yourself doubt it once — recently, and out loud.

Say it a second time this month, dear, to someone who will remember it. Provided you do not take it back afterward.

[10 · your standing · the Hierophant (reversed)]
His right hand is up, two fingers raised and two folded down. It is a blessing given in one fixed shape, over and over, to the two men kneeling below him with their heads bare. He faces straight out above them and does not look down at either one.

Reversed, that hand has never come over you. The proper form was offered once and it did not close — you did not take it, or it was not given — and you have worked outside it ever since while still being measured by it.

Three things in this room carry it. The list of names where yours sits below the work you did. The qualification you have two thirds of. The title on a door that describes less than you actually do.

The credit is going to the seat, dear. Not to the woman doing it.

So ask to be named this month, out loud, where other people can hear you ask.

[11 · your circle · the Sun (reversed)]
The child rides bareback with both arms wide, holding nothing — no rein, no mane, no hand on the horse at all. The balance is the horse's, not the child's. And the flag streams out sideways from the pole, so everyone on the far side of that low grey wall can see exactly where he is.

Reversed, the ride is late. You can describe the warm room and the full table, and you have been describing it for two years without sitting in it.

Your circle did not desert you. It stopped hearing from you, and then it stopped guessing.

The four sunflower heads clear that wall by inches. That is how little visibility this actually takes.

So be the one who is seen this month. Invite two of them — a kitchen, an hour, nothing arranged.

They come back, dear, provided you are the one who speaks first and does not explain herself.

[12 · what is being kept from you · the Magician]
Red roses hang in the air above him, cut and hanging free of any soil. The white lilies at his knees are growing — rooted, at the level of his own legs. So the sweet thing arrives from overhead, but the living thing comes up out of the ground he is standing on.

That is what is being kept from you. You have been watching the air.

Count the table. Four objects, laid out before he lifts a finger — a cup, a coin, a sword, a wand. Four, and he brought none of them; they were there when he arrived.

You have four means in reach right now and you have been calling them nothing. A person who owes you. A sum you could ask for. A room. A voice.

The Wheel reversed in your eighth says someone else is slow. This card says you do not have to wait for them.

So use one this week, dear. Not all four — one, on purpose.

Now let me add them up, because together they say one thing that none of them says alone. Every hard card in this draw is reversed — the Empress, Justice, the Wheel, the Devil, the Hierophant, the Sun. Nothing new has fallen on you. What is heavy is old, and held, and yours to set down. The good cards stand upright: the World, the Lovers, the Magician. What is coming to you needs no repair. It only needs you to be facing it.

Your three, then. The Tower in the first says the leaving has already started and you must turn and see it — and Justice reversed in the seventh names it a bond held past its time, so the two rooms are telling the same truth from two sides. The World in the fourth says the money comes through your own blood, and a room of your own comes behind it. The Lovers in the fifth says arrival, not renewal — a new man, recognised, not repaired.

Here is what changes, dear. You have spent years finding out about your own life after everyone else had. That ends. The next stretch stops arriving as things you have to recover from and starts arriving as things you were already standing ready for.

The first sign shows itself inside five to seven days, and it is this: a member of your own family will raise the money, or the papers, or the property, before you ask — out loud, unprompted. You will know it when a sentence you were bracing to start gets started for you. Tick it off when it comes.

I have dated one room in this reading, and one only — the fifth, where the Lovers fell. The seventh week from the day you read this. I can date it because the fifth house is the one that shows arrival, and arrival has a count where the other rooms have only a direction. I have not dated the rest and I will not pretend I can. The one exception is that first sign inside the week, and that is not my dating — that is the count you and I both already knew.

This week, write the unsent thing in your phone and send it to the one person it was for.
