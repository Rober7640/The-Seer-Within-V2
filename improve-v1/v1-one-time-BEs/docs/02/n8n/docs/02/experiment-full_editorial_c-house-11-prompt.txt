THE SOURCE THE BUYER WILL SEE PRINTED DIRECTLY ABOVE THIS INTERPRETATION
A winged angel, with the sign of the sun upon his forehead and on his breast the square and triangle of the septenary. I speak of him in the masculine sense, but the figure is neither male nor female. It is held to be pouring the essences of life from chalice to chalice. It has one foot upon the earth and one upon waters. A direct path goes up to certain heights on the verge of the horizon, and above there is a great light, through which a crown is seen vaguely. Hereof is some part of the Secret of Eternal Life. It is called Temperance fantastically, because, when the rule of it obtains in our consciousness, it tempers, combines and harmonises the psychic and material natures.
PLAIN TRADITIONAL MEANING: two things becoming one, at the speed of a season

DRAFT
Temperance is usually read here as moderation — keep the peace, don't overcommit, be the reasonable one among difficult friends. That reading mistakes a temperament for a process. The card is not about restraint at all; it is about combination. Two things that were separate become one usable thing, and they do it by degrees, in proportions that cannot be hurried.

In the eleventh house that means your circle is currently mixing. Not expanding by acquisition, not contracting by loss — mixing. Two parts of it that have existed side by side without much traffic between them are beginning to have traffic. Someone from one context appears in another. A person you associate with one setting turns up connected, by ordinary means, to a person you associate with a different setting. The pattern is lateral. People are meeting each other rather than meeting you.

The tension worth naming is that this house is where a person usually looks for arrival — the new friend, the invitation, the group forming around something. Temperance does supply arrival, but sideways and through the existing structure, which is why it rarely registers as an event. The stream in the picture crosses the gap between the two cups without falling straight down; the transfer happens, but not by the obvious mechanism. That is the shape of it. Connections in your circle are being made through intermediaries and shared occasions rather than through anything that looks like an introduction.

The other half of the pattern is quiet. When a circle is blending, some of its parts go still — not everyone, and not for a reason you would be told. A person who was frequent becomes occasional; a thread of conversation thins without ending. Under this card, silence is a proportion rather than a verdict. Two liquids mixing means one is temporarily displaced by the other, and displacement looks, from inside, exactly like withdrawal. I would not read anyone's motive from a lull right now. I would read tempo.

Waite says the figure tempers, combines and harmonises what was previously two natures; he does not say it announces anything. The work here is invisible while it happens and legible only when you look back at it. So the honest description of your social life at present is that something is being made of it, slowly, while your attention is on other matters entirely, and that the finished shape will be recognisable before it was ever visible.

What follows from that is small and entirely reversible. Stop treating your circle as a set of separate lists. If you have been keeping people mentally sorted by where you know them from, let one of those partitions go soft — accept the overlap when it presents itself rather than managing it, and notice, without arranging anything, which of your people already know each other. That is all. You are not required to host, introduce, or accelerate. If you decide in a month that you preferred the compartments, you can simply resume them; nothing has been spent.

The verdict for this house is that your circle is consolidating rather than turning over, and the consolidation is real. Two portions of it are becoming one, at the pace such things take, and no part of that requires your intervention to complete.
