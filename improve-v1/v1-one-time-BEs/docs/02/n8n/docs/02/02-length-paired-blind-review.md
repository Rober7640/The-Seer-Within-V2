**Reading checkpoints.** At the opening, A: maybe continue; B: yes. Both create discovery rather than spoil the answers. “You are not required to accept every illustration as biography” (both p. 3) earns confidence. A’s opening catalogue of “visible fact, inherited form, private knowledge, public measure, and shifting circumstance” (p. 3) first weakens attention; it asks the reader to absorb a method before experiencing its usefulness. B’s first weakening comes in House 2’s “The comparison ends there” (p. 7): explanation begins policing itself. After House 3: probably continue with both; B’s communication teaching is easier to retain. After House 8: probably continue with A, definitely with B, because the second windfall connects clearly to the romantic question. After House 10: probably with both; repetition has accumulated. Completing House 12 and the gift confirms B’s advantage, while improving my trust in both through the permission to find no pattern. It does not lift either to release quality.

**Promise coverage, assessed separately.** No required answer is missing in either. House 2 gives money through public recognition and replenishment of what is kept/received (A pp. 7–8; B p. 7). House 5 chooses arrival of an already-known twin flame, the third week, composed/direct manner and practical responsibility, support located in home/House 4, and the surrendered choice of daily proportion in House 6; the Wheel supplies reasoning (A pp. 15–16; B pp. 13–14). B omits the mechanical phrase “It chooses the arrival branch” but the answer remains explicit. House 8 gives news through a discreet woman and its romantic consequence (A p. 22; B p. 20). House 12 identifies a present person leaving, an underway stage, observation versus fear, the information limits of repeated interpretation, and High Priestess reasoning (A pp. 31–32; B pp. 27–29). The five-to-seven-day first sign remains (A p. 33; B p. 29). The cover date is administrative, not an additional calendar prediction. Required forecasts themselves receive no penalty merely for being predictions.

**Two strengths.** First, teaching usually earns application: “A request states a need without pretending it is a command” (A p. 10; B p. 9) makes the Magician’s tools useful in actual exchanges. Second, the gift protects independent judgment: “The ledger does not owe this reading a result” (A p. 35; B p. 31). The printable pages make that principle usable.

**Five consequential defects and mechanisms.**

1. **Repeated interpretive instruction.** “Distinguish visible merit from approval that cannot be verified” (A p. 27; B p. 24) is useful alone, but joins many similar bold distinctions. By Houses 9–11, observation-versus-story feels like the answer to almost every room. Give each house one distinct application and remove repetitions that add no new reasoning. B reduces this burden, not eliminates it.

2. **Expository self-consciousness.** “The printed image makes this unusually exact” (A p. 18; B p. 16) announces precision instead of letting the explanation demonstrate it. Repeated source/method announcements and contrast formulas reduce naturalness. Retain the substantive image-to-life connection while reducing these signposts.

3. **Overextended inference around a required forecast.** “A delayed warmth, a changed tone, an absence of clarity” may establish altered connection (A p. 31; B p. 28), but these examples are weak evidence of departure by themselves. Preserve the promised departure answer; distinguish its tarot basis from what ambiguous everyday signs independently establish.

4. **Overbroad practice instruction.** “Above all don't act on it” (A p. 34; B p. 30) covers observations including offers and refusals. It could discourage an ordinary timely response. Scope the restriction to acting on speculative interpretations; later safeguards do not fully repair the initial breadth.

5. **Uneven pacing and transitions.** A’s isolated recap “So the central tension in this house” (p. 8) occupies a sparse continuation page. B avoids many such tails, but its House 5 conclusion, “the day cannot be allowed to silence every summons that might renew it” (p. 14), leaves a sparse page and a somewhat abrupt endpoint. Rebalance pagination and preserve only bridges that help the next room.

B loses A’s explicit House 5/7 distinction (A p. 16), a useful orientation, but B’s opening House 5 definition and House 7 teaching still supply it. No essential explanation disappears. Rendered layouts show consistent cards, quotation panels, readable hierarchy, and no visible clipping; B more consistently starts houses cleanly. Long dense prose pages remain in both.

**Decision: revise both.** B is the preferred basis. A friend-facing verdict would be: thoughtful and specific in its promises, but more repetitive and instructional than intimate. Best purchase reason: connected answers with useful discernment tools. Strongest refund reason: too much general counsel around the personal answers. Confidence is moderate: one simulated editorial comparison, not customer testing, NPS, demographic prediction, draw verification, or a statistically reliable effect estimate.
