## The rule this adds

The Soul Urge picks **one 12-beat outline**. Every card section keeps its fixed job and its flat
answer (those come from the numbers, 11-P1 §5). The outline adds **what that job means for this
one thing**. The card's only work is to give one picture for that beat.

- Karmic debt no longer names what they took (parked 2026-11-03). Soul Urge does, for every buyer.
- Numerology stays hidden from her. The only stated reason stays: "Your birth name and date set how I lay your twelve cards."
- These are notes for the writer, not sentences. ⛔ Nothing in this file is quoted into the prompt
  (quoted lines get copied word for word: memory `prompt-examples-become-templates`).

## Soul Urge 9 — softness

**The one idea:** softness is the part of her that lets things *reach* her and lets her *show*. It runs
both ways: care coming in, warmth and need going out. They made it cost. She shut it both ways and
called that "strong". It comes back when she reads the admission. The seven days open it a crack at a time.

**The row (already approved, 11-P1 §4):** took = your softness · cover word = strong · regret =
teaching her that going soft costs her · unsaid = "You weren't weak. I made it expensive." · do = let
one thing show to someone safe.
