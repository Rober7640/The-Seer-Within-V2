## Soul Urge 2 — trust

**The one idea:** trust is the part of her that takes people at their word and lets weight rest on them. It runs
both ways: words coming in are believed as given, and things going out (a job, a secret, a plan) are handed over
without watching what's done with them. They let her go on believing them while they weren't true, so believing
itself came to look like the mistake. She stopped both ways and called it "careful". It comes back when she reads
the admission that believing was right and the fault was in what she was told. The seven days let one small thing
rest on someone else, unchecked.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "2", used as written):** took = your trust · cover word = careful ·
regret = letting her go on believing them · unsaid = "You were right to believe me, and I was the one who wasn't
true." · do = Let one person do one small thing a day, and don't check it.

**Card 5's one excuse (written in the report):** "She believed what she wanted to believe." It puts the fault in her
believing, which is exactly what this Soul Urge's story turns on. The letter echoes it: "I told myself you believed
what you wanted to believe."
