## Soul Urge 1 — nerve

**The one idea:** nerve is the part of her that goes ahead without asking anyone's leave, and the part that
holds its ground when pushed. It runs both ways: going first, and not backing down. They made standing up
cost her until she quit, then took her backing down as agreement. She now talks herself out of things before
anyone has to, and calls that "sensible". Waiting for them to admit it is the last thing she's asking their
leave for; giving up that wait is the first act of nerve back. The seven days hand it small, safe tries.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "1", as written):** took = your nerve · cover word = sensible ·
regret = they got her to back down, and liked it · unsaid = "You could have stood up to me, and I made sure
you didn't." · do = one thing a day she'd normally talk herself out of, or ask permission for.

**Card 5's one excuse:** "She went along with it." Fits this story: from the outside, giving way looks like
agreeing. The letter echoes it: "I told myself you went along with it."
