# 11-P6: How buyers describe an ex, in their own words

*2026-11-04. A read-only pull from the live V1 database. The database refused a test write, as it should. The rows are 3,789 PAID V1 buyers whose topic was lost love, betrayal or reunion. Fields used: concern, deeper response, how she'd feel, block, vision. No names or emails are kept here. The raw rows are in the session scratchpad only.*

## What we found
1. **The buyers never talk about qualities.** They talk about him: does he love me, will he come back, why did he do it, is there someone else. Spark, freedom, softness, judgment and nerve are words a writer chooses. "Spark" turns up in 0.5% of buyers, "lost myself" in 1.3%, and "freedom" (in any sense) in 4%.
2. **Our premise does show up, in their words.** Buyers say "a part of me is missing," "whole again," "weight lifted off my heart" and "I'd feel lighter." So "they still have a piece of you" is a frame they recognize. The piece needs one of their names for it, not ours.
3. **What they want, in the order they say it:** heart, then happy, then answers and the why, then peace, then feeling safe, then moving on, then trust, then whole, then not alone.

In "how would you feel if it were resolved," the top phrases are **my heart** (237), **my life** (108), **at peace** (64), **move on** (47), **my mind** (39) and **my chest** (31).

## Lines that show the shape (short and anonymous)
- "I feel like a part of me is missing. I think I'd feel more whole."
- "I'm tired of my life being on hold."
- "I feel like so much weight would be lifted off my heart."
- "My life would be worth living."
- "I just want to move on and be happy. I have been in agony over him for too long."
- "I would at least know that I have to move forward."
- "Everything, my whole body relaxes and releases all of my anxiety."

## SU 5, proposed from this (nothing written yet)
- **Anchor:** "life on hold" / stuck / moving on (10.6%, and the 5 is the number of change).
- **What they took:** the part of you that moves forward.
- **Cover phrase:** "I've moved on." She says it and means it. She's a little proud of it, and nobody argues with it.
- **Big idea:** *You moved on from them. Your life didn't move with you.*
- **Unsaid:** "You were never meant to stand still, and I left you waiting."
- **The one thing:** once a day, change one thing in a week that's run the same way since them.
