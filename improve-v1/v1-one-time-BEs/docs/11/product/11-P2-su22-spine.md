## Soul Urge 22 — plans

**The one idea:** plans are the part of her that counts on a later. She puts something down now for a day she can't see yet, and she builds on the people in it. It runs both ways: she leans her weight on what's coming, and she lets other people lean on it with her. They let her build on them when they knew it wouldn't hold. Now she builds on nothing past next week and calls that "one day at a time", and she's a little proud of how easy she is to please. It comes back when she reads them say they knew. The seven days put one thing a little further out each day.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "22"):** took = your plans · cover word = one day at a time · regret = letting her plan on them when they knew better · unsaid = "I let you build on me, and I knew it wouldn't hold." · do = each day, put one thing on the calendar further off than she'd normally dare.

**Her life, now (the everyday details, cards 2, 5 and 8 mostly):** "we'll see" when a friend floats a trip for next spring · the calendar has the dentist and other people's birthdays on it and nothing of hers · things written in pencil · "let me see how I feel that day" · nobody's ever been let down by her, because she never says yes that far out (the part she's proud of) · the friends who quit asking her in on the summer week. What they did stays general: it suited them, and they let it go on.

**Card 5's one excuse:** "I never promised her anything." It held because it was true on paper: she made the plans, they nodded along. She backs it up by saying she got ahead of herself. She still does their work: she says "no promises" before anybody can ask her for one. Letter: "I told myself I never promised you anything."
