## Soul Urge 7 — judgment

**The one idea:** her judgment is the part of her that sees what's in front of her and believes it, then decides
and stays decided. It runs both ways: trusting what she sees (people, things, a bad feeling), and trusting what
she chooses (not going back on it). They told her she was wrong when she was right, often enough that she
stopped going by her own eyes and started checking everything with somebody else first. She calls that "a second
opinion". Waiting for them to admit she was right is the biggest second opinion she's ever asked for; giving up
that wait is her first call made alone, so it's already back. The seven days hand her small calls, made alone, kept.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "7", as written):** took = your faith in your own judgment · cover word =
a second opinion · regret = telling her she was wrong when she was right · unsaid = "You saw it, you were right,
and I told you that you weren't." · do = one small decision a day she asks nobody about and doesn't go back on.

**Card 5's one excuse:** "She was imagining things." Fits this story: it's the line that turns what she saw into
something in her head. It held because she'd heard "you're wrong" so often it sounded true even to her; she backed
it by saying it first ("I'm probably imagining it, but…"). The letter echoes it: "I told myself you were imagining things."
