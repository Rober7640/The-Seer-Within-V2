## Soul Urge 3 — the laugh

**The one idea:** her laugh is the part of her that lets things *tickle* her and lets the delight *out*
before she's checked the room. It runs both ways: the funny thing gets in (she's easy to set off), and the
laugh, the silly remark, the goofy voice comes out unedited. They made it cost: the look, the sigh, being
called silly in front of people. So she put an editor on the door. Now she checks first and lets out the
grown-up version: a smile, a polite laugh at the right moment, a clever line. She calls that "grown up". It
comes back when she reads the admission. The seven days let one silly thing out at a time.

⚠ **Her Life Path 3 line (card 7) is "makes a joke of it".** So the report never says she stopped being funny.
The clever, guarded joke and the real, unplanned laugh are two different things. What they took is the second.
Card 2 says so in plain words; card 7 and the letter then read right for the LP 3 woman too.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "3", as written):** took = your laugh · cover word = grown up ·
regret = making her feel silly for it · unsaid = "You were never too much. I liked you smaller." ·
do = Once a day, say the silly thing out loud to someone before she edits it.

**Card 5's one excuse:** "I was only teasing." It held because objecting made *her* the one who can't take a
joke. She backed it up by laughing along and calling herself silly first. She still does their work: "sorry,
that's dumb", "ignore me", checking who's listening before she laughs. Letter: "I told myself I was only teasing."

## The 12 beats

| Card | Fixed job (unchanged) | SU 3 beat | Card → the one detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The thing | Name the laugh as letting things tickle her and letting it out unchecked. Before them it came out before she'd looked where it would land | **the Fool** → still in the middle of a step at the cliff edge, eyes on the sky, not on the drop (+ the small dog up on its back legs, mouth open) |
| 2 | The proof · cover word | "Grown up" is the editor on the door. She can still be funny on cue; the unplanned laugh is gone. Shown both ways: what tickles her gets a smile, what she'd say gets checked first | **the Hierophant** → his right hand up, two fingers raised and two folded down: half let out, half kept back |
| 3 | What they saw (her Expression) | Whatever they saw, it came with her laugh out in the open, unguarded, so easy to take. They kept the use of her and made the laugh cost | **the Magician** → everything he has laid out on the table in front of him, in plain view |
| 4 | Why they kept it | Giving the laugh back means admitting it was never silly: they wanted her smaller, and she was fine | **Justice** → one foot showing under the hem of the red robe, the rest hidden |
| 5 | Why so long (the excuse) | "I was only teasing." Why it held, how she backed it (laughing along, calling herself silly first), how she still does their work. The months = the line wearing out | **the Emperor** → seated square on the stone throne, facing straight out: whoever's in the chair decides what counts as a joke |
| 6 | The regret | Making her feel silly for it. Not love; pays nothing back, reaches nobody | **the Hanged Man** → his hands out of sight behind his back: the regret never comes around to the front where it could reach anyone |
| 7 | Your half (her Life Path) | The decent ones since get the grown-up version and her LP pattern. They never hear the real laugh | **the Lovers** → the one gray mountain standing up between the two of them, far off |
| 8 | The bill (Tower) | Attack "grown up": it worked, and here's the cost. The jokes not said, the parties she was the sensible one at, the laughing she sat out. Years | **the Tower** → the lightning that knocks the gold crown clean off the top, still tipping in the air |
| 9 | The fork | Get-back: they want the laugh back with themselves as the one it's for; part of her is glad. Giving: they want rid of it; the day she kept free for their explanation isn't coming | **the Moon** → the crayfish crawling up out of the pool at the bottom: the old hope coming up out of the dark |
| 10 | Will they act: no (Sun) | The turn. The laugh was never theirs to hand back. When she stops waiting for their say-so, it's already hers | **the Sun** → four sunflowers with their heads up over the gray wall: nobody lifted them (+ the child, arms open) |
| 11 | The unsaid | Their sentence says she was never too much: they wanted her smaller. Reading it ends the search and the job of writing their half | **the Star** → one huge yellow star with seven small white stars around it: the big one isn't asked to shrink |
| 12 | Stop and do (World) | Retire "grown up": a grown woman with the whole circle done is the one dancing. Seven days, one silly thing out loud a day | **the World** → one leg crossed behind the other, caught mid-step: she's dancing |

The opening, the letter and the close keep their jobs. The letter names what they did to her laugh, in the
regret's words, and gives the unsaid sentence word for word.
