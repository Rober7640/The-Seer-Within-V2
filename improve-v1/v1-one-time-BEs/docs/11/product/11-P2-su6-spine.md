## Soul Urge 6 — the belief that she'd be looked after

**The one idea:** being looked after is the plain, unthought belief that if she went down, someone would come.
It runs both ways: she looks after people, and she lets herself be looked after, without keeping count, because
she trusts it comes back around. They took her care and gave none back, so she learned nobody's coming. She
stopped expecting it, then stopped asking, then stopped taking it even when it's offered, and called that "fine
on my own". It comes back when she reads that someone should have looked after her and they knew it. The seven
days let one small help in a day, with nothing paid back for it.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "6", used as written):** took = your belief that you'd be looked after ·
cover word = fine on my own · regret = taking her care and giving none back · unsaid = "Someone should have
looked after you, and I knew it." · do = Ask for one small help a day and take it with only a thank you.

**Card 5's one excuse (written in the report):** "She can look after herself." It held because it was true: she
could, and she proved it every day. Being able to was turned into having to. The letter echoes it: "I told myself
you could look after yourself."
