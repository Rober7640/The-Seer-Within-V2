## Soul Urge 8 — worth

**The one idea:** worth is the part of her that knows what she's owed and asks for it, and the part that
takes what's given without checking first whether she deserves it. It runs both ways: asking out, and
letting in. They paid her short on purpose and let her think short was her size. She learned to need less,
take the small piece, say "anything's fine", and calls that "easy to please". Waiting for them to say what
she was worth is the last bill she's still waiting on them to settle; when she stops waiting, it's hers.
The seven days hand it small asks, one a day, with no "sorry" in front.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "8", as written):** took = your sense of your own worth · cover word
= easy to please · regret = letting her think it was all she deserved · unsaid = "You were worth more than I
gave, and I knew what I was paying." · do = once a day, ask for the thing that's hers, with no apology in
front of it.

**Card 5's one excuse:** "She never asked for more." Fits this story: it's true on its face, she didn't ask,
and that's exactly what was taught. The letter echoes it: "I told myself you never asked for more."

**A thread through the pictures:** a crown worn (1) → a crown knocked off (8) → a whole wreath worn (12).
