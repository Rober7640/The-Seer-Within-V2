Three small things, for thirty days:

**Stop going back.** Rereading old messages, looking up the name, writing a message you swear you'll never send: it's all one habit. When the pull comes, put the phone in another room and set the oven timer for twenty minutes. The pull drops off if nobody feeds it.

**The one thing to do.** Once a day, let one thing of yours go out the way you first made it: the text without reading it over, the dish without a word about it, the work without one more pass. Nothing involving anybody from back then.

**Ask for one hand you don't need.** Once a week, ask somebody for a small hand: a lift, a second pair of eyes. You can manage fine. Ask anyway. Asking doesn't prove a thing about you anymore. Part of you will mark it down as a fail. It isn't one.

Start with the stepladder, the next time a bulb goes. Before you climb, call somebody over to hold the bottom. Not because you'd fall. You never have. At the top you'll have both hands free and no knee against the rail, and the new bulb goes in on the first turn. It's back. Go and use it.
