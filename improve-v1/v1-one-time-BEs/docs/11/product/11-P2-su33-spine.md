## Soul Urge 33 — her wanting

**The one idea:** wanting is knowing what she'd like and saying it out loud, as if it counts. It runs both ways:
inside, she knows her own mind about small things (where to eat, which one, what to watch); outside, she says it
before anybody else has decided for her. With them, what she wanted came second and then stopped coming up at all;
it suited them, and they let it stay that way. So she quit saying it, and then got slow at knowing it, and called
that "I don't mind". She's a little proud of being easy to please. It comes back when she reads that she was
allowed to want things. The seven days have her say one small want a day, first, before anyone else speaks.

**The row (11-acts.json → soul_urge → "33", used as written):** took = your wanting · cover word = I don't mind ·
regret = teaching her that what she wanted didn't count · unsaid = "You were allowed to want things, and I made
that too much to ask." · do = Each day, before anyone else speaks, say what she wants, however small.

**Card 5's one excuse (written in the report):** "She never asked for anything." It held because by the end it
was true: she'd stopped asking. "I don't mind" backed it up. She still does their work: "either one's fine" and
then watching their face to find out which one she should want; "whatever's easiest". The letter echoes it:
"I told myself you never asked for anything." They knew enough back then to let it go on.
