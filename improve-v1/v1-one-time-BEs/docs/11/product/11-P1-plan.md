## 1. The shape

**One story, told in the order it happened, by Evelyn at her table over one night and the day after.** Twelve cards in four rows of three, then the letter. That is also the picture for D12: four rows of three cards, then a sealed envelope.

```

Why they kept it      [4] [5] [6]
What it has cost you  [7] [8] [9]     ← low point
How it comes back     [10][11][12]    ← turn at 10

```

## 5. The flat answers

**The same buyer always gets the same answers, because they come only from her three numbers.** No random step, no model choice. **The number decides the answer. The card only decides how it lands:** which picture it's told through.

Worked from `su`, `ex`, `lp`, each brought to one digit for this sum only (11→2, 22→4, 33→6).

| Question | Rule | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| Giving it back, or trying to get you back? | `(su + ex + lp) mod 3` | 0 = **Trying to get you back.** 1 or 2 = **Giving it back** |
| Why has it taken this long? | `ex mod 3` picks the excuse they used | 0 = "It was nobody's fault." 1 = "She'll be fine." 2 = "She was too much." Evelyn knocks it down |
| Will they act? | **Always no.** `lp mod 3` picks the reason | 0 = pride. 1 = shame: they can't stand to be looked at by the one person who knows. 2 = doing nothing costs them nothing |
| Should she answer? | Always no | You don't need to. It's already back |
| What they took, regret, unsaid | Soul Urge row (§4) | One each |
| What they saw · her pattern | Expression · Life Path (§4) | One each |
| Why they kept it | Same for all | Giving it back means saying what they did |

Run over about 23,000 made-up name and date pairs, each key splits close to a third per value, so the fork is about 1 in 3 "trying to get you back".

**Test buyer:** 2 + 4 + 7 = 13, mod 3 = 1, so giving it back. `ex` 4 mod 3 = 1, so "She'll be fine". `lp` 7 mod 3 = 1, so shame.

| House 9 | "They're trying to get you back." The presence doesn't leave. It comes right up to the table and stays. The low point is that part of her is glad to read this |

## 6. The one thing to stop, the one thing to do

Both sit in house 12, as E2 promised. Neither needs any contact with this person.

**Stop going back.** Rereading, looking them up, starting a message are one act. When the pull comes: phone in another room, oven timer for twenty minutes, hands busy, no arguing with it.
*Why it works:* an urge rises and falls by itself if it isn't fed. Going to look at the peak of it is what teaches it to come back. Letting it pass weakens it each time. This is ordinary habit science, not card lore, and Evelyn says it in kitchen words. Twenty minutes is a round figure, so nothing should hang on it being exact.

**Do the seven-day practice.** One small act of the thing that came back, once a day for seven days, row in §4. Only with people who've never given her cause.
*Why it works:* she can't think her way back to it. Her head has one huge example and has gone over it thousands of times. Small acts that turn out fine give it something new to go on, and one that goes wrong shows her she survives it. Seven days sits inside the 5–7 day window on purpose, so she has something to do when the pull is strongest.

Each earlier section ends on one small do or stop. They lead here, but they aren't scored or listed back to her.

## 7. The story plan, written per buyer before any card

The system writes this short plan first. Then it writes the reading in one go, from the plan. This is what stops twelve separate verdicts.

**The night is fixed. These beats happen in this order for everyone:**

| When | At Evelyn's table |
|---|---|
| Opening | Clears the table. Free cards on the windowsill. Name and date on a clean sheet. Tea. They arrive at her shoulder |
| Cards 1–3 | They lean in. She can't write yet. She asks out loud why they still have it |
| Cards 4–6 | They back away to the door. Past midnight. Still nothing on the sheet |
| Card 8 | She leaves the table and stands outside |
| Card 9 | Three in the morning. They go, or close in. The sheet is blank. She doesn't know if she'll get the letter |
| Card 10 | Window goes grey. She sees she's been waiting, same as the buyer. She stops. They're across the table. First line comes |
| Card 11 | The rest starts to come |
| Card 12 | Full light. Free cards back in the deck |
| The next day | She writes the letter by hand. This is the 24 hours |

| Cover word | Named in 2, attacked in 8, retired in 12 | Careful |

## 10. How it gets written

| Rule | Why |

| Prompt gets the plan and the meanings. ⛔ **No sentences from this file or the test reading go in the prompt** | Quoted examples get copied. Measured on 02 |
| Evelyn's night is hers. Her life is common behaviours only, stated flat | 02 invented private scenes. Test: could a hundred women each point at something different and all be right? |

| Say "this is someone living" in the opening and again if Death lands. Say "you owe them no reply" in the opening and house 11 | 11-A, 11-B |

| Check after writing: banned words, they/them throughout, no gendered word for the person, name count, no numbers talk, letter 400–600 words, the exact last line | Machine check only. Your reading is the gate |
