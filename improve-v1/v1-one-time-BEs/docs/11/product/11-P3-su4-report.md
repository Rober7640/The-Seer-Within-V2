**Within 7 days.** The pull hits hardest late, after you've tried the front door and gone to bed. You'll be tempted to search the name, or dig out something old to read. That's the old watch keeping you up, and it doesn't mean anybody's coming back.

Signs to watch for:

- Waking up and not remembering whether you got up to try the lock.
- A friend staying over and never once hearing you up in the night.
- The one who said "You used to be able to sleep anywhere" finding you asleep on the couch.
- A noise in the night, and you staying in bed.

Three of those inside a month means it's settling.

For the next thirty days, three small things:

**Stop going back.** Looking up the name, rereading old messages, typing one you swear you won't send: it's all one habit. When the pull comes, leave the phone on the counter and go to another room for twenty minutes. By then it will have gone back down.

**The one thing to do.** Once a day, take ten minutes off watch. Sit in a chair at home with the phone out of reach and nothing to listen for, and let your eyes close if they want to. Leave anybody from back then out of it.

**Let somebody else drive.** Once this month, when a ride's on offer, say yes and leave your keys on the hook. Don't work out how you'd get home.

Start with the porch light, if there's one by your door that's burned every night since. Get a bulb with a motion sensor in it. They fit the same socket. Screw it in, leave the switch on, and go to bed. The step goes dark like the rest of the street. If anybody ever does come up the walk, the light's on before they reach the door, and you didn't have to sit up for it. The night is yours again. It's back. Go and use it.
