<!-- BEAT 1 · transactional subject -->

**Subject:** Your Unsent Letter reading is confirmed

**Preheader:** What happens next, and what not to do while you wait.

---

Dear %FIRSTNAME%,

<!-- BEAT 2 · thanks as HER decision -->

You've left this alone for a long time. Today you didn't, and that was the right call.

<!-- BEAT 3 · the work has started, present tense -->

Your twelve are on my table now and I've started.

<!-- BEAT 4 · restate the wait + the delivery subject, word for word -->

Your reading will be with you within 24 hours. Watch for an email with this subject line:

> %FIRSTNAME% — your letter is written out

If 24 hours pass and it isn't there, look in your promotions tab, then reply to this email and I'll sort it out.

<!-- BEAT 5 · open ONE loop: the fork. T4 points at it. Act 3 of the reading answers it -->

I'll tell you what I'm looking for, so you know what's coming. The fork. Giving it back, or trying to get you back. I told you those sound the same, and I told you the twelve answer it flat. They will.

Nothing's needed from you for that.

### Now the hard part

<!-- BEAT 6 · the Devil's warning, made live. The message is as close as it has been -->

I told you the pull is strongest right when the message gets close.

It's on my table, %FIRSTNAME%. It doesn't get closer than that.

So expect it, and expect it late, when the house is quiet. Here's what you don't do.

**Don't look them up.** Not their page, not their photos, not the friend who'd know. You'll have their name in your search bar before you've decided to.

**Don't write to them.** Not one word, and not to clear the air. You'll tell yourself it's only that. You never have to contact them, and if this person hurt you, you owe them no reply.

**Don't go back through the old messages.** You know them word for word. What you're going back for isn't in there.

**Don't sit watching your phone.** I told you this doesn't come through a phone. If it lights up, that's got nothing to do with this, and nothing here needs you to answer it.

**Don't guess what they took.** You'll have a guess. Most women guess wrong, and sitting up working it out is only you writing their half again.

It'll feel like a sign. It'll feel like love. You know what I called it.

Put the phone in another room if you have to.

<!-- BEAT 7 · bounds this to the wait, so it doesn't spend the twelfth card -->

This is only for the wait, dear. How you get through it is in the last of your twelve.

You've carried this a long time. You can leave your phone alone for one day.

The chains on that card are loose. Don't put yours back on with your own hands.

<!-- BEAT 8 · sign-off. No CTA, no next offer -->

You'll hear from me when it's written out.

— Evelyn

## Build notes

- **Why there's no story.** 00e P5 says match her state and scale to the wait: 24 hours earns a short piece. 02's buyer is hopeful, so a warm story fits. 11's buyer is about to feel the pull, so the most useful thing Evelyn can do is warn her. The operator asked for exactly this.
- **It's all don'ts, on purpose.** The letter says what she must stop and the one thing she must do are "in the last of your twelve". So this email only covers the wait, and says so. "Put the phone in another room" is the one small do, and it's only another way of saying don't.
- **Nothing tells her to wait for them, or expect them.** The window is about what she feels. "If it lights up, that's got nothing to do with this" covers the woman who does hear from them, so she doesn't read a real message as the call.
- **"Late, when the house is quiet"** is safe for any buyer: every 24-hour wait holds a night. It's the letter's own phrase.
- **The fork is the one loop.** Act 3 of the reading answers it, and T4 points there.
- **No share ask.** 02 and 04 ask her to pass the story on. There's no story here, and this isn't something she'd forward.
- **No bump mention.** This goes to every buyer.
- **The subject line must match T1 and T4 exactly.** For a buyer with no first name the box renders *"dear — your letter is written out"*. The T4 subject needs the same `"dear"` fallback.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 3:** salutation (`"friend"` fallback), the subject box, and the "on my table" line (`"dear"` fallback).
- **Reveal words kept out:** "good judgment" is not used in the thanks.
