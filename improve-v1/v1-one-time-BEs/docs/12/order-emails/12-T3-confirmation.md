# 12-T3 — Confirmation email + wait-filler *(Lucky Numbers)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | `Your Lucky Numbers reading is confirmed` — transactional and searchable. ⛔ Not the broadcast format |
| **Trigger** | AWeber Campaign, tag applied: `be-12-lucky-numbers` *(proposed, code only)*. Wait: none |
| **Purpose** | The receipt, and the wait. Restates **within 24 hours**, names the delivery subject word for word, opens one loop (the number to stop using), and gives her one small job for the wait. **Sells nothing** |
| **Wait-filler** | No story. One job: write down where her numbers live today. See build notes |

---

<!-- BEAT 1 · transactional subject -->

**Subject:** Your Lucky Numbers reading is confirmed

**Preheader:** What happens next, and one small job while you wait.

---

Dear %FIRSTNAME%,

<!-- BEAT 2 · thanks as HER decision -->

You've let your luck run on guesswork for a long time. Today you asked for your own numbers, and that was the right call.

<!-- BEAT 3 · the work has started, present tense -->

Your name and date are on my table now, and I've started.

<!-- BEAT 4 · restate the wait + the delivery subject, word for word -->

Your reading will be with you within 24 hours. Watch for an email with this subject line:

> %FIRSTNAME% — your lucky numbers are worked out

If 24 hours pass and it isn't there, look in your promotions tab, then reply to this email and I'll sort it out.

<!-- BEAT 5 · open ONE loop: the number to stop using. T4 points at it -->

I'll tell you what I'm looking for first, so you know what's coming. The number that's been working against you. I told you it sits in your house number, your phone or your PIN, or close to one of them. Your reading names it, and tells you where to look.

### One small job while you wait

<!-- BEAT 6 · the wait-filler: a job that sets up the reading -->

Get a scrap of paper and write down three things:

**Your house number.** The one on your front door.

**Your phone number.** Just the last four digits will do.

**Your PIN.** Don't write the whole thing anywhere it can be found. Just know where it is in your head.

That's all. Don't change any of them yet. When your reading comes, you'll hold them up against the number to stop using, and you'll see straight away where it's been hiding.

<!-- BEAT 7 · the promise, bounded to the wait -->

And no tickets on a guess today. Wait one day for your own numbers.

You made me a promise in my letter. Your numbers are for turning up, not for chasing.

<!-- BEAT 8 · sign-off. No CTA, no next offer -->

You'll hear from me when they're worked out.

— Evelyn

## Build notes

- **Why there's no story.** 24 hours earns a short piece, and her state is impatient, not anxious: she wants to use her numbers now. So the most useful thing Evelyn can do is give her a small job that pays off the moment the reading lands.
- **The job sets up the product.** The letters' stop-number section says "look in the places you use most. Your house number. Your phone number. The PIN." The job puts those in front of her before the reading names the number.
- ⛔ **Never ask her to send any of them.** The PIN line says so outright. Nothing here is a reply.
- **"No tickets on a guess today."** Ties the wait to the letters' condition, and stops her spending before she has her six.
- **The stop-number is the one loop.** T4 points at where it is in the reading.
- **No bump mention.** This goes to every buyer.
- **No win, no sum, no date.** Nothing here says what her numbers will bring.
- **The subject line must match T1 and T4 exactly.** For a buyer with no first name the box renders *"dear — your lucky numbers are worked out"*.
- **`%FIRSTNAME%` × 2:** salutation (`"friend"` fallback) and the subject box (`"dear"` fallback).
