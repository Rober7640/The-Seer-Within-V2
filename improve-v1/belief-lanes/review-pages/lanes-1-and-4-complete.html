What the words data says

  • Buyers name signs. They don't use chart jargon. Of 90,321 love and someone chats in the past year, 625 used any astrology word. 367 named a sign ("he is a Scorpian and I am a Gemini", "Interesting that all 3 are born under Gemini") and 162 said "compatible". Chart words barely appear: horoscope 18, birth chart 15, zodiac 15, retrograde 2, Saturn return 0. Signs and "compatible" are this lane's "attract".
  • The buyers who name signs skew older (quotes mention 56, 61, 62, 68, 70), so plain words beat Instagram jargon.
  • "Karmic" stands out. 167 chats used it, and 20% of them bought, against 8–14% for the other spiritual words in the same pull (soulmate 7.8%, twin flame 10.8%, fate or "meant to be" 9.2%, the universe 13.9%).
  • Instagram astrology-love posts are almost all "zodiac compatibility" and "comment your sign and his". The most-commented one (545k views, 2.2k comments) asks which sign Scorpio "falls in love with over and over again… without even realizing it". That's the pattern belief in astrology clothes.
