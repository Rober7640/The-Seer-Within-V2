## Decide first: the headline

All three share the sub-line and the button below. Each one names a problem and has "pattern" in it.

1. **Your Pattern Started Long Before Him, and It's Still Blocking Love — I Can See It** (my pick). It bets on the third ad and the button. "Long before him" takes the blame off him without putting it on her, and "pattern" is the second word.
2. **Something Is Keeping You Stuck in the Same Pattern With Men — I Can See It.** It bets that she notices the repeat before she wonders about the cause. It's the plainest, and the closest to today's "Something Is Holding You Back". "Pattern" sits mid-line.
3. **It Isn't Him. Something in Your Pattern Is Blocking Love — I Can See It.** It bets on the first ad's turn ("I used to think it was him"). It's the strongest for a woman who has blamed the men, and the riskiest for one who's still angry at him.

Rest of the card:
- Sub-line: *Evelyn has sensed an old lesson from your childhood. It's why the same kind of man never quite chooses you. She can see where it started, from your first name.*
- Button: *Yes Evelyn, Show Me Where It Started*
- Under it: *It's free to message Evelyn. She's online now.*

## What the buyer data said

Source: the live database, read only. Love and someone chats from the past year: 90,529 chats, 6,653 paid. Our chat asks for this lane's words itself ("Where did this start...", "Has anyone in your family struggled with this same pattern?"), so I counted **her first message only**.

- **Our own question inflates this lane.** "Childhood" shows up in 97 first messages but in 1,357 chats overall, and 1,084 of those are answers to "Where did this start". "Pattern": 117 first messages against 811 overall. The README says the lane is 3,107 chats (re-run today: 3,427, with 484 paid). Counting first messages only, the same word list hits **551 chats, 77 paid (14.0%)**. It's still a real lane, but about a sixth of the size the README says. Worth correcting in the README.
- **Her own word is "the same".** 1,614 first messages use "same". 167 use it about men ("always choosing the same type of man", "it always ends up the same way"). She also says "always" (377) and "keep meeting / keep attracting" (196).
- **Both stories are already in her words:** "choose me / pick me" 241, "just friends / friend zone" 243, "won't marry / won't commit" 70.
- **The pattern words buy best:** childhood 21.6% paid, repeat 20.7%, cycle 18.6%, pattern 15.4%. Compare these with each other, not with the overall rate.
- **Community words barely show up in chats:** inner child 4 first messages, little girl 17, shadow work 11 (none paid). So "inner child" is on the page for Meta and the community. "Pattern", "the same" and "childhood" are her words.
- **What the keep-out words cost:** heal/healing is the biggest word in this whole area, with 1,038 first messages. Trauma: 202, and 20.8% of them paid. Both stay out, along with narcissist (367), toxic (219), abandon (126) and attachment (46).
- **Therapy is common here:** 99 first messages mention a therapist or counselling, and 19.2% of those paid. That's why the page never mentions it.
- **Not pulled:** Instagram and TikTok (Virlo), the Meta Ad Library and Reddit, because no paid calls were allowed. The only community lines come from the saved Instagram pull on the Nov 17 draft (not refreshed): "The quiet one. The girl who learned to read the room, keep the peace…" and "A child who had to earn love often becomes an adult who's terrified of losing it." Both back up "go quiet" and "the little girl".

## Calls I made without asking

1. **Therapy is nowhere on the page.** There's no comparison and no "Evelyn isn't a therapist". A woman in therapy has nothing to read as a dig.
2. **Inner child work isn't in the "tried" list, and isn't in the page description either.** It's the lane's belief. Listed among things that didn't work, it would knock it.
3. **"Pattern" is the main word, "childhood" second, "the same" is her word.** "Inner child" is the lane's name, but buyers almost never type it, so it's used 3 times, not 6–8.
4. **The list heading carries the keyword** ("What you've tried to break the pattern"). Lanes 1 and 4 don't do that.
5. **The mentor's line** ("Keep quiet. Want nothing. Make it easy to stay.") is reused for message match. It states the belief, not her story, so it fits every ad.
6. **The friends' lines are made up,** not quotes from buyers. No buyer's words are on the page.
7. **"Cycle" is left for the family lane,** and "shadow work" is left off as younger-skewing (11 first messages, none paid).

## What else to decide

1. Therapy stays off the page, and the chat only respects it if she brings it up. OK? If you want a respectful nod for viewers of the therapist video, it belongs in that ad.
2. Inner child 3 times and little girl twice. Enough for Meta, or starting to sound like a counselling page?
3. Heal/healing and trauma stay out of the page and the chat. Confirm.
4. Use "cycle" here too, or keep it for the family lane?
5. Message 6 asks how far back she remembers it. That sets up the chat's own "Where did this start" step, which may now feel like a repeat. Keep it, or stop the question at "how it usually goes with men"?
6. Boundaries and self-love in the tried list: right for women 35 to 65 and up?
