## No blame

You said: don't blame mothers or grandmothers; they carried it, and she gets to put it down. I did it the same way as manifestation ("None of it was wrong") and astrology. These lines carry it:
- *It isn't your fault, or hers.*
- *not one of them chose it*
- *They carried it as far as they could. You're the one who gets to put it down.* These are your words.
- *She won't blame your mother, or hers. She'll show you what they were carrying.*
- On the card: *It's nobody's fault.*
- In the chat: *Nobody chose it, dear. Not you, and not them.*

The grandmother's wry line includes herself ("The women in this family…"), so it's hers to say, not a charge against her. The list says "your story would go differently", not "never end up like your mother", so there's no judgment of her mother's life. The directive tells every later chat step never to blame any woman before her.
