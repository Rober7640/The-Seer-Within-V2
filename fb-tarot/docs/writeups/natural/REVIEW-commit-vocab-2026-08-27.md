# Natural Tarot-Cut — commitment age-matrix + connection-vocab, 2026-08-27

Six landers on `return-mhf`, face down. Seven cuts per card; cuts 3–6 fold into registry beat 3.
VOC behind every one of them: `fb-tarot/docs/drafts/rewrites/_voc-commit-vocab-2026-08-27.md`.

**Method:** `natural-cut` — cut 3 ANSWERS her fear flat.

**Magician detail, rationed across the six** so the table does not open every card: the four
objects (expecting · stopping), standing in the open (wife), the flowers at his feet (instant —
the only read here that is about desire), the lemniscate as endlessness (how-long — never as a
loop), the arrangement as intent (heading).

predict a marriage or a proposal · never touch money, property or legal standing · never rule

***"Am I not good enough?"*** So cut 3 answers **it isn't you** — it never names his obstacle.
🔴 Never affirm a man she has only met online is real · never rule on a third party's
