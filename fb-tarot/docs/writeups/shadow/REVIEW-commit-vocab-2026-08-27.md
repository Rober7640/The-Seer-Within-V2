# Inherited Shadow — commitment age-matrix + connection-vocab, 2026-08-27

The shadow arm of the same six landers. Six beats per card: claim · proof · her · but · so ·
look closer. Beat 5 carries one handle she can picture — **position, timing or manner, once each
across the three cards** — plus the mandatory origin finding. Beat 6 is a neutral pointer and
adds no property.

**Method:** `inherited-shadow` — beat 3 OPENS her question and never answers it. The card is the
WARRANT, never the block. The lander never says "family line"; the inheritance goes in as origin.

VOC: `fb-tarot/docs/drafts/rewrites/_voc-commit-vocab-2026-08-27.md`. Same per-lander bans as the
natural manuscript — they are properties of the audience, not of the method.
