## Open flags carried forward (see chat for full detail)

1. **Money-cheated (65+)** — headline revised to "I lost what I had through someone I trusted..." Both "cheated" and the direct label "dishonest" are retired. Scam-victim overlap in the VOC still stands regardless of wording — needs a scam-safe guard on the read.
2. **Money-broke (22-29)** — headline revised to "I'm living paycheck to paycheck and can't get started..." ("broke" flagged, "paycheck to paycheck" accepted as lower-risk).
3. **Money-debt ("the two best")** — resolved as "I keep paying what I owe. Why is my money still blocked?" The word "debt" is retired; the payment/obligation meaning remains, so the money-safety guard still applies.
4. **Alone/Connection trio** — likely decode-him, not loneliness; only 1 VOC source, and it's about a real man.
5. **Money "still working" (55–64)** — near-duplicate of live `cards-still-working`.
6. **Commit "years together" (55–64, operator's pick)** — near-duplicate of live `cards-wont-commit`.
7. **Alone duration questions** — will need a hard "never give a length of time" rule, not just a date ban (a soft version leaked once before on a different family).
8. **God/Prayer (money + loneliness)** — loneliness needs its own prayer-guard clause, mirroring the one already built for money.
