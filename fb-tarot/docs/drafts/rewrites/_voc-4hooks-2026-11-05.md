## Q1 · "Why do my relationships always end the same way?"

**Exact buyer:** *"Tired of trying in relationships because they all end the same way."*

**The word doing the work:** *same*. It's not that they end; it's that she can see the ending coming.

**The fear under it:** *it's me, and it will happen again* → *"A relationship never sticks and I
fear I will be alone without a partner for the rest of my life."* The SAME ending is mostly HIS
act: *"every relationship I have had they cheated. Two husbands"* · *"all of the men I have loved
have let me down and gone on to marry the next woman"* · *"I'm the one who loves hard and winds up
getting hurt."*

**Who the ad will harm:**
- Women leaving toxic or cheating men (*"72 hours of no contact with my last extremely toxic ex"*).
  A read that says "you keep choosing this" blames her for what was done to her.
- Women stuck in a cycle with ONE man (ghost → return, *"over a year of this same cycle"*). The
  ad pulls decode-him readers too; the read can't promise he'll change or that it'll end.

**What she has already been told, which the read must refuse:**
- *"I was told that there is some kind of pattern repeating itself"* (another reader).
- *"my childhood attachment wounds"* · *"I have done the work, for years…"* She arrives already
  diagnosed and already in therapy. **No therapy or diagnosis language, no "break the pattern",
  no "do the work".**
