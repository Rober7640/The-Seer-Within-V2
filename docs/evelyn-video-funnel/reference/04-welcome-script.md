## Why stones, not cards — the structural reason

The competitor's bridge uses **crystals** and their VSL is a **card-by-card reading**.
That is not an accident. **The choice device and the reading mechanism are deliberately
different, so the bridge does not spend the reading's reveal.**

If she picks a card in video 1, video 1 has already spent the thing video 2 exists to do.
A stone is a free, cheap, zero-cost choice that leaves the cards entirely withheld — and it
gives video 2 its opening move: *the stone you chose decides the card I turn.*

Our card mechanism is untouched. It just starts in video 2.

### Beat 4 — one number, not a funnel (2026-10-07)

Originally cut as an unverifiable claim. **Operator supplied real numbers: over 6,000 customers,
over 100,000 free readers.**

**Operator's call: state one number, 10,000 — not the 100,000 → 6,000 funnel.** Right instinct:
the funnel version makes the reader do arithmetic and land on "only 6% of them bought", which
argues against us inside our own proof beat.

**Three things this line gets right, keep all three:**
1. **"a reading", not "this reading".** "This reading" points at the paid product, where the
   true count is 6,000 — 10,000 would then be false. Against a reading generally it is true
   with an enormous margin.
2. **Usage, not efficacy.** Mia's "worked for over 10,000 women" claims an outcome. Ours claims
   only that women *had* it. That difference is the safety margin — do not let it drift.
3. **Deliberately understated.** 10,000 sits far below the real base, so it survives scrutiny.

⚠ Worth revisiting once: **6,000 is the more impressive claim** (those are *buyers*), and odd
specific numbers usually read as more credible than round ones. Operator chose the round
10,000 — noted, not blocking.

⚠ **Live site is understating badly.** `TarotBridge.tsx` ships `100% Private · 2,400+ readings`.
Stale by more than half. Worth updating, and whatever ships should be dated and checkable.

## 🔴 Present from frame 0, but not presented — corrected 2026-10-08

Frame-tracked in `06-competitor-frame-track.md`. My earlier note said "the stones are in frame
for all 57 seconds and that is what makes the choice feel real." Half right, and the other half
is the useful bit.

**They are there from frame 0 and never leave** — nothing is placed, revealed or introduced.
**But for the first ~46s they are deliberately not presented:** low in frame, partly cropped by
the bottom edge, sitting *behind* the brass table rim, slightly soft while she is sharp, never
looked at, never gestured to, never cut to. Only at ~46s and ~52–56s do her hands come down and
point, and only then do they become the subject.

**The rule to copy: present from the first frame so the choice feels like it was always waiting,
withheld from attention until the moment of choosing.**

Also from the track, and worth having:
- **One continuous take, no cuts, 57.6s.** Nothing in their bridge is edited.
- Burned captions, 2–5 word phrase chunks, plus a **down-arrow graphic at ~56s** at the buttons.
- Their table is glass with a brass rim. **Ours stays dark wood in the warm room** — the decks
  are the thing to copy, not the furniture.
