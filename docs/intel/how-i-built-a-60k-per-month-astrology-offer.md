## The origin story

The inspiration came while in **Brasov, Romania** (near Dracula's castle) — relaxing, hiking, and talking esoterica (witches, pagan rituals, tarot, astrology) with a friend who was into the occult. A bookstore visit turned up two books that sparked the idea: a **love spell book** and a **tarot book**. Bought both, went back to the hotel, and started studying the market potential.

## The CRO booster: checkout-button popup

Initial conversion rate was lower than hoped. The single biggest lift came from adding a **popup on the checkout button** that made the visitor **agree to 3 crucial points** before proceeding (framed as commitment/consistency psychology — the author doesn't elaborate on the mechanism, just confirms it worked). One popup variant referenced a "HIS NAME" personalization field, which the author notes he didn't actually run himself — attributed to a later swipe/clone of the funnel.

## Pricing

- **Frontend price testing:** split-tested $7, $11, $17, $27, $37 → **$17 won**.
- **Order bump:** $12.77 for "double the power of the enchantment" — best-performing bump.
- **Resulting AOV:** ~$50.

## The "request page" (in-between page innovation)

Placed **between the pitch and checkout**. Purpose:
- Gather more information about the customer
- Create commitment (sunk-cost / foot-in-the-door effect)
- Prolong the sales process before the ask

Seen replicated by at least one competitor as a distinct "request page" step in their funnel.

## Upsell 1: the "Rival Remover"

Best-performing upsell 1 = **Rival Remover / Hex Her / Cast Her Away** (naming varies by swipe). Premise: if the man is ignoring her, there's likely another woman occupying his attention, so the upsell removes that rival. Origin: customer support found this was the **single biggest pain point** — customers repeatedly reported the man was already in another relationship — making this the natural, most-requested upsell.

## The top LTV booster: tarot readings

## Why astrology/occult offers work

Unlike a "make money" or "lose weight" offer, there's no direct, literal promise — the appeal is **answering the unknown**. Every empire in history had its own form of the esoteric (shamans, astrologers, "magicians" advising kings and leaders). It's human nature to want answers to the unanswerable, and the astrologer's job is to provide that guidance — *"Why did he break up with me? Why is he not answering his phone? How do I get him to answer my texts?"*

## Delivering the product & building the relationship

Answering these emotional questions is how you build a relationship with the customer, not just deliver a product:

1. Ask (e.g., in the confirmation email or a survey) what went wrong and **why** they bought the love spell.
2. Respond with personalized thoughts based on their answer.
3. Become their personal advisor — described as "almost a form of therapy": customers often want someone who listens and cares, not just a literal solution.

This personalized care is credited with **keeping refunds low** and customers happy. The author had no prior tarot/reading experience, did the reading and email replies himself early on (referencing the books he'd bought), and eventually outsourced it to a VA. This hands-on, personalized backend is framed as the difference between a **$100/day** and a **$1,000/day** business.
