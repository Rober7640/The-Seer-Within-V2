# ALONE

Theme n=2,765 · buy 8.0% · $5,099/1,000 · **+10%** vs bucket base
Control: "Will I be alone forever?" → `cards-alone-forever` *(built)*

## Alone — age matrix

### 25–44 · she says **destined**; a deadline she set has passed
> *"Im 38 and never married and never had any kids and I feel like I'm destined to be alone forever"*

- WHY — "Why do I feel destined to be alone?"
- HOW LONG — "How long am I going to be alone?"
- BINARY — "Did I miss my chance, or am I not destined to be alone?"

### 45–54 · she counts the years, and asks **what's holding it there**
> *"at 52 I hvae lived alone for 6 years and I am wondering what's holding me back"*

- WHY — "I've been alone for years. What's holding it there?"
- HOW LONG — "How many more years alone before something changes?"
- BINARY — "Is something holding me here alone, or is this just my life now?"

### 55–64 · the house has emptied, no one to catch her
> *"63… never married and no children so often feel alone with no one to pick me up when I fall"*

- WHY — "Why is being alone hardest now the house is empty?"
- HOW LONG — "I've been alone a decade. How much longer?"
- BINARY — "Is it too late for me, or is being alone just where I am now?"

### 65+ · **"the rest of my life"**, and the recurring word is *still*
> *"I'm 67 will I be alone the rest of my life?"*

- WHY — "Why does being alone feel heavier now than it used to?"
- DURATION — "Will I be alone for the rest of my life?"
- BINARY — "Am I meant to be alone now, or is there still time?" → `cards-meant-alone` *(built)*

⚠ **20% of this band mention a death.** Bereavement is a flagged sensitive theme — a
deliberate decision, never a default. If run: "He's gone. Do I spend the rest of it alone?"

**connection** — she isn't strictly alone; there's an ambiguous someone she carries alone.
> *"I held the connection alive on my own, when i do not contact i do not hear him"*

- WHY — "Why have I kept this connection alive on my own?"
- HOW LONG — "How long have I been alone waiting on this connection?"
- BINARY — "Is a real connection coming, or do I stay alone?"

**destiny** — ⚠ she says **destined**, never "my destiny".
> *"Am I destined to be alone the rest of my life?"*

- WHY — "Why do I feel destined to be alone?"
- HOW LONG — "How long before I know I'm not destined to be alone?"
- BINARY — "Am I destined to be alone, or has it just not happened yet?"

**god/prayer** — ⚠ God is **not** absent. He's present and she still wants a person. Never
write an accusation. Recruits a religious audience — brand decision, not performance.
> *"I don't think it's God's intention for any of us to be alone"*

- WHY — "God is with me. Why am I still alone?"
- HOW LONG — "How much longer does God mean me to be alone?"
- BINARY — "Is it God's intention for me to be alone, or is someone coming?"

**Probe** *(drops keyword and theme word)* — "Why does love never stay with me?"

# COMMIT

Theme n=719 · buy 11.0% · $6,400/1,000 · **+38%** vs bucket base
🔴 "Will he ever ___" is the losing form — presupposing beat it by 23%. Incumbent is control only.
Control: "Will he ever commit?" → `cards-will-commit` *(built)*
Better control: "Why won't he commit to me?" → `cards-wont-commit` *(built)*

## Commit — age matrix

### 25–44 · **wasted time**, non-committal men
> *"Im tired of non committal men wasting my time. Im 44. I want to be in a committed relationship or marriage!"*

- WHY — "Why do I keep picking non-committal men?"
- HOW LONG — "How long do I give him to commit before I move on?"
- BINARY — "Is he slow to commit, or is he wasting my time?"

### 45–54 · **self-blame**, and a man scared by his history
> *"What am I doing wrong to make him not commit, am I too nice, not stable…"*

- WHY — "What am I doing wrong that he won't commit?"
- HOW LONG — "How long do I wait for him to commit this time?"
- BINARY — "Is he scared to commit, or just never going to?"

⚠ The ad may ask her self-blaming question; the lander must **not** answer it as her fault.
`cards-wont-commit` already routes the "why" to where he is stuck. Keep that.

### 55–64 · **years together, still nothing official** — the strongest cell
> *"I am 55 been together for 9 years and I would like to marry"*

- WHY — **"Why won't he commit after all these years together?"** ← best line in the set
- HOW LONG — "How many years together before he makes a commitment?"
- BINARY — "Am I expecting too much, or should he have committed by now?"

### 65+ · **companionship vs commitment**, and neither has runway
> *"I am interested in remarriage, even though I am 73 years old… I want companiondhip for the rest of my life"*

- WHY — "Why won't he commit when neither of us has time to waste?"
- HOW LONG — "How much longer do I wait for him to commit?"
- BINARY — **"Does he want to commit, or does he just want company?"**
- optional — "I've played the wife without the commitment. Why?"

## Evidence grade

Words she wrote **after clicking** — a correlate with a valuable customer, not proof an ad
carrying the word attracts her. Only a live test closes that gap.

Cells are small: the biggest keyword cell is n=55, several rest on 3–7 buyers. Rank order is
more trustworthy than magnitude. Age sample is self-selected (0.8% volunteer an age).
