# Same headlines, run through direct-response

Companion to `fb-ad-levers-alone-commit.md`. Same 41 headlines, run through
`direct-response-copy`.

**Result: 3 lines change. 38 stay as they were.**

The first version of this doc recommended 13 rewrites. Operator rejected them
2026-08-21 — "unusually direct, won't work" — and was right. The rule that killed them is
below, along with every binned line, so nobody derives them again.

---

## 🔴 The rule: the ad is a mirror, not a testimony

**Be specific about the feeling. Never about her biography.**

DR's specificity principle (Hopkins, Ogilvy) is sound, and I applied it on the wrong axis —
putting *her facts* in the line rather than *her wound language*. Four reasons that fails
on this funnel:

1. **The lander cannot echo a fact it doesn't know.** Beat 2 of every read repeats the ad
   question back — *"You asked me about alone and forever."* / *"You asked whether he'll
   ever commit."* There is no version of that beat that survives an ad saying "37 years
   alone." The handoff breaks mechanically, not just tonally.
2. **A number is a disqualifier.** If hers isn't 37 years, or nine years together, it isn't
   her ad. The age lever exists precisely to add specificity *without* costing pool; a
   number in the copy hands that back.
3. **It stops being her question.** "Will I be alone forever?" is a wonder she is already
   having. "Never married, no kids, and I feel destined to be alone" is a stranger's post.
4. **A click is semi-public.** The question form gives her deniability — she is only
   curious. The confession form asks her to admit something on a scrolling feed.

This does **not** contradict the standing preference for long, VOC-traced headlines over
short direct-response fragments. That preference is about **wound language**, which stays.
Biographical facts were never what made those lines specific.

## What I rejected, and why

DR's best-known formulas do not survive contact with this funnel. Written down so nobody
re-applies them.

| DR move | Why it fails here |
|---|---|
| **Put her number in** (Hopkins specificity)<br>*"Nine years together. Why won't he commit?"* | The rule above. This was the single biggest "win" of the first pass and it is the most wrong |
| **Caples story-in-a-line**<br>*"Engaged eight years, still not married."* | Same — a story needs facts, and the facts are hers, not ours to assert |
| **Master formula** — "[verb] + [outcome] + [timeframe]" | Promises an outcome. The funnel forbids forecasts in both directions |
| **Caples' question headline** — *"Do You Make These Mistakes in English?"* | Second person — Meta's banned form for personal attributes |
| **Shorten it** | Already tested. Long VOC-traced lines beat short DR fragments here |
| **"Secret" / "Discover" / "Unlock"** | Off-voice for Evelyn, reads as hype to the platform |
| **Social proof, urgency, deadlines** | No product in the ad, and deadlines are banned outright |
| **Transformation headline** — "From [bad] to [good]" | States the ending |

**What survives:** contrast, curiosity-gap discipline, and Collier's *"enter the conversation
already taking place in her mind"* — which is the VOC method under an older name, and
confirms the existing approach rather than changing it.

**Schwartz check:** she is **Problem-Aware** (level 2) — knows the wound, doesn't know a
reading addresses it. Level 2 wants the problem named, not a solution pitched. Every current
headline already does that. No change needed.

## 3 borderline lines — your call

No numbers in these, but each puts a **self-verdict** in her mouth rather than a question.
That is a softer version of the same problem, so I am not shipping them either way without
a decision.

| Theme | Current | Proposed | The risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| alone, 25–44 | Did I miss my chance, or am I not destined to be alone? | I think I had my chance and blew it. Am I destined to be alone now? | Near-verbatim VOC, but it asks her to agree she blew it before she has clicked |
| alone, 65+ | Why does being alone feel heavier now than it used to? | Nobody asks how my day was. Why does being alone feel different now? | Fixes "heavier" being abstract, but asserts a detail of her household |
| commit, 45–54 | What am I doing wrong that he won't commit? | Am I too nice? Is that why he won't commit? | Sharper and VOC-traced, but names her flaw for her |

My read: the third is the safest — "too nice" is her own guess, and it's still a question.
The first two assert something about her life. I'd keep the current lines for both.
