Stop rehearsing the reunion
 

You've played it out a hundred times, Sarah — the text, the apology, the moment it all comes rushing back. So let me be honest with you: when a current actually returns, it almost never arrives the way you rehearsed. It's quieter. Smaller. Easy to miss if you're staring at the door waiting for fireworks. I won't promise you it's coming — I don't read the future, and anyone who does is guessing. What I can tell you is whether the cord is still carrying, or whether you've been keeping it warm by yourself. Those are very different — and only one of them is worth your waiting.

 
 
 
Bring me the connection you keep replaying. I'll feel whether the current still runs both ways — or only out of you. You deserve to know which before you wait another week.
