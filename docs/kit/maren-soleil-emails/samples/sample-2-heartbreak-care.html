You are not too much
 

Someone, somewhere, taught you that feeling things this deeply is a flaw to manage, Sarah. I want to gently disagree with them.

The intensity you've been apologizing for — the way you love hard, miss harder, and can't just switch it off — that isn't too much. It's the instrument. It's the exact thing that lets you feel a cord at all. People who love carefully don't get hurt like this. They also never get to feel what you feel.

I won't tell you the ache will be gone by morning — that wouldn't be honest, and you've had enough of people managing your feelings. But I'll tell you this, and I mean it: the depth is proof of your capacity, not your brokenness. And the right connection will never ask you to turn the volume down.
