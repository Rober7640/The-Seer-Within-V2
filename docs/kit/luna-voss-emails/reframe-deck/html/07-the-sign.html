The sky didn't do this to you

 

Every time Venus goes retrograde, I get some version of the same message: "is that why my ex texted?" "Is that why I suddenly miss someone I haven't thought about in a year?"

I understand the instinct. Something shifts in the sky, something shifts in you, and it's tempting to make the sky the reason.

It isn't the reason. It's the flashlight.

You think a retrograde, a full moon, an eclipse made something happen to your love life. It's actually surfacing a decision or a person you'd already parked — the sky didn't write the question, it just turned the lights on in the room where you left it.

That's why the same transit hits ten people ten different ways. It's not handing out a universal experience. It's shining on whatever's already sitting there, unresolved, in each person's own chart — an old ex, a stalled decision, a "we never actually talked about it."

So when something old resurfaces during a transit like this, the useful question isn't "why is the universe doing this to me." It's: what did I leave unfinished that's asking to be looked at again?

Tell me what surfaced for you, dear. Not everyone gets the same flashlight beam — yours is personal.
