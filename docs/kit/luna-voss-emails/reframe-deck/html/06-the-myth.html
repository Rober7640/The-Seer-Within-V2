"Not compatible" is half a sentence

 

"We're just not compatible" is the astrology version of "it's not you, it's me." Technically a sentence. Not actually an explanation.

Two people can share zero "easy" aspects and still work, and two people can be sign-matched on paper and fall apart in six weeks. Compatibility isn't a yes/no. It's a shape — where two charts click, where they grind, and whether the grinding is workable or wears you down.

"Not compatible" sounds like a verdict from the universe. It's usually a smaller, less dramatic sentence hiding underneath: we didn't handle the hard parts well.

Every real relationship has hard aspects somewhere. The chart isn't why it ended — how the two of you met the friction is.

Some of the strongest relationships I've read for have "difficult" aspects between them — a square, an opposition, something a quiz would flag as a warning sign. Difficult aspects aren't a red flag. They're friction, and friction is just where growth happens if two people are willing to do the work.

Next time "we're just not compatible" comes out of your mouth, ask yourself the more specific, less comfortable version of that sentence. It's usually there.
