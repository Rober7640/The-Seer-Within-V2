# Evelyn — Situation & Fable Library

Fuel for the `evelyn-daily` skill. Each situation already carries the hard part: a **mechanism seed** (the non-obvious *why*) so the drafter starts with substance, not a sentiment. Pull from here honoring the ~30-day no-repeat in `STATE.md`; **balance the threads** (the first 14 over-indexed over-giver and had zero money). When the well runs low, invent fresh situations from the threads below and append them here.

Format: **`id`** — situation. *Mechanism seed:* the turn that gives it substance. *Practice hint:* a do-it-today with teeth. *Sketch:* drawable subject. `bucket` · default soft/door.

> Guardrail reminder while drafting from these: tendencies not promises, no orphaned objects (no candle — use lamp/notebook/morning), `dear` ×2–4, Christianity-free, rotate the intuitive tell.

## Money-block / inherited scarcity  (MISSING from first 14 — use early)

- **`money-lid-on-jar`** — you keep a lid on the jar; you can't let good things (money, ease, help) in. *Mechanism:* receiving was taught to make you indebted and exposed, so scarcity started to feel like safety, even virtue. *Practice:* let one good thing in this week un-repaid; notice the urge to square it. *Sketch:* a jar with a lid, a few coins beside it. `money` · soft.
- **`money-friend-rate`** — you discount your own work, give everyone the "friend rate." *Mechanism:* you're pricing your *worth*, not the *work*; charging full feels like greed because wanting more was shamed in you young. *Practice:* name the full number out loud once, alone, without flinching. *Sketch:* a price tag, a lower figure written under the crossed-out one. `money` · soft.
- **`money-inherited-fear`** — your mother's money-fear lives in your body though your circumstances differ. *Mechanism:* you inherited a nervous system tuned to a scarcity that isn't yours anymore; the panic is a hand-me-down. *Practice:* when the clutch comes, ask "whose fear is this — mine, or hers?" *Sketch:* a purse held shut with both hands. `money` · soft.
- **`money-spend-on-self`** — you can earn it, even save it, but not spend it on yourself. *Mechanism:* spending on others reads as love, on yourself as waste — the self-last rule wearing a money coat. *Practice:* buy the one small thing that's only for you; sit in the guilt without undoing it. *Sketch:* a coin set on a shop counter, a hand hovering over it. `money` · soft.
- **`money-clenched-fist`** — you hold so tight nothing flows; saving curdled into fear. *Mechanism:* the grip tells your body there's danger, and the danger-feeling keeps you poor in spirit no matter the balance. *Practice:* loosen one small thing — a gift, a tip, a treat — on purpose, and watch the roof hold. *Sketch:* a closed fist, one coin showing. `money` · **door** (the-page-that-turns).
- **`money-never-ask`** — you never ask for the raise or the fair share; you wait to be noticed and rewarded. *Mechanism:* "good girls don't ask"; being given-without-asking felt safer than risking a "no." *Practice:* ask one small thing this week you'd normally wait to be offered. *Sketch:* a hand half-raised, then lowered. `money`/`purpose` · **door**.

## Grief of the living & loss  (thin — only day 13 so far)

- **`grief-living-loss`** — missing someone who changed or drifted but didn't die (a grown child, a friend, a spouse "still here"). *Mechanism:* you're not *allowed* to mourn the living, so the grief gets no ritual and stays stuck with nowhere to go. *Practice:* let yourself name it as grief, plainly, once — that alone unsticks it. *Sketch:* two cups, one untouched. `love` · soft.
- **`grief-ordinary-ambush`** — it's their mug, their chair, the ordinary thing that floors you. *Mechanism:* grief lives in the mundane, not the milestones; that's why it ambushes you in the kitchen, not the cemetery. *Practice:* let the object be a hello, not only a wound — greet it. *Sketch:* an empty chair pulled slightly out. `grief` · soft.
- **`grief-body-anniversary`** — your body grows heavy near a date your mind tries to skip. *Mechanism:* the body keeps anniversaries the diary forgets; the heaviness isn't random, it's a remembering. *Practice:* mark the day on purpose, small, instead of bracing against it. *Sketch:* a calendar page, one day faintly circled. `grief` · soft.
- **`grief-imagined-future`** — you grieve the tomorrows you'd pictured, not only the person. *Mechanism:* the imagined future is its own loss and needs its own mourning, or it masquerades as "I should be over them." *Practice:* name the specific tomorrow you lost; grieve *that* separately. *Sketch:* a folded map. `grief`/`purpose` · soft.
- **`grief-no-timeline`** — you think you should "be over it by now." *Mechanism:* grief isn't a task to complete; the pressure to be done is a second weight laid on the first. *Practice:* give yourself the day's grief its hour, then let the day continue. *Sketch:* a clock with no hands. `grief` · soft.

## Late-life reinvention & invisibility  (thin)

- **`latelife-too-late`** — "too old to begin again" at 55–70. *Mechanism:* "too late" is a story that shields you from the risk of starting; the door was never age-locked, you locked it. *Practice:* take the first ten-minute step toward the thing, today, before the story wakes. *Sketch:* a sapling in an old pot. `purpose` · **door** (recurring-pull).
- **`invisibility-after-children`** — the needing ended; the loving didn't; the house has gone quiet. *Mechanism:* you built a self around being needed, so quiet reads as erasure — but it's space you were never taught to fill for *you*. *Practice:* put one thing in the quiet that's only yours. *Sketch:* a single place set at a long table. `purpose` · soft.
- **`latelife-unlived-self`** — the woman you set aside to cope is still in there. *Mechanism:* she wasn't killed, only paused; the ache isn't regret, it's her knocking. *Practice:* do one small thing she'd have loved, this week. *Sketch:* a coat on a hook, long unworn. `purpose` · soft.
- **`invisibility-unseen`** — feeling unseen by shops, by the young, by the world. *Mechanism:* a youth-worshipping culture trained you to read invisibility as "less"; it's their blindness, not your fading. *Practice:* take up your space once, on purpose, unhurried. *Sketch:* a woman walking, the others blurred past. `self` · soft.

## Forgiveness, anger & resentment

- **`forgiveness-self`** — the hardest forgiveness is your younger self, for what she didn't yet know. *Mechanism:* you judge a past you with a present you's knowledge; she was doing her best with far less. *Practice:* say to her the thing you'd say to a frightened friend. *Sketch:* an old photograph, one corner bent. `self`/`grief` · soft.
- **`resentment-ledger`** — the silent scoreboard you keep in a relationship. *Mechanism:* the ledger guarantees you both lose; love can't grow where every gift is logged for repayment. *Practice:* give one thing this week and refuse to write it down. *Sketch:* a tally chalked on a slate. `love` · soft.
- **`anger-old-stone`** — anger at someone long gone that you still carry daily. *Mechanism:* the anger is how you stay tied to what hurt you; setting it down feels like letting them off, but it only lets *you* go. *Practice:* name what the anger costs you each day; weigh that against what it costs them (nothing). *Sketch:* a stone resting in an open palm. `forgiveness` · **door**.

## Self-worth, comparison & the inner voice

- **`comparison-inside-outside`** — you measure your insides against everyone's outsides. *Mechanism:* you compare your behind-the-scenes to their edited front; the scale is rigged before you start. *Practice:* this week, when you compare, finish the thought with "…to a version of them I've never met." *Sketch:* a stage curtain, half open. `self` · soft.
- **`notenough-goalpost`** — you reach the goal and the bar leaps ahead. *Mechanism:* "enough" was never the destination; the post moves because the rule is *keep proving*, not *arrive*. *Practice:* mark one finish line as final and stand still on it for a day. *Sketch:* a finish line drawn, another faint beyond it. `self`/`purpose` · soft.
- **`inner-voice-rented`** — the cruel inner commentary you'd never aim at a friend. *Mechanism:* that voice was someone else's once; you can stop renting it your ear. *Practice:* catch one cruel line, ask "whose voice is that," and answer it as you'd defend a friend. *Sketch:* a small figure under a large shadow. `self` · soft.
- **`apology-habit`** — you apologise for existing, for asking, for taking room. *Mechanism:* "sorry" became the toll you pay to be allowed in the room. *Practice:* swap one reflex "sorry" for "thank you" ("thank you for waiting," not "sorry I'm late"). *Sketch:* a worn doormat. `self` · soft.

## Anxiety, worry, control & overthinking

- **`control-grip`** — gripping the future to feel safe. *Mechanism:* worry disguises itself as preparation; it's a tax you pre-pay on a debt that rarely comes due. *Practice:* when it spins, ask "is this planning, or just paying tax?" *Sketch:* a fist closed around a string. `self` · soft.
- **`overthink-motion`** — the 3am loop that solves nothing. *Mechanism:* the mind spins because spinning *feels* like progress; it's motion mistaken for movement. *Practice:* write the worry on paper; the loop needs your head, not the page. *Sketch:* a rough spiral. `self` · soft.
- **`permission-giver-gone`** — waiting to be told it's allowed (to rest, to want, to go). *Mechanism:* you're waiting on a permission-giver who left long ago; the only one left to grant it is you. *Practice:* grant one permission out loud, in your own name. *Sketch:* a closed gate, the latch already undone. `purpose` · soft.

## Loneliness, connection & being seen

- **`lonely-in-crowd`** — lonely though surrounded; known by no one. *Mechanism:* you perform "fine" so well that no one reaches; the armour that protects you also hides you. *Practice:* let one person see one true, un-fine thing. *Sketch:* a lit window seen from the dark outside. `self`/`love` · soft.
- **`asking-is-a-gift`** — you'd rather break than ask for help. *Mechanism:* asking feels like handing over your weakness; really it hands someone your *trust*, which is a gift to them. *Practice:* ask one small thing, and watch them be glad to be asked. *Sketch:* a hand reaching, not quite touching another. `self` · **door**.
- **`the-honest-no`** — you say yes and resent it; "no" feels dangerous. *Mechanism:* a yes you don't mean is a quiet betrayal of yourself, dressed up as kindness. *Practice:* give one honest small "no" this week and survive the silence after. *Sketch:* a door held half-shut. `self` · soft.

## Love — longing, recognition, choice

- **`waiting-to-be-chosen`** — you wait to be picked rather than choosing. *Mechanism:* being chosen feels safer than choosing — no risk of the "no" — but waiting hands the pen to everyone but you. *Practice:* choose one thing first this week, before you're invited to. *Sketch:* a hand on a door handle, not yet turning. `love` · **door**.
- **`old-flame-whatif`** — the one that got away, polished over years. *Mechanism:* you're not missing *them*; you're missing the version of *you* that you were then. *Practice:* name what you miss about who you were, and bring one piece of her back. *Sketch:* a faded, refolded letter. `love` · soft.
- **`cracked-cup-love`** — you can't hold love you don't believe you're worth. *Mechanism:* the leak isn't the love coming in; it's the belief that drains it before it lands. *Practice:* notice one piece of love you discount; let it count, just once. *Sketch:* a cup with a hairline crack. `love`/`self` · soft.
- **`staying-from-fear`** — staying from fear of being alone, not from love. *Mechanism:* "alone" is the thing you're fleeing, and avoidance — not love — is quietly making the choice. *Practice:* ask, plainly, "if I weren't afraid of alone, what would I choose?" Just ask. *Sketch:* two coats on one hook. `love` · **door**.

## Purpose, wasted years & meaning

- **`wasted-years-soil`** — the fear that the past was thrown away. *Mechanism:* nothing's wasted that taught you; the "lost" years are the soil, not the loss. *Practice:* name one thing a "wasted" year planted that you still use. *Sketch:* a bare tree with visibly deep roots. `purpose` · soft.
- **`small-life-proximity`** — your life feels too small to matter. *Mechanism:* you measure meaning by scale — reach, applause — but meaning is made in proximity, in the few you actually touch. *Practice:* count the people one arm's length away you've quietly held up. *Sketch:* a small lamp on a kitchen table. `purpose` · soft.  *(note: no candle — lamp.)*
- **`body-faithful-friend`** — at war with your changing body. *Mechanism:* the body you criticise is the one that carried you through everything; the war is with your most faithful friend. *Practice:* thank one part of it that still does its quiet work. *Sketch:* two older hands, folded. `self` · soft.

## Fable well  (Christianity-free — tag the tradition; no-repeat within ~30 days)

Retell haltingly, in Evelyn's mouth, then land the *mechanism* — never clean storybook prose, never a tidy moral bolted on.

- **`f-oak-reed`** (aesop) — *USED day 2.* rigidity vs the give that survives.
- **`f-empty-boat`** (taoist) — *USED day 9.* anger needs an intender.
- **`f-sun-and-wind`** (aesop) — wind can't tear the coat off; the sun's warmth does it. *Mechanism:* force hardens people; warmth opens them — true of others and of yourself. *Sketch:* a coat, a low sun.
- **`f-cracked-pot`** (folk/indian) — the cracked pot that watered wildflowers along the path it leaked on. *Mechanism:* the flaw you apologise for is doing quiet good you can't see. *Sketch:* a pot leaking a thin trail of water, small flowers.
- **`f-two-wolves`** (folk, secular) — which one wins? the one you feed. *Mechanism:* you don't kill the harsh voice; you starve it and feed the other. *Sketch:* two rough wolf shapes, one fainter.
- **`f-nasrudin-lamp`** (sufi-secular) — searching for the lost key under the lamppost "because the light's better here." *Mechanism:* we look for answers where it's easy, not where we dropped them. *Sketch:* a lamppost, a hand searching the lit ground.
- **`f-zen-full-cup`** (zen) — the teacher pours tea till it overflows the professor's cup: a full cup can hold no more. *Mechanism:* you can't receive anything new while clutching what you already "know." *Sketch:* an overfull teacup, tea on the saucer.
- **`f-two-monks-river`** (zen) — one monk carries a stranger across the river and sets her down; hours on, the other is still carrying her, in his head. *Mechanism:* the weight isn't the event; it's the carrying you keep doing after. *Sketch:* a shallow river crossing.
- **`f-farmer-maybe`** (taoist) — the horse runs off, returns with others, the son breaks a leg, is spared the war: "good luck, bad luck, who's to say." *Mechanism:* we judge an event's meaning long before it's finished revealing it. *Sketch:* an open gate, a horse beyond.
- **`f-stonecutter`** (japanese folk) — the stonecutter who wished to be sun, cloud, mountain, and found a stonecutter could undo a mountain. *Mechanism:* the grass-is-greener wish is a loop with no door; the power you envy is undone by the life you left. *Sketch:* a mountain, a small chisel.
- **`f-crow-and-pitcher`** (aesop) — the crow raising the water one dropped pebble at a time. *Mechanism:* small, dull, repeated acts move what one big effort can't. *Sketch:* a pitcher, a few pebbles.
- **`f-tortoise`** (aesop) — steadiness past the flashier hare. *Mechanism:* your unglamorous pace is a strategy, not a shortfall. *Sketch:* a tortoise mid-step.
- **`f-bundle-of-sticks`** (aesop) — one stick snaps; the bundle won't. *Mechanism:* what breaks you alone holds easily when it's not carried alone. *Sketch:* a tied bundle of sticks. *(natural door — asking for company.)*
- **`f-lion-and-mouse`** (aesop) — the mouse too small to matter frees the lion. *Mechanism:* nothing and no one is too small to be the thing that helps. *Sketch:* a tiny mouse at a great paw.
- **`f-starfish`** (folk) — thrown back one at a time: "it mattered to that one." *Mechanism:* meaning isn't scale; it's the one in your hand. *Sketch:* a starfish in an open palm.
- **`f-kintsugi`** (japanese) — the broken bowl mended with gold, the seam now the loveliest part. *Mechanism:* the mend doesn't hide the break; it makes it the beauty. *Sketch:* a bowl with a bright gold seam.
- **`f-nasrudin-donkeys`** (sufi-secular) — the smuggler everyone searched, whose cargo was the donkeys themselves. *Mechanism:* the obvious thing, in plain sight, is the thing we never check. *Sketch:* a laden donkey.
- **`f-zen-mending`** (zen/folk) — the invisible stitch, the patient repair done where no one looks. *Mechanism:* quiet repair is real work; it counts even unseen. *Sketch:* a needle and thread mid-stitch.
