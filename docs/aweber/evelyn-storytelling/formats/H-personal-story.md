# H · Personal Story

**Status:** ⛔ BLOCKED — needs an operator decision on Evelyn's own "life"
**In one line:** something that happened to ME yesterday → a line someone said → the lesson → buy. Furey's main format.
**Source:** Furey, *Tao of Email Copywriting* — "How to Force People to Read Your Emails" (PDF p.26: his father's stroke + how his father taught him to read aloud), "I Dare You" (p.66: bowling, "Throw a gutter ball"), "The Finished Line" (p.67: *"You need to finish something,"* his wife said), the marathon monk (p.4–5), "Attack Your Fear" (p.21). His rule: *"Nothing bad ever happens to an email copywriter: EVERYTHING is MATERIAL. Share openly… Let the reader have a peek behind the curtain."* (p.34)

## Why it's blocked
Evelyn is a persona. Her "yesterday" would be invented, which breaks our truth rule (no invented memories, events or clients) and the operator's own rule from the Marcus emails ("never invent client stories").

## Ways it could be unblocked (operator to choose)
1. **Established canon** — the operator writes a short, fixed Evelyn biography (where she lives, her grandmother, her kitchen…) and we only use events from it, consistently.
2. **Thoughts, not events** — "I was thinking this morning about…" (already allowed in A's opening reflection).
3. **Leave it** — use true stories (A) instead.

## If unblocked — the shape
A small, specific moment (with dialogue) → the line that stuck → the lesson → her version → pitch. One laugh. ≈350–500 words.
