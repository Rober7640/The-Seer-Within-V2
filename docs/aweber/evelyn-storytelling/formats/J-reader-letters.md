# J · Reader Letters

**Status:** ⛔ BLOCKED — needs real letters and permission
**In one line:** a short intro → 2–3 real reader messages, each with a one-line reply from Evelyn → close → a "Columbo" P.S. with one more letter.
**Source:** Furey, *Tao of Email Copywriting*, PDF p.50–53 & p.61 — "He's a Crack Smoker" / "They Must Be Smoking Crack": selling against belief ("it works whether you think it will or not"), real testimonials with "M.F.:" replies, scarcity, and the **Columbo P.S.** (p.53): *"appear to be closing the email. But then, in the P.S. you introduce another letter. And this letter needs to be hard-hitting."*

## Why it's blocked
Needs genuine messages (AWeber replies, chat feedback) with permission to quote. Never invent or "tidy up" a testimonial; never imply typical results.

## The device we CAN use now
The **Columbo P.S.** — a strong second point in the P.S. after the email seems finished (works in any format).
