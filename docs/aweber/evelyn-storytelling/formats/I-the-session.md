# I · The Session

**Status:** ⛔ BLOCKED — needs a real client and permission
**In one line:** one coaching session told as a scene — her problem, a surprising instruction, a big result — then "what I taught her comes from…" → buy.
**Source:** Furey, *Tao of Email Copywriting*, PDF p.40 — "How One Girl Used Her Imagination to Score Big Points" (Sean Furey). A basketball player hitting 2 in 10; he tells her to stay OUT of the gym for a week and play "imaginary basketball"; she hits 24 in a row. Furey: *"See how this email is personal… the reader is entertained — but not taught anything valuable… And finally, note how the reader is told to buy something."*

## Why it's blocked
For Evelyn this would be "a reading I did" — an invented client and an invented result = a fake testimonial. Not allowed.

## Could be unblocked by
- A real V2 chat, anonymised, with the client's explicit permission (and the privacy policy allowing it).
- An openly-framed composite ("Women tell me versions of this all the time. Here's one.") with **no result claimed**.
