# Format 07 — The sign

*Read [PLAYBOOK.md](../PLAYBOOK.md) and [hooks.md](../hooks.md) first. This spec only adds what's specific to this format.*

**Type:** interactive (drives replies) · **The turn:** an external omen → an internal signal about the reader's own unfinished choice

## 1. Purpose / when to use
Take a "sign" people believe is a message about their person — *11:11, a recurring bird, their song everywhere* — and reframe it as a message about the reader's own **attention and unfinished business.** It's spiritually congruent (Evelyn *honors* the sign, then redirects its meaning), and **interactive** (tell me your sign), so it pulls replies. Use for engagement and a gentle empowerment turn, especially when the reader is stuck waiting on "signs."

## 2. The reframe mechanic
The sign is **real but misread**. It's not the universe pointing at *them*; it's the reader's own attention flagging a question they've left open. Reframe from **external omen → internal compass**, from **leash → lamp**, from *"wait for them"* → *"decide something."* Frame the mechanism honestly-spiritually (their heart pointing at the unfinished thing) — never a dismissive "it's just confirmation bias." The audience believes; meet them there and lift, don't debunk.

## 3. The hook
**Pattern:** the omen / recurring sign ([hooks.md](../hooks.md) #7). Name the thing they keep seeing, then deny the meaning they were handed.

Hook bank:
- "You keep seeing it, don't you, dear. 11:11, every time you look. It doesn't mean what they told you." — *gold; names it, denies the given meaning.*
- "The same song, in three shops in one afternoon. You think it's a message from him. It is a message. Just not from him." — *grants the sign, moves the sender.*
- "A white feather. A robin. His initials on a stranger's cup. You've been reading these all wrong — and the right reading is better." — *stacks specifics + promises a better read.*
- "You asked the universe for a sign. It's been answering all week. You just don't like the actual answer." — *flips who's avoiding what.*
- "Every time you think of leaving, you see something that 'tells' you to stay. Let me show you who's really sending those." — *exposes the mechanism as the hook.*

## 4. The skeleton
1. **The sign** (hook) — name what they keep seeing + the given meaning (*"they're thinking of you; wait"*).
2. **The gentle pivot** — *"I'm going to tell you something gentler and truer."*
3. **Honor it** — *"the sign is real."* (Validate the spiritual instinct before redirecting — never mock it.)
4. **The reframe** (pull-quote) — it's a message about where your attention lives, not about them.
5. **The mechanism** — your attention keeps landing there because a question is open; it flags the world until you answer it.
6. **Why it matters** — reading it as *"wait"* hands your peace to a clock; reading it as *"decide"* makes it useful. **Leash → lamp.**
7. **The practice** — ask what the sign is asking *of* you, not what it's promising you.
8. **Interactive ask** — tell me your sign; I'll tell you what it's really pointing at.

## 5. Subject + preheader
Subtle emoji or none (card-cue 🌙 works). Formula: `{first}, <the sign / "you keep seeing it">. It doesn't mean what they told you.`
- `{first}, you keep seeing it. It doesn't mean what they told you.`
- `{first}, you asked for a sign. You don't like the answer it's giving.`
- `🌙 {first}, 11:11 isn't about him. It never was.`

Preheader: *"The number, the bird, the song on repeat."*

## 6. The ask / CTA
Interactive — a reply-style prompt. Benefit-worded: **"Tell me your sign."** The click leads to chat where they name it. Single CTA. Tag `campaign=reframe-07-sign`.

## 7. Formatting notes
- The two competing readings in *italics* (*"they're coming, keep waiting"* vs *"there's something I haven't decided"*).
- Reframe **pull-quote** carries "a message about where your attention lives."
- **Bold** the *leash → lamp* turn.

## 8. Do / Don't
- **Do** honor the sign before redirecting it. The audience is spiritual; validate the instinct, then lift it — never debunk or condescend.
- **Do** keep it **empowering** (their agency), not deflating.
- **Don't** rationalize it away ("just your brain / confirmation bias"). Frame the mechanism honestly-spiritually — *your own heart pointing at the unfinished thing.*
- **Don't** name a specific person or promise a specific outcome (guardrail).
