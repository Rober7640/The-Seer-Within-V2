# Format 05 — Stop calling it that

*Read [PLAYBOOK.md](../PLAYBOOK.md) and [hooks.md](../hooks.md) first. This spec only adds what's specific to this format.*

**Type:** conversion beat (one of ~2/week) · **The turn:** a kind euphemism → the frightened real behaviour underneath

## 1. Purpose / when to use
The bluntest format, and the second **conversion beat.** Take a self-flattering euphemism people use for a love behaviour — *"giving them space," "protecting my peace," "keeping my options open," "being low-maintenance"* — and rename it as the frightened thing it often is. It converts naturally because the CTA ("come see what you're really doing") **is** the reframe. Confronting, but always compassionate. Use as one of your two weekly beats; don't stack it beside Format 01.

## 2. The reframe mechanic
Rename in three beats: **the euphemism → the real behaviour → the cost.** We rename fear as a virtue because the virtue is bearable and the fear isn't. The reframe strips the kind word and shows the protective (often self-sabotaging) function underneath. Crucially it keeps an honest out — *sometimes* the euphemism is accurate — which is what keeps it truthful rather than preachy.

## 3. The hook
**Pattern:** the named euphemism ([hooks.md](../hooks.md) #5). Say the kind word the reader uses, then promise to look under it.

Hook bank:
- "You call it *'giving them space.'* Here's what it really is." — *gold; the promise to unmask.*
- "*'I'm just protecting my peace,'* you tell yourself. Sometimes that's true. Sometimes it's a locked door with the lights off." — *concedes, then the vivid rename.*
- "You say you're *'keeping your options open.'* Let's see whose options that actually protects." — *turns the phrase into a question.*
- "*'I'm being realistic,'* you call it. Realistic and braced can look identical from the outside. They are not the same thing." — *names the confusable pair.*
- "You call it *'being low-maintenance.'* I've watched *'low-maintenance'* quietly starve a lot of good people." — *the cost previewed.*

## 4. The skeleton
1. **The euphemism** (hook) — *"You call it 'X,' dear."*
2. **Why it sounds so good** — concede its appeal; the generous story it lets you tell.
3. **The honest look** — *"let's put the gentle word down."* Sometimes it's true; sometimes it's [the frightened thing].
4. **The reframe** (pull-quote) — the real behaviour, named. (*"You call it space. Your body calls it **bracing**."*)
5. **Why we do it** — the mechanism: rename fear as virtue because the virtue is bearable.
6. **The cost** — what the euphemism costs them (the manufactured distance turns real).
7. **The practice** — say the truer sentence for a day; see if it fits — *with an honest out* (*"if it doesn't fit, good, you really were being generous"*).
8. **Conversion ask** — if it landed with a thud, don't sit in it alone; come see what you're bracing against.

## 5. Subject + preheader
🚩 / ⚠️ suit the confronting register. Formula: `<emoji> {first}, you call it "<euphemism>." Here's what it really is.`
- `🚩 {first}, you call it "giving them space." Here's what it really is.`
- `{first}, "protecting my peace" — sometimes that's a locked door.`
- `⚠️ {first}, "low-maintenance" is quietly starving you.`

Preheader: *"The gentle word we use to hide the frightened thing."*

## 6. The ask / CTA
Conversion beat. Benefit-worded: **"Show me what I'm really doing."** Single CTA. Tag `campaign=reframe-05-<euphemism-slug>`.

## 7. Formatting notes
- The euphemism in *italics* every time.
- Reframe **pull-quote** carries the rename; **bold** the single rename word (*bracing*).
- The "truer sentence" the reader tries → *italics*.

## 8. Do / Don't
- **Do** give the honest out (*"sometimes X is exactly what it says"*). This keeps it true (the euphemism sometimes *is* accurate) and compassionate rather than accusatory.
- **Do** stay gender-neutral (*them / the person / the other one*).
- **Don't** scold, and don't claim the euphemism is *always* a lie — that's false and preachy. Always "sometimes X, sometimes Y."
- **Don't** run it back-to-back with Format 01 — space the two conversion beats across the week.
