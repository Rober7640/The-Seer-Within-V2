# 04 · The sign

- **Format:** The sign
- **Type:** interactive
- **Reframe:** The recurring dream is them reaching you → it's the one hour you stop managing what you want, and it shows you the unedited version.
- **Cast:** neutral
- **State:** B — waiting
- **Subject:** `{{ subscriber.first_name | capitalize }}, three nights this week. It wasn't them visiting you.`
- **Preheader:** The dream is real. The sender is not who you were told.
- **CTA:** Tell me what happened in the dream → `campaign=reframe-c3-04-the-dream`

---

Three nights this week, dear. The same person, never the same room.

You woke with your chest going, and you gave the first ten minutes of the day to working out what it meant. Somebody has told you already, or you've read it somewhere: *they're thinking about you. That's why they're coming through. It's a sign.*

I'm not going to take that from you. A dream that keeps returning is a message, and I have never once thought otherwise. I only want to move who it came from.

> **↳ THE REFRAME.** A dream about them isn't them reaching you. It's the one hour of the day when you stop managing how much you want, and it shows you the unedited size of it.

Think about what you do with the wanting while you're awake, dear. You keep it reasonable. You have had months of practice now at being fine, at not checking, at caring the correct amount in front of people who would worry. That is real work and you are good at it.

Then you fall asleep and there is nobody in there to be fine for. Nothing in a dream has to be sensible, so it all comes out at full size, and you meet the version of yourself you've been trimming since March.

This is why the reading matters. If the dream came from them, your nights belong to their schedule, and there's nothing to do but wait for the next one and try to decode it. If it came from you, it just told you exactly where you are, and you can do something about that before lunch.

So the next time it happens, don't ask what they were telling you. Ask what *you* were doing in it. That's the part nobody looks at, and it's the whole message. Chasing. Explaining yourself again. Being finally, properly heard. Forgiving them, or being forgiven. Whatever you were doing in there is the thing you have not been able to do in daylight, and naming it turns a haunting into an instruction.

Then tell me your dream. Not what they said in it. Tell me what you were doing, and I'll tell you what it's pointing at.
