# 01 · The question behind the question

- **Format:** The question behind the question
- **Type:** conversion beat
- **Reframe:** "Was any of it real?" → "Can I still trust my own eyes, now the only other witness has changed their story?"
- **Cast:** male POV (a man's letter)
- **State:** C — bereft

- **Preheader:** The word was *real*. He said it three times and never once said hers.

---

Four lines came in at two in the morning. One word in three of them, and it wasn't her name.

> *"Three years. Last week she told me she was never really in love with me, not the way I thought she was. So was any of it real? Was it real for even part of it? I need to know if it was real."*

That looks like a question about her. **It isn't one.**

Look at what he's actually done, dear. He didn't ask whether she'd come back. He didn't ask what he missed. He asked a stranger to tell him whether three years he lived through, every day of them, happened at all. He was there. He is the only witness left standing to his own side of it, and he has come to me to have his own eyes checked.

> **↳ THE REFRAME.** "Was any of it real" is never a question about them. It's a person asking whether they're still allowed to trust what they saw, now that the only other witness has changed their story.

Here is why it grips so hard. When someone rewrites the story on their way out, the tidiest way to survive it is to agree with them. Fooled the whole time. One clean explanation for three years. It costs less on the night, and far more later, because to believe none of it was real you have to demote yourself as a witness. Do that, and you walk into the next thing with your own eyes disqualified.

So I didn't answer his question. I answered the real one. **People revise. That is not the same as finding out.** Her account changed last week. It did not reach back and change what happened in his kitchen in the first year.

Try this today. Pick one moment from it that had no audience. Not the anniversary, not the photographs. The 6am one, the ordinary one, when neither of you was performing for anyone. Nobody else was in the room. There is nothing there for anyone to revise, and that part stays yours.

You have your own version of his question, dear. Bring it to me and I'll show you the one sitting underneath it.

**→ Help me trust what I felt**

— Evelyn
