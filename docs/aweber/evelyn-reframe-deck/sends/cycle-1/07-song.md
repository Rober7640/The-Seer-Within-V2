# 07 · The sign

- **Format:** The sign
- **Type:** interactive
- **Reframe:** The song finding you everywhere isn't a message from them → it's your attention flagging a choice you haven't made.
- **Cast:** neutral
- **State:** C — bereft
- **Subject:** `🌙 {{ subscriber.first_name | capitalize }}, that song keeps finding you. It isn't the message you think.`
- **Preheader:** The number, the bird, the song on repeat.

---

You keep hearing it, don't you, dear. That song. The one that was yours. In the café, in a stranger's car, on a playlist you didn't build, three times in a day that had no reason to hand it to you.

And someone told you what it means: *they're thinking of you. It's a sign. Wait.*

I'm going to tell you something gentler and truer, and I hope you'll trust me with it.

The song is real. What it means is not what they told you.

> **↳ THE REFRAME.** A thing you keep meeting is not a message from the person you're missing. It's a message about where your attention still lives.

Out of all the sound in a day, dear, your ear keeps catching those few bars. Not because they were sent. Because a question is open in you, and an open question makes you scan the whole world for it. Your attention flags what you haven't finished. The song is only the flag.

And here is why it matters. If you hear it as *"they're thinking of me, so I'll wait,"* you hand your peace to a playlist. But if you hear it as *"there's something here I haven't let myself decide,"* it becomes useful. **The song stops being a leash and becomes a lamp.**

So the next time it finds you, don't ask what it's promising you. Ask what it's asking *of* you. Usually it isn't *"will they come back."* It's *"how long will I keep my life paused, waiting for a song to answer a question only I can?"*

Tell me your sign, the thing that keeps finding you, and I'll tell you what I think it's really pointing at.
