Do you need help, dear?

I'm asking because I pulled the Hanged Man this morning, and the moment I saw him, I stopped.

He hangs upside down, held by one ankle. He isn't struggling to escape. He's seeing everything from a different angle.

This card often appears when pushing harder won't bring the answer any closer. Something may need to be released—or simply looked at another way.

But one card in a general email can't show me what you're facing, or what you may not be seeing yet. For that, I need to hear your question.

If something came to mind while reading this, come and tell me.

**→ Tell me what's on your mind**

— Evelyn
