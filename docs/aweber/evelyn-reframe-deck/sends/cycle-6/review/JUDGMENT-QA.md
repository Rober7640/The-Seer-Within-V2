These are honest and each has a different concrete event. Keep the truth framing. Optional polish would move one framing cue later in its opening rather than remove it.
2. **Receiving help recurs in 06 and 13.** `06-two-of-cups.md`: “Accept it with a simple thank you”; `13-keeping-busy.md`: “accept the meal someone offered.” The actions are related, but 06 concerns automatic refusal/repayment in a new connection, while 13 concerns making actual space for food or support in an overfull day after loss. Seven sends apart, this is acceptable. Preserve the calendar swap in 13 if either is revised; it is what keeps that practice distinct.

This is not an unsupported assertion about the reader's situation.

The initial repeated “company with this” closings in 04/08 were revised by root and rechecked; that note is resolved. The varied continuation openings remain faithful, natural, and each asks one question. Subject 14 now withholds the overlooked second sentence while the body pays it off.
