# 02 · The tell

Someone sat across from me on Tuesday and said *"it's complicated"* twice inside ten minutes.

Nothing had changed in those ten minutes. The second time, I hadn't even asked about the situation. The word came out on its own, the way a hand goes to a bruise.

That is the tell, dear. Not the word. The repeating of it.

So I asked them to tell me the whole thing without using it. They managed in eleven words. Nothing tangled in any of them.

> **↳ THE REFRAME.** *"It's complicated"* is not a description. It's a shelter. You aren't describing a complicated situation, you're protecting yourself from a simple one, because a simple one would ask you to do something about it today.

Look at what the word earns you, dear. A plain answer, said out loud, wants an action by the end of the week. Complexity wants nothing. It asks only for more consideration, which looks like wisdom from the outside and feels like patience from the inside.

Better still, it never runs out. There is always one more factor. Their work. The timing. What they went through last year. What it would cost the people around you. Each one true, and that is why it holds. You never have to lie to stay where you are. You only keep weighing.

**The situation isn't what's complicated. The next move is what's unbearable.**

The advice you'll be handed is *just be honest with yourself.* It assumes you don't know. You know. You have known since some particular afternoon. Knowing was never the missing piece. The missing piece is being willing to do a thing you would then have to live with.

So here is today's task, and it is harder than it sounds. Write your situation in one sentence. No clauses. No *but*. No *although*. One line, then stop.

If you cannot get it under a single line, dear, the complexity is doing a job for you, and the question worth sitting with is what job.

I won't tell you what your sentence should say. It isn't mine to write.

But if the long version keeps winning, bring it to me and we'll find the word that is holding the door open.
