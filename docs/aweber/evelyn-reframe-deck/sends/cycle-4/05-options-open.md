# 05 · Stop calling it that

- **Format:** Stop calling it that
- **Type:** conversion beat
- **Reframe:** "Keeping my options open" → you aren't collecting options, you're abstaining, so that whatever happens you were never the one who chose and never the one who was wrong.
- **Cast:** neutral
- **State:** A — uncertain
- **Subject:** `🚩 {{ subscriber.first_name | capitalize }}, "keeping my options open." Open for who?`
- **Preheader:** The sensible phrase for never being on record.
- **CTA:** Show me what I'm not choosing → `campaign=reframe-c4-05-options-open`

---

You call it *keeping your options open*, dear.

It's the sensible thing to say. Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Don't get ahead of yourself over someone who hasn't decided about you either. Say it out loud and nobody in the room will disagree with a word of it.

It's the second word I want to look at. *Open.*

Sometimes the phrase means exactly what it says. Early weeks, nothing promised in either direction, two or three people you're genuinely curious about and straight with. That isn't fear, that's just how it starts.

But options are only options if somebody eventually picks one.

> **↳ THE REFRAME.** Notice how long yours have stayed open. If nothing is ever chosen, you're not collecting options, you're **abstaining**. And an abstention is the one position that can never be wrong.

There's a reason it's so restful, dear, and it has nothing to do with wanting variety. To choose a person is to state a preference out loud, and a stated preference can be turned down, or can turn out badly, and either way you'd be the one who picked. Abstain and none of that can reach you. Things happen, and they happen to you, and nobody can say you were the fool who wanted it.

The bill comes quietly. People can only meet you as far in as you've come, and everyone can feel a door held open behind you, so they hold one of their own, and now there are two half-present people being careful at each other. Then the ones who could have chosen you go and choose someone who was willing to be on record. You never got refused. You also never got picked.

So here's today's test, and answer it fast, before the sensible part of you gets there. Name the one you would pick, if picking could not turn out to be wrong. A person, a city, a life. Whatever surfaced in that first second is your actual preference, dear, and you have been carrying it around unstated for a while now.

If nothing surfaced, wonderful. Your options really are open and you've simply not met the right one yet.

And if a name arrived before you'd finished reading the question, don't sit with that on your own.

**→ Show me what I'm not choosing**
