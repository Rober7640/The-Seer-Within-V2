# 01 · The card, reframed

- **Format:** The card, reframed
- **Type:** insight
- **Reframe:** The Ten of Swords is total ruin → one sword ended it. The other nine went in afterwards, one per retelling, and those are the ones you can pull out.
- **Cast:** neutral
- **State:** C — bereft
- **Subject:** `😱 {{ subscriber.first_name | capitalize }}, ten swords. One would have been enough.`
- **Preheader:** Look at who put the other nine in.
- **CTA:** Show me which ones are mine → `campaign=reframe-c4-01-ten-of-swords`

---

A figure face down at the water's edge. A red cloak. Ten swords in his back, and behind him a black sky already going gold along the bottom.

People flinch at this one harder than any card in the deck. It reads as the worst that can happen, and the worst having happened thoroughly.

**They're counting wrong.**

Look at the number, dear. Ten. One would have done it. Nobody needs ten swords, which means nine of them went in after it was already finished, and somebody had to stand there and put them in.

> **↳ THE REFRAME.** The first sword is what happened to you. The other nine went in afterwards, one at a time, every time you told yourself the story again in the car.

You know the places you do it. Driving. At the sink. Halfway through a walk you've taken a hundred times. And it never stays the same size, dear. Each pass adds a detail you'd missed, then what it proves about you, then the thing you ought to have seen in the first month, then what everyone must have thought of you all along. The event finished in an afternoon. The telling has been running for a year, and it gets a little crueller each time, because a story you rehearse alone has nobody in it to argue back.

Which is why *stop dwelling on it* slides straight off. It aims at the memory, and the memory was never the problem. What you're doing on that walk isn't remembering. It's prosecuting a case against yourself with the defence absent.

And here is the mercy that the count gives you, the one nobody takes from this card. Only one of those wounds was done to you. Nine were not, and the nine that were not are the only ones you were ever going to be able to reach.

So today, find one sentence you now say about that ending which was not true on the day it happened. *I should have known. I wasted those years. Nobody stays.* Something added later, by you, in a car. That is a sword. Take that one out and leave the true one where it is.

If you know exactly how your version goes by the time you reach the end of the road, come and tell it to someone who will argue back.

**→ Show me which ones are mine**

— Evelyn
