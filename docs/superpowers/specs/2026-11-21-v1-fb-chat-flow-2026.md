# /fb chat flow: interview (A) vs reading (B) · `v1_fb_chat_flow_2026`

Decided with the operator 2026-11-21. /fb only. /fb-tarot gets its own version later.

## Why
Live /fb transcripts (2026-11-17 to 11-20) read as an interview, not a reading:
- **Too many questions.** About 7 questions after the email before the offer. Chats with 8+ questions before the pitch bought at 13.1% against 18.1% with fewer (correlation, n=224).
- **Stock lines everywhere.** "Your heart has walls" in 28% of /fb chats, "A shadow is blocking" in 23%.
- **Her question goes unanswered.** 36% ask Evelyn something directly and the script rolls on.
- **The family question becomes the diagnosis.** "Has anyone in your family…" turns into the answer every time.
- **The name gets no reading.** The lander says "Send Evelyn your first name", and the chat answers "It's lovely to meet you".

## B, step by step
| Step | Evelyn tells her | Evelyn asks |
|---|---|---|
| Name | candle bends and smokes; her name's number (scripted) | what's weighing on your heart (topic buttons) |
| Topic | "That's where the smoke was pulling" (scripted) | the email, word for word as A |
| 1. What's happening (`fbB1`) | the pattern inside her concern | what she wants instead |

| 3. Where it comes from (`fbB3`) | how that start built the pattern; what it cost her | ready to let it go? |
| Diagnosis (`fbBDiagnosis`) | what the black smoke was; why it explains her; why it found her; it won't lift by itself | nothing |

Dropped from A:
- DEEPENING_2 ("how long?")
- FUTURE_VALIDATION ("how would that FEEL?")
- the second yes ("I need your full permission")
- "The link is complete."
- "a flicker of shadow"
- the "not enough" pushbacks

A clear yes at step 3 goes straight to the offer. Anything short of a clear yes gets A's urgency turn and the permission button.

## Copy (scripted)

### After her name (replaces "It's lovely to meet you, {name}.")
    {name}...
    I said your name out loud just now, and the candle beside me bent. Hard to one side.
    Like someone had opened a door. But every window in this room is shut.
    Then it gave off a thin line of black smoke.
    I've sat with candles for over forty years, dear. I know what that means.
    Your name carries a number too. {name} comes to a {n}.
    {number line}
    Everything we discuss stays between us... our secret.
    Now, what's weighing on your heart today?

How the number works:
- Each letter has a value: A=1 … I=9, J=1 … R=9, S=1 … Z=8.
- Add up the letters, then keep adding the digits until one is left.
- Accents are stripped first.
- If the name has no letters A–Z, both number lines are skipped.

Number lines:
1. Ones go first. You've always been the one who figures it out alone... even when you wish you didn't have to.
2. Twos feel everything in a room before anyone speaks. You give people a softness you rarely get back.
3. Threes light up a room... then go home and wonder why it still feels empty.
4. Fours are the steady ones. Everyone leans on you. Nobody asks what it costs you to stay standing.
5. Fives were born restless. You keep reaching for a life that feels like yours... and it keeps slipping out of reach.
6. Sixes hold everyone else together... and quietly wait for someone to hold them.
7. Sevens see straight through people. You've always known things you couldn't explain... and kept most of it to yourself.
8. Eights were built to carry weight. You've worked harder than most for what should have come easier.
9. Nines give and give. You've loved people through things that would have broken others... and you're tired.

### After the topic (replaces A's per-topic lines)
- **love:** "Love. That's where the smoke was pulling, {name}." / "Something is sitting over your heart that was never yours to carry."
- **money:** "Money. That's where the smoke was pulling, {name}." / "Something is sitting over your money that was never yours to carry."
- **purpose:** "Your path. That's where the smoke was pulling, {name}." / "Something is sitting over your path that was never yours to carry."
- **someone:** "Someone's on your mind. That's where the smoke was pulling, {name}." / "Their energy is tangled with yours... and so is something else." / "Tell me... what's their first name?"

### After the email
"Thank you, {name}." (no "The link is complete."), then A's "Now, tell me more about what's on your mind..." / "Your thoughts, your feelings... I'm listening."

## Copy (AI): `server/lib/fbChatFlowPrompts.ts`
- **A's base persona and every rule are kept word for word,** including the safety override.
- **The quoted "Cold Reading Techniques" block is the one thing swapped.** In its place is a reading method: tell before asking, built from her words, every turn new, no line that fits anyone, answer her question first, never "can't tell", no courtroom or exam words, her name at most once.
- **Each step is handed Evelyn's earlier lines** and told not to repeat them.

Proven offline on 10 real /fb love chats (her real answers, run through B). Across those runs:
- stock lines fell from 2–7 per chat to 0–1
- B asked 3 questions before the offer, where A asked 7
- after the fixes: no courtroom words, no dodges, no soft diagnosis, and every diagnosis picture came from her own life

The side-by-side stays in the operator's workspace, because it holds customer words.
