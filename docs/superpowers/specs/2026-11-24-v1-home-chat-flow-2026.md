# Home chat flow: interview (A) vs reading (B) · `v1_home_chat_flow_2026`

Decided with the operator 2026-11-24. Base V1 (home) funnel only. This is the /fb reading
(`v1_fb_chat_flow_2026`, commit `b226952d`) run as its OWN test on the home storefront.

## Why
The /fb test moved the reading flow onto FB traffic. Joel wants the same reading engine
(arm B) tested against the base V1 home funnel (`/` → `/chat`), as a separate experiment so
the two read independently. Same hypothesis as /fb: today's chat reads as an interview
(~7 questions after the email before the offer); the reading flow tells her something from
her own words before each ask and gets to the offer in three steps + a diagnosis.

## Decisions (operator, 2026-11-24)
- **Arm B reuses the /fb reading VERBATIM.** The candle + name-number opener
  (`fbBNameLines`), the smoke topic lines (`fbBTopicLines`), the three reading steps
  (`fbB1/fbB2/fbB3`) and the diagnosis (`fbBDiagnosis`) are all funnel-agnostic — they
  fork purely on `userData.chatFlow === 'b'`. No new copy. The opening greeting ("Greetings,
  dear friend… My name is Evelyn Cross… What's your first name, dear?") is identical in both
  arms and untouched; only the step right after her name forks.
- **New experiment** `v1_home_chat_flow_2026`, A (control, `{}`) 50 / B (`{"flow":"b"}`) 50.
- **Visitor-keyed** (`ab_vid`), assigned at chat start, like the /fb one.
- **Conversion** `v1_main_funnel`, windowDays 7, targetN 9600.

## Why the home page reports PostHog `"v1"`, not `"root"`
`getPostHogFunnel()` returns `"v1"` for `/`, `/chat`, `/welcome1`, `/welcome2`, `/success`
(see `client/src/lib/funnel.ts`). `currentFunnel()` returns `undefined` for the base funnel,
but that is also true of backend-offer chats, so gating on `getPostHogFunnel() === 'v1'` is
the precise base-V1 signal and is what the client uses.
