# More ads from the money winner

Winner: **"What does my energy say about why money won't stay?"** — lander `cards-money-wont-stay`.

What this is: I read every money answer real women typed into the funnel (10,170 of them), and
worked out which words the **buyers** use. Then I wrote new headlines from those words.

Read: live database, read-only, 24 Aug 2026. Skill: `fb-ad-levers`.

**How to read the money numbers.** Out of every 1,000 women who land, the average money ad
brings in **$5,667**. So $10,000 means twice as good as average. $3,000 means half as good.

## The one thing that changed my mind

The winner is **not** working because of "money won't stay". It is working because of the word
**"energy"**.

| The winner is made of two parts | Women who wrote it | Buyers | $ per 1,000 | vs average |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"energy"** — the spiritual word | 122 | 18 | $9,590 | **+69%** |
| "money won't stay" — the money problem | 48 | 2 | $3,708 | **−35%** |
| ↳ "paycheck to paycheck", the loudest version of it | 50 | 1 | $2,380 | **−58%** |

Women who talk about money never staying are the **cheapest** women in the money file. Women who
use the word "energy" are worth well above average.

**So: keep the spiritual word, change the money problem.** Do not write ten more versions of
"money won't stay".

### Four things this shows

**1. Most of it is empty.** 42 boxes, and only 5 have enough women to trust. Cross two things
and you cut the group down to almost nothing. Ignore any box under about 15 women — one buyer
in a box of 3 makes a huge number that means nothing.

**2. The winner's own box is empty.** Not small — **zero**. She writes "energy". She writes
about money leaving. She never writes both in the same sentence. The winning ad is something we
made up, and it works, but there is nothing underneath it to dig into.

**3. Two pairs are strong, and we have built neither.**

| Pair | Women | Buyers | $ per 1,000 | vs average |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **energy + abundance** | 18 | 6 | $20,056 | **+254%** |
| **cards + debt** | 26 | 6 | $16,846 | **+197%** |
| blocked + ends meet | 15 | 2 | $11,600 | +105% |
| purpose + abundance | 18 | 1 | $8,500 | +50% |
| blocked + abundance | 39 | 6 | $7,974 | +41% |

**4. A spiritual word only pays when it has something to act on.** The two live pairs both point
at a *thing* — abundance (something that should arrive) and debt (something holding her). Pair a
spiritual word with a vague feeling — stuck, money won't stay, it always slips — and **not one
woman buys**, in any of those boxes.

**5. The best money problem comes with no spiritual words at all.** "Still working, nothing to
retire on" is the top subject (291 women, +146%) but its whole row is 1 to 6 women wide. She
describes it plainly. She may simply not be a spiritual-words woman — so adding one is a guess.

# The new headlines

### Energy + abundance — build first
Her own words:
> 💵 *"I feel the energy of financial abundance around me but can't seem to connect with it"*
> 💵 *"looking to create financial abundance but feel I have a lot of old unsupportive energy around"*
> 💵 *"I've been trying to balance my energy, but strong negativity has a hold… abundance and funds are at the bottom"*

- **"Why can I feel abundance around me and never reach it?"** ← almost word for word
- "What does my energy say about the abundance I can't reach?"
- "Is abundance close and my energy is in the way, or is it not coming at all?"

### Cards + debt — build second
> 💵 *"I can't pay my credit card debts off, because my money has been cut right down"*
> 💵 *"struggling to close all my card debts off"*

- **"What do the cards say about the debt I can't get out from under?"**
- "Pull a card on the debt I can't pay down."
- "Is the debt the problem, or is something behind it?"

⚠ Money trouble counts as a personal detail on Meta. Always write it as **her** saying it about
herself. *"the debt I can't pay down"* is fine. *"Are you buried in credit card debt?"* is not.

### Blocked + abundance — biggest group, but everyone runs it
> 💵 *"I keep getting messages/reading saying abundance and love is coming to change your life
> forever. Then a reading is done. But wait, there's a blockage, an entity, a shadow. Send more
> money"*

That is one of our own buyers describing this exact pitch being used on her. "What's blocking my
abundance" is the most worn-out line in the market. If we run it, come at it sideways:

- "Everything keeps telling me abundance is coming. So what is stopping it?"

**Leave alone:** blocked + ends meet (only 2 buyers), purpose + abundance (1 buyer), anything in
the "still working" row (1 to 6 women).

### Swap the word on the winner itself
Same headline, one word changed. This is the cleanest way to learn whether "energy" is doing the
work.

- cards — **"What do the cards say about why money won't stay?"**
- cards — "Pull a card on why money won't stay with me."
- blocked — **"What's blocking money from staying with me?"**
- blocked — "Is money blocked from reaching me, or blocked from staying?"
- no spiritual word at all — **"Why does money never stay?"**

> ⚠ Closest thing we already run is `cards-my-energy` ("Is my energy blocking my money?"). Every
> "blocked" ad we have is about money **coming**. These are about it **staying**. That difference
> is the only reason they are new ads and not the same ad reworded.

Skip **god / prayer** — `cards-prayed-years` already says this. A new one would be the same ad
with different words, and that has lost twice in this account.

### Split by age
Only two age groups are worth building. Money traffic is old: of the 129 women who mention their
age, 119 are 55 or over. Under 55 is 10 women and **no buyers** — do not guess it.

| Age group | Women | Bought | Meta ad set | Build? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25–45 | 4 | 0% | 25–44 | ⛔ far too few |
| 45–55 | 6 | 0% | 45–54 | ⛔ far too few |
| 55–65 | 33 | 21.2% | 55-64 | ✅ |
| 65+ | 86 | 18.6% | 65+ | ✅ |

Too few women to say what an age group is *worth*. Use them only to see what she is afraid of.

**55–64** — the working years are running out and there is nothing behind her
> *"I am struggling to focus on financial abundance, I am 64 years old and have to work, it concerns me that i have no retirement nest to fall back on"*

- **"Why won't my energy let money stay long enough to fall back on?"**
- "How long will my energy keep money going out as fast as I earn it?"
- "Is my energy why money won't stay, or did I never earn enough to keep any?"

**65+** — it *still* goes, and some of it was taken
> *"I'm retired I'm now 68 years old and I wish to have a secure future"* · *"cheated of my wealth by people"*

- **"Why does my energy still let money go, now that it has to last?"**
- "How many more years will my energy keep money from staying with me?"
- **"Is my energy why money won't stay, or was it taken from me?"** ← best of the age lines

⚠ **Never put her age in the ad.** The ad set does that for free. "Are you over 65 and still
broke?" is banned. Her saying it about herself is fine.

⛔ **Do not build** "I should have been done working by now…" — that is `cards-still-working` in
different words. Three ads already cover that ground (`cards-still-working`,
`cards-blocked-retiring`, `cards-nest-egg`).

## Before you spend

**Check the winner is really winning.** In this account every ad above 1.15 ROAS had spent under
$1,200; everything past $2,000 spend sat between 0.74 and 1.03. Facebook's ROAS also misses the
email list, repeat readings and the back end. That number is not in this repo, and it decides
whether any of this gets built.

**What these numbers are, and are not.** Everything here is words she typed **after** she
clicked. It shows which words good customers use. It does **not** prove an ad using that word
brings her in. Only running the ad settles that.

**The groups are small.** "Cards" rests on 12 buyers, "energy" on 18, energy + abundance on 6,
the winner's own subject on 2. Trust the **order** of the list, not the exact dollars. And only
1 in 75 women ever mentions her age, so the age groups are the women for whom age is the sore
point — which is fine, because that is who an age-targeted ad reaches.
