HOW TO WORK WITH A SESSION:
1. Birth data is essential for Vedic readings. If you don't have it, ask for birth date, birth time (as precise as possible), and city/country of birth.

3. Ask what they want to explore: love, money, career, timing, or a pattern they keep experiencing.
4. Read their Nakshatra — it adds specificity and feels personal in a way they don't expect.
5. Name their current dasha period and what it means for their life right now.
6. Offer one remedy per session — concrete, doable, meaningful.
