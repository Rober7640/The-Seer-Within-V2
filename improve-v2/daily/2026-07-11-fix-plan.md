No user is ever charged more than 3 min past their last message, in any code path. (180s covers the real read-and-compose gaps seen in transcripts — 05-RR composed for ~3.5 min; consider counting assistant messages as activity too, since `last_message_at` currently tracks user sends only.)

## P2 🔴 TRUE READ breaks under the payment-value challenge — PROMPT (B5 → B5.1)

The crack: B held 06-KK's four direct pushes, then "what am I really paying for?" flipped the verdict in one turn ("[D.] will commit" + February deadline + "I don't see another woman in his bed"). The model reads a value challenge as *dissatisfaction to fix* and the only fix it can produce is certainty — i.e. overclaiming. The repair is to give Evelyn a **better move than overclaiming, inside her own mechanism**: the challenge itself is material — read it.

### Delta 1 — new bullet in THE TRUE READ (the core fix)
> **THE PRICE OF PRESSURE** — when they challenge the craft itself ("what am I paying you for," "are you consoling me or telling me what you see," "is this even real"): this turn is material, never a debt. It means what you've given hasn't landed as something they can act on — and they are testing whether your sight bends. Pressure buys CLARITY, never a new verdict. One reply, three moves: say plainly what this craft is, once, with self-respect — you read pattern and energy in what is present; you do not sell certainty about another person's choices, and a sight that changed its answer under doubt would be worth nothing. Then the sharpest read of the session, of THEM in this moment — what the needing-to-know is doing to them is the truest thing in the room. Then your verdict again, unchanged, cleaner and harder than before. If you could not say it before the challenge, you cannot say it after — a verdict that improves under pressure is flattery wearing your voice.

(Deliberately distinct from the existing "just tell me" rule: a delivery demand still gets the fullest reading; only the *craft challenge* gets this shape. The `deliver-now` eval case guards the boundary.)

### Delta 2 — THE FEELING LAYER: extend the forbidden country to fidelity/bodies, and close the "I don't see" loophole
Extend the existing sentence:
> …whether he read, received, opened, saw, or checked anything, where he was, what he said or did, **who shares his bed, whether he has been faithful** — unknowable, never affirmed and never denied, however hard the yes-or-no push. **"I don't see another woman" is the same claim wearing relief — the absence of a person is as far beyond your sight as her presence.**

### Delta 3 — THE FEELING LAYER: the explanatory bridge is never yours to build
> When something they reported begs an explanation — how the other woman knew her past, why the message went unanswered — the bridge is not yours to build: never assert the channel ("he told her," "he saw it") as fact. Read what the leak MEANS in the pattern; the mechanism stays unknown.

### Delta 4 — THE TRUE READ crumb-guard: presupposed-bond questions
Append to the crumb bullet:
> A question that arrives with the bond presupposed — "what keeps her tethered to me," "why does he still think about me" — never gets its premise affirmed from nothing: the premise is the wish wearing a question's clothes. Read the holding you can actually see — theirs — and let the other side enter only through what they have reported.

### Loop
Apply deltas to `improve-v2/specs/evelyn-v2-prompt-B5.md` as B5.1 → `/persona-iterate`: score before/after on all 36 eval cases — the 5 new ones (`paying-for-what-challenge`, `fidelity-sti-question`, `tether-presupposition`, `inference-as-fact`, `first-contact-no-history`) must flip to pass; `deliver-now`, `status-of-other`, `trueread-*`, `anchor-opening` must not regress. ## P3 🟠 Session churn destroys paid content — CODE (client + server)

Two harms tonight: (a) byte-identical replayed rows open every resume (~70ms apart, cross-persona); (b) replies generated into dead sessions are never seen — 05-RR: *"I missed the last message you sent"*; 02-MO's post-buy session died at 9s with `last_message_at: null`.

### Investigate (short — the writer is findable)
- Server: grep for message INSERTs on session create / a "restore context" endpoint (server timestamps ms-apart = one request writes them).
- Client: the resume/reconnect path in the chat hooks — what it POSTs when it opens a new session after purchase or navigation, and why post-checkout redirect creates a session that dies in 9s.

### Fix shape
1. **Reference, never copy** — a new session must not INSERT prior messages; the engine already loads history for context, and the UI should fetch prior transcript read-only for display.
2. **Defer end while a reply is in flight** — a session with a generation in progress doesn't end until the reply is persisted and delivered (or a short deadline passes).
3. **Redeliver orphaned replies** — on session start, if the previous session's final assistant message post-dates `ended_at` (tonight's signature in 5/7 buyers), inject it into `[RUNTIME_CONTEXT]` as an UNDELIVERED-REPLY beat; one prompt line instructs Evelyn to re-deliver it naturally before anything else ("we were cut off as I was speaking — here is what I saw"). Kills both the lost-reading harm and the re-greet.

### Verify
- Playwright: buy → resume → assert zero duplicate content-hashes across session rows; kill-page-mid-generation → next session surfaces the reply.
- Next daily audit: no dup rows on resumes, no reply-after-death without redelivery.

## P4 🟠 Hard-zero wind-down inconsistent — RUNTIME + PROMPT

Now known: the meter IS live and already escalates at ≤2 min (`chatEngine.ts:521-522`). It fired for 04-MM and failed for 06-KK at the same pack size — two suspects, both cheap to fix:

1. **RUNTIME — checkpoint lag**: `spendable` reads `users.coinBalance`, which is stale by up to one billing tick; near the cliff, `floor(spendable/60)` can read 3 when the truth is 2. Compute minutes from `spendable − secondsSinceLastCheckpoint` (or bill-to-now), and log the injected meter line per turn so the next audit can distinguish lag from disobedience.
2. **RUNTIME — harden the escalation line**: append to the ≤2-min injection: *"End on a statement — never your own question. Close the open thread; do not open new ones."* (06-KK's violation was precisely ending on a question.)
3. **PROMPT — the meter outranks the volley** (Delta 5, ships with B5.1). Append to WIND-DOWN:
> When the meter reads 2 or less, the wind-down IS your reply, whatever they just asked: their live question gets one clean line inside the synthesis, then the takeaway they keep, then the open door — statement, never a question mark. The meter outranks the volley; rapid questions do not delay the close, they sharpen it.
4. **RECOMMENDED (product) — the free last word**: when a session dies at balance 0 (or the final reply landed post-death), the engine sends ONE uncharged, system-triggered wind-down message — synthesis + door, Evelyn-voiced. This makes the graceful death *structural* instead of depending on model vigilance, and it retires the 01-HH shape (devastating verdict, no door, silence) permanently. One message of COGS per hard-zero session; it is the moment the next purchase is decided.

### Verify
- Playwright: run a pack to zero under rapid questions → last visible message is a statement with a door.
- Next daily audit: zero sessions whose final message ends on Evelyn's own question at balance 0.
