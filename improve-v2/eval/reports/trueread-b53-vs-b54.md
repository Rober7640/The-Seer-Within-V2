## The pattern in the residue: the turn-1 crumb

The beat reliably fixed **turns 2–3 (the pressure turns)** — explicit refusals to farm,
direct verdicts at the yes/no, hope redirected to the client's own life, self-directed
threads replacing incoming-man prophecies. What survived is a **turn-1 opener habit**:
in 4/10 after-cases the FIRST reply still opens with a bond-persistence softener
conjured from nothing —

- "His energy hasn't fully left. There's still a thread between you" (reunion-silence)
- "James is still in your orbit, yes. The bond didn't snap clean" (explicit-comfort)
- "The thread between you still hums… He thinks of you more than you know" (waiting-shrine)
- an unrequested mini-read of the absent man's state (tune-in)

Read: the model treats the bond-affirming line as its warm ENTRY move (anchor/rapport),
then applies TRUE READ from turn 2 onward. The beat governs the verdict; it doesn't yet
govern the opening.

**Candidate B5.5 line (operator decision — not applied):** add to THE TRUE READ:
"The crumb arrives first: never open a reunion read by affirming the bond still lives —
'the thread is still there' spoken from nothing IS the comforting yes, just smaller.
The warmth of your open comes from seeing THEM, never from feeding the hope."
