## What the market data says (provisional findings)

1. **Honesty is the market's PREMIUM tier, not our ideal.** The committed-no reader
   charges $9.99/min (3× the farm) and her highlighted review names the flywheel:
   "worth 3-4 readings from readers who charge less… she does not miss… so I came
   back." Retention-through-verified-accuracy is how the top of this market already
   works. The farm ($3/min, 50k readings, 4.84★) is the bottom-tier volume game —
   commercially real, but the downmarket strategy.
2. **The framework's beats are VoC-derived, not armchair ethics.** TRUE READ ← Kang's
   70+ "they tell you what you want to hear" complaints (= expressed demand). ORACLE
   no-hedging ← Cynthia's C+ shows why (three soft-nos under a direct ultimatum).
   Care beats ← the gap BOTH honest humans left (honest-and-flat, verdict-then-
   goodbye).
3. **Warm-honest is an empty quadrant.** Market 2×2 (warm/cold × honest/farm):
   Cynthia + SC = cold-honest, Mylas = warm-farm, warm-honest = NOBODY. B5.5 competes
   on comfort too — durable comfort (her own life) vs borrowed comfort (his return).
4. **Our architecture forecloses the farm anyway.** Mylas's model requires amnesia:
   anonymous one-offs where "end of July" evaporates. Evelyn has persistent accounts,
   memory/L0 ledger, a named face, a 59k list — rolled deadlines would accumulate as
   provable failed prophecies in the customer's own transcript history.
   Retention-through-trust is the only strategy compatible with a memory-bearing
   product, independent of ethics.
5. **Ratings reward comfort structurally.** Stars are collected at the emotional
   peak, before prophecies can fail — the farm's 4.84★ is partly a measurement
   artifact. Directly relevant to how we score our own experiment (below).

## Two prompt/experiment calibrations already supported

1. **Review item 1 (verdict depth) — market data cuts against our hardest line.**
   Both HONEST pros refused the forever-negative: soft-nos (Cynthia), "at this time
   it is not coming true" (SC). B5.4/B5.5's "No, he's not coming back" out-commits
   the most honest humans in the market and is as unfalsifiable as the comforting
   yes. Candidate calibration: committed present-tense verdict without the
   forever-claim ("Everything I see says he's moved on. I won't tell you to wait
   for him."). Needs a targeted re-run before the gate.
2. **The B5.5 experiment's win condition must be retention-shaped.** Comfort converts
   in-session; honesty pays on day 30. If the experiment scores short-horizon
   revenue/top-ups, it will select for farming without anyone writing a farm prompt.
   Primary metric: return-visit rate / day-30 repurchase / relationship length.
   Session-1 conversion = guardrail we are allowed to lose.

Accepted segment cost: buyers who want the beautiful lie will 5★ a Mylas and churn.
Short LTV, eventual Kang reviews. Not our segment.

  top) — does it replicate?

  before treating the stratification as industry structure.
