## ⚠ Why this hook needs a `PALM_HOOK_YES` entry — the sharpest compliance risk in the set

This is the only headline in the batch that addresses a woman **inside a live relationship**, and the obvious "satisfying" answer is exactly the one Evelyn must never give: *"no, he's wrong for you — leave."* That is a verdict on a third party and, functionally, relationship advice.

`heart-safe` already solved this shape and its guardrail is the precedent to copy: *"NEVER promise that he will commit and NEVER pronounce that he will not — affirm HER knowing and her worth, not a forecast of his choice."* Without an entry here, Version C falls through to `DEFAULT_HOOK_YES` (*"yes, she has already met them / yes, she will love again"*) — which, delivered to someone asking whether to stay, reads as *"your real one is still out there."* That is an unintended nudge to leave. **This entry is mandatory, not a nice-to-have.**
